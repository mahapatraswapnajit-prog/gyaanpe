Class 1 English Chapter 1: Two Little Hands

NCERT Mridang · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

We have two little hands that help us clap, wave, eat and write. We keep them clean by washing with soap.

Key concepts

👐My Hands

We have two hands. Hands help us clap, wave, eat and write.

Example: Clap your hands! 👏

In real life: We wash hands before eating.

💡 Did you know? Each hand has five fingers.

👏Action Words

clap, wave, hold, write, draw

Example: I clap my hands.

In real life: Rhymes with actions.

💡 Did you know? Action words tell what we do.

🧼Clean Hands

Wash hands with soap and water.

Example: Before eating and after the toilet.

In real life: Hand washing song.

💡 Did you know? Clean hands keep germs away.

🔢Counting Fingers

1, 2, 3, 4, 5 on one hand.

Example: Two hands = 10 fingers.

In real life: Finger games.

💡 Did you know? Fingers help us count.

🎵Rhymes

Rhymes are short fun poems.

Example: Two little hands go clap, clap, clap.

In real life: Class rhyme time.

💡 Did you know? Say rhymes with actions.

Key terms

TermMeaning
HandPart of the body we use to hold
ClapHit hands together
WaveMove hand to say hi or bye
FingerPart of the hand
RhymeShort fun poem

Quick revision

  • We have two hands.
  • One hand has five fingers.
  • Wash hands before eating.
