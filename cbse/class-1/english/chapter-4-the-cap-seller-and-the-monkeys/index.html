Class 1 English Chapter 4: The Cap-seller and the Monkeys

NCERT Mridang · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

A cap-seller's caps are taken by monkeys who copy everything. He cleverly throws his own cap, the monkeys copy him, and he gets his caps back.

Key concepts

🧢The Cap-seller

A cap-seller sold caps from village to village.

Example: He rested under a tree.

In real life: Hawkers sell things.

💡 Did you know? This is a popular folk story.

🐒Naughty Monkeys

Monkeys took his caps and climbed the tree.

Example: Monkeys copy what they see.

In real life: Monkeys in parks.

💡 Did you know? Copying is called imitating.

💡Clever Idea

The cap-seller threw his own cap down. The monkeys copied him and threw theirs too!

Example: He got his caps back.

In real life: Puzzle solving.

💡 Did you know? Thinking cleverly solves problems.

🔤Action Words

sell, sleep, climb, throw, copy

Example: The monkey climbed the tree.

In real life: Act out the story.

💡 Did you know? Action words show what happens.

🔢Many Caps

One cap, two caps, many caps.

Example: cap → caps (more than one).

In real life: Counting caps.

💡 Did you know? Add -s for more than one.

Key terms

TermMeaning
CapSmall hat
SellerPerson who sells
CopyDo the same as someone
CleverSmart
ClimbGo up

Quick revision

  • Monkeys copy what they see.
  • cap → caps.
  • The cap-seller was clever.
