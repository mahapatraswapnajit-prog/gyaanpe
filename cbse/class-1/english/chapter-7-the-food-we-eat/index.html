Class 1 English Chapter 7: The Food We Eat

NCERT Mridang · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

We eat many foods like rice, roti, dal, fruits and milk, from plants and animals. Healthy food helps us grow, we eat meals on time and we never waste food.

Key concepts

🍛Our Food

We eat rice, roti, dal, vegetables, fruits and milk.

Example: Breakfast, lunch and dinner.

In real life: Family meals.

💡 Did you know? Food gives us energy to play.

🌾Where Food Comes From

Some food comes from plants, some from animals.

Example: Rice from plants; milk from cows.

In real life: Thank farmers.

💡 Did you know? Farmers grow our food.

🥗Healthy Food

Eat fruits, vegetables and dal to grow strong.

Example: Too many sweets are not good for teeth.

In real life: Healthy tiffin.

💡 Did you know? Drink clean water.

🍽️Meal Times

breakfast (morning), lunch (afternoon), dinner (night)

Example: We eat breakfast before school.

In real life: Daily routine.

💡 Did you know? Eat on time.

🙏Don't Waste Food

Take only what you can eat.

Example: Finish your plate.

In real life: Good habits.

💡 Did you know? Many people don't have enough food.

Key terms

Quick revision

  • Breakfast is the morning meal.
  • Milk comes from cows.
  • Don't waste food.
