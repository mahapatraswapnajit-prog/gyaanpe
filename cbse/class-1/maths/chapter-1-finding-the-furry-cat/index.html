Class 1 Maths Chapter 1: Finding the Furry Cat!

NCERT Joyful Mathematics · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Position words like on, under, inside, outside, near, far, left and right tell us where things are.

Key concepts

🐱Where is the Cat?

Words like on, under, in, near and far tell where things are.

Example: The cat is under the table.

In real life: We use them every day.

💡 Did you know? These are called position words.

⬆️Up and Down

Up is high; down is low.

Example: The bird is up in the tree.

In real life: Fans are up on the ceiling.

💡 Did you know? Top and bottom also tell position.

⬅️Left and Right

We have a left hand and a right hand.

Example: The ball is to the right of the box.

In real life: We drive on the left in India.

💡 Did you know? Wave your right hand!

📦Inside and Outside

Inside means in something; outside means out of it.

Example: The toy is inside the box.

In real life: Keep shoes outside the room.

💡 Did you know? Birds fly outside the house.

🔎Near and Far

Near means close; far means not close.

Example: The school is near my home.

In real life: The Moon is very far.

💡 Did you know? Far things look small.

Key terms

TermMeaning
OnAbove and touching
UnderBelow
InsideIn something
NearClose
FarNot close

Quick revision

  • Under means below.
  • Near means close.
  • We have a left and a right hand.
