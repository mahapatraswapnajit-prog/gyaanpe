Class 1 Maths Chapter 2: What is Long? What is Round?

NCERT Joyful Mathematics · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Things can be long or short, tall or short, round or flat. Round things roll and flat things slide.

Key concepts

📏Long and Short

Some things are long, some are short.

Example: A snake is long; a worm is short.

In real life: A pencil is longer than an eraser.

💡 Did you know? We compare to find long and short.

⚽Round Things

Round things can roll.

Example: A ball and an orange are round.

In real life: Wheels are round.

💡 Did you know? Round things have no corners.

📦Flat Things

Flat things do not roll; they slide.

Example: A book and a box are flat.

In real life: Bricks are flat.

💡 Did you know? Flat sides help things stand.

🔼Tall and Short

Tall means high from the ground.

Example: A giraffe is tall; a cat is short.

In real life: Children grow taller.

💡 Did you know? A tree is taller than a man.

🧩Shapes Around Us

Look around for shapes.

Example: A clock is round; a door is long.

In real life: Count round things at home.

💡 Did you know? Shapes are everywhere.

Key terms

LongBig from end to end
