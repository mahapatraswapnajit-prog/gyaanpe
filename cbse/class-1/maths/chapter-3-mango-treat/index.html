Class 1 Maths Chapter 3: Mango Treat

NCERT Joyful Mathematics · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

We count from 1 to 9, read number names, compare which is more or less, find numbers before and after, and learn that zero means nothing.

Key concepts

🥭Counting 1 to 9

We count one by one: 1, 2, 3 ... 9.

Example: 3 mangoes: 🥭🥭🥭.

In real life: Count fingers on one hand: 5.

💡 Did you know? Each thing is counted once.

🔢Writing Numbers

Numbers have names: one, two, three...

Example: 5 is five; 9 is nine.

In real life: House numbers.

💡 Did you know? Numbers tell 'how many'.

⚖️More and Less

More means a bigger number; less means a smaller number.

Example: 5 mangoes are more than 3.

In real life: Who has more sweets?

💡 Did you know? Matching helps compare.

➡️Before and After

After 4 comes 5; before 4 comes 3.

Example: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5.

In real life: Steps on a ladder.

💡 Did you know? Each number is 1 more than the one before.

0️⃣Zero

Zero means nothing.

Example: No mangoes in the basket = 0.

In real life: An empty plate has 0 rotis.

💡 Did you know? 0 comes before 1.
