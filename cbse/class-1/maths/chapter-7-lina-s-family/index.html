Class 1 Maths Chapter 7: Lina's Family

NCERT Joyful Mathematics · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

In Lina's family we compare heights, measure with hand spans and steps, find heavy and light things, and see which container holds more.

Key concepts

📏Tall and Short

Family members have different heights.

Example: Father is taller than Lina.

In real life: Height charts at home.

💡 Did you know? We compare by standing side by side.

✋Measuring with Hands

We can measure with hand spans, feet or steps.

Example: The table is 6 hand spans long.

In real life: Measuring a bed with steps.

💡 Did you know? Different hands give different counts.

⚖️Heavy and Light

Some things are heavy, some light.

Example: A watermelon is heavy; a feather is light.

In real life: Carrying bags.

💡 Did you know? A see-saw shows who is heavier.

🥛More and Less

Containers can hold more or less.

Example: A bucket holds more water than a glass.

In real life: Filling bottles.

💡 Did you know? Fill and compare.

👨‍👩‍👧Comparing in Family

Who is oldest? Who is youngest?

Example: Grandmother is oldest; baby is youngest.

In real life: Family photos.

💡 Did you know? Ages can be compared too.

Key terms

TermMeaning
TallHigh
ShortNot tall
HeavyHard to lift
LightEasy to lift
Hand spanMeasure with a stretched hand

Quick revision

  • Father is taller than Lina.
  • A feather is light.
  • A bucket holds more than a glass.
