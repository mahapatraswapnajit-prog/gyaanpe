Class 1 Maths: NCERT Joyful Mathematics chapter notes & questions

All 13 chapters of the new NCERT Joyful Mathematics book, explained simply with practice questions.

  1. Chapter 1: Finding the Furry Cat!Position words like on, under, inside, outside, near, far, left and right tell us where things are.
  2. Chapter 2: What is Long? What is Round?Things can be long or short, tall or short, round or flat. Round things roll and flat things slide.
  3. Chapter 3: Mango TreatWe count from 1 to 9, read number names, compare which is more or less, find numbers before and after, and learn that…
  4. Chapter 4: Making 10Ten is one more than nine. Pairs of numbers like 5 + 5, 6 + 4 and 9 + 1 make 10. Fingers and bundles of ten help us…
  5. Chapter 5: How Many?We count from 11 to 20, see tens and ones, compare numbers, put them in order and count on to add.
  6. Chapter 6: Vegetable FarmAt the vegetable farm we add by putting things together and subtract by taking away, up to 20. Order does not change…
  7. Chapter 7: Lina's FamilyIn Lina's family we compare heights, measure with hand spans and steps, find heavy and light things, and see which…
  8. Chapter 8: Fun with NumbersNumbers from 21 to 99 have tens and ones. We count by tens, compare numbers by tens first, and find numbers before,…
  9. Chapter 9: UtsavAt festivals we see patterns in lamps, flowers, colours, shapes, numbers and sounds. Finding what repeats helps us…
  10. Chapter 10: How do I Spend my Day?A day has morning, afternoon, evening and night. Some things happen before or after others, a week has 7 days, clocks…
  11. Chapter 11: How Many Times?Equal groups can be counted by skip counting in 2s, 5s or 10s, or by adding the same number again. This is the start…
  12. Chapter 12: How Much Can We Spend?We use coins and notes to buy things. We add money, pay prices, make the same amount in different ways, and save money…
  13. Chapter 13: So Many ToysWe sort toys into groups, count them, show counts in picture charts and find which group has the most or least.
