Class 10 English Chapter 1: First Flight: A Letter to God

NCERT First Flight & Footprints Without Feet · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Lencho loses his crop to a hailstorm and writes to God for 100 pesos; the kind postmaster sends 70, and Lencho, ironically, blames the post office employees for 'stealing' the rest.

Key concepts

🌧️The Story

Lencho, a farmer, hopes for rain for his ripe corn field. Rain comes, but it turns into a hailstorm that destroys the crop.

Example: Lencho calls the raindrops 'new coins'.

In real life: Farmers everywhere depend on weather.

💡 Did you know? The story is by G.L. Fuentes.

✉️Faith in God

Lencho writes a letter to God asking for 100 pesos. The postmaster, moved by his faith, collects money from employees and sends 70 pesos signed 'God'.

Example: The postmaster did not want to shake Lencho's faith.

In real life: Acts of kindness often stay anonymous.

💡 Did you know? Lencho's faith is total and unquestioning.

😅Irony

Lencho is angry that he got only 70 pesos and writes that the post office employees are 'a bunch of crooks' — the very people who helped him.

Example: Irony: the result is opposite of what is expected.

In real life: Good intentions are sometimes misunderstood.

💡 Did you know? The ending makes readers smile and think.

📝Themes

Faith, hope, kindness and irony.

Example: The postmaster's charity is a quiet good deed.

In real life: Board questions often ask about the irony.

💡 Did you know? Lencho's house was on the crest of a low hill.

🔤Vocabulary

Hailstorm, locusts, crest, faith, amiable, contentment.

Example: 'The field looked as if covered with salt' after the hail.

In real life: Similes make descriptions vivid.

💡 Did you know? 'Amiable' means friendly.

  2. 70 pesos

Q3. Lencho actually received

  1. 100 pesos
  2. 70 pesos

Q4. Lencho's crop was destroyed by

  1. A hailstorm
  2. Fire
  3. A flood
