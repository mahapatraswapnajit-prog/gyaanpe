Class 10 English Chapter 16: Poem: The Trees

NCERT First Flight & Footprints Without Feet · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

In 'The Trees', Adrienne Rich imagines trees inside a house breaking free and moving back to the empty forest, suggesting liberation — of nature and, symbolically, of women.

Key concepts

🌳The Poem

Adrienne Rich describes trees inside a house moving out into the forest at night. The empty forest gets its trees back.

Example: Roots work to disengage from the cracks in the veranda floor.

In real life: Trees indoors feel trapped.

💡 Did you know? Branches strain toward the glass.

🌙Imagery

Leaves stretch toward the glass, small twigs are stiff with exertion, and the moon breaks like a broken mirror.

Example: 'The whole moon … broken like a mirror.'

In real life: Vivid description creates mood.

💡 Did you know? Imagery appeals to sight and sound.

🕊️Symbolism

Many read the trees as women breaking free from domestic confinement; others see nature reclaiming its space.

Example: Adrienne Rich was a feminist poet.

In real life: Freedom is a universal desire.

💡 Did you know? Poems can have more than one valid interpretation.

🌲Nature

The forest is empty without trees: no bird sits, no insect hides, the sun buries its feet in shadow.

Example: Trees belong outdoors.

In real life: Urban greening brings trees back.

💡 Did you know? Deforestation empties forests.

💭Theme

Freedom, nature's rightful place, liberation.

Example: The poet sits indoors writing letters, not mentioning the departure.

In real life: Respect for nature.

💡 Did you know? Change can be silent yet huge.

Practice questions

Q1. 'The Trees' is written by

  1. Adrienne Rich
  2. Carl Sandburg
  3. Robert Frost
  4. Robin Klein
Show answerA) Adrienne Rich

Q2. In the poem, the trees move from the

  1. River to the sea
  2. Forest to the house
  3. Hill to the plain
  4. House to the forest
Show answerD) House to the forest

Q3. Without trees, the forest is

  1. Crowded
  2. Burning
  3. Flooded
  4. Empty
Show answerD) Empty

Q4. The moon is described as broken like a

  1. Plate
  2. Mirror
  3. Glass of water
  4. Stone
Show answerB) Mirror

Q5. The poem is often read as a symbol of

  1. Women's liberation
  2. Wealth
  3. Sports
  4. War
Show answerA) Women's liberation
