Class 10 English Chapter 19: Poem: For Anne Gregory

NCERT First Flight & Footprints Without Feet · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

In 'For Anne Gregory', Yeats tells Anne that young men will love her only for her yellow hair; she wants to be loved for herself, but he replies that only God can love a person for themselves alone.

Key concepts

💛The Poem

W.B. Yeats writes a conversation between a young man (the poet) and Anne Gregory about her beautiful yellow hair.

Example: The young man says lovers will love her only for her hair.

In real life: Outer beauty attracts attention.

💡 Did you know? Anne replies she could dye her hair.

🗣️Dialogue

Anne wants to be loved for herself, not for her looks. The poet replies that only God could love her for herself alone.

Example: An old religious man says the same.

In real life: Inner beauty matters.

💡 Did you know? The poem is in three stanzas of dialogue.

👀Outer vs Inner Beauty

People are often attracted to appearance first; true love values the inner self.

Example: 'Ramparts of your ear' — her hair falls like walls around her ears.

In real life: Social media often focuses on looks.

💡 Did you know? Yeats won the Nobel Prize in 1923.

🎨Devices

Metaphor ('great honey-coloured ramparts'), rhyme scheme abcbdefe, dialogue form.

Example: Honey-coloured describes her yellow hair.

In real life: Dialogue poems feel like conversations.

💡 Did you know? Yeats was an Irish poet.

💭Theme

Physical beauty vs inner self; only God loves unconditionally.

Example: Anne wants to be loved for who she is.

In real life: Value character over looks.

💡 Did you know? Self-worth beyond appearance.

Practice questions

Q1. 'For Anne Gregory' is written by

  1. Robert Frost
  2. Adrienne Rich
  3. W.B. Yeats
  4. John Berryman
Show answerC) W.B. Yeats

Q2. Anne Gregory's hair is

  1. Black
  2. Yellow
  3. Brown
  4. Red
Show answerB) Yellow

Q3. Anne says she could change her hair by

  1. Dyeing it
  2. Shaving it
  3. Hiding it
  4. Cutting it
Show answerA) Dyeing it

Q4. The poet says only ___ could love her for herself alone.

  1. Her father
  2. Her friends
  3. A king
  4. God
Show answerD) God

Q5. Anne wants to be loved for her

  1. Money
  2. Hair
  3. Inner self
  4. Dress
Show answerC) Inner self
