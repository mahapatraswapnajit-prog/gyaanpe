Class 10 English Chapter 21: Footprints: The Thief's Story

NCERT First Flight & Footprints Without Feet · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Ruskin Bond's story shows Hari Singh, a teenage thief, steal from the trusting Anil but return the money, realising that education is worth more than stolen cash; Anil silently forgives him.

Key concepts

🕵️Hari Singh

Hari Singh, a 15-year-old thief, befriends Anil, a young writer, hoping to rob him. Anil gives him work and teaches him to write his name and numbers.

Example: Anil earned money irregularly by writing.

In real life: Trust can transform people.

💡 Did you know? Story by Ruskin Bond.

💰The Theft

Hari steals 600 rupees Anil had kept under his mattress and goes to catch a train to Lucknow, but cannot board it.

Example: He hesitates because he realises he would lose the chance to become educated.

In real life: Choices define character.

💡 Did you know? Education is a greater treasure than money.

🔄Change of Heart

Hari returns and puts the money back. Anil knows but says nothing, and gives him a 50-rupee note, saying they will have money regularly.

Example: The notes were damp from the rain.

In real life: Second chances matter.

💡 Did you know? Anil's silence shows forgiveness.

📚Value of Education

Hari realises that learning to read and write could make him a 'big man'.

Example: Anil promised to teach him full sentences.

In real life: Adult education programmes help.

💡 Did you know? Literacy opens opportunities.

💭Theme

Trust, forgiveness, education and moral change.

Example: Hari was most impressed by Anil's trust.

In real life: Mentors change lives.

💡 Did you know? Kindness can reform people.
