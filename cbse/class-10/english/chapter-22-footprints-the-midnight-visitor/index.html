Class 10 English Chapter 22: Footprints: The Midnight Visitor

NCERT First Flight & Footprints Without Feet · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Secret agent Ausable outwits the armed rival Max by inventing a balcony and a police visit; Max jumps out of the window and falls, proving wit beats force.

Key concepts

🕵️Ausable

Ausable, a secret agent, is fat and does not look like a spy. Fowler, a young writer, accompanies him, expecting excitement.

Example: Ausable spoke with a slight accent.

In real life: Appearances can deceive.

💡 Did you know? Story by Robert Arthur.

🔫Max

In Ausable's room, a rival agent Max waits with a pistol to take an important report about missiles.

Example: The report was very important.

In real life: Danger builds suspense.

💡 Did you know? Max had used a passkey.

🧠Quick Thinking

Ausable invents a story about a balcony below the window and says the police will come. When there is a knock, Max jumps out to the 'balcony'.

Example: There was no balcony — Max fell.

In real life: Presence of mind wins.

💡 Did you know? The knock was a waiter with drinks.

📖Suspense

The story builds tension and ends with a twist.

Example: Fowler was disappointed at first by Ausable's looks.

In real life: Detective stories use clever tricks.

💡 Did you know? Twist endings surprise readers.

💭Theme

Wit over force; don't judge by appearance.

Example: Ausable won without violence.

In real life: Calmness in danger helps.

💡 Did you know? Intelligence is a weapon.
