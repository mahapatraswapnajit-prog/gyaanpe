Class 10 English Chapter 23: Footprints: A Question of Trust

NCERT First Flight & Footprints Without Feet · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Locksmith Horace Danby, who robs safes once a year to buy rare books, is tricked by a lady in red into opening a safe; she escapes with the jewels and he is jailed, ironically betrayed by another thief.

Key concepts

🔐Horace Danby

Horace Danby is a respectable locksmith who robs a safe once a year to buy rare books.

Example: He suffered from hay fever.

In real life: Even 'honest' people can do wrong.

💡 Did you know? Story by Victor Canning.

🏠Shotover Grange

He breaks into Shotover Grange to steal jewels, knowing the family is away.

Example: He fed chocolates to the dog Sherry.

In real life: Crimes have consequences.

💡 Did you know? Planning made him overconfident.

👗The Lady in Red

A young lady in red appears, claims to be the owner, and asks him to open the safe — saying she forgot the combination.

Example: Horace, sneezing due to flowers, opens it for her.

In real life: Con artists use charm.

💡 Did you know? She was actually a thief herself.

🚔Arrest

The jewels disappear, Horace's fingerprints are on the safe, and he is arrested. No one believes his story about the lady.

Example: He was jailed.

In real life: Trust must be earned.

💡 Did you know? Irony: a thief was cheated by another thief.

💭Theme

Honour among thieves? Irony, trust, consequences of crime.

Example: Horace thought all lady thieves were honest… no!

In real life: Think before trusting strangers.

💡 Did you know? Crime does not pay.

Key terms

TermMeaning
LocksmithPerson who makes and opens locks
Hay feverAllergy to pollen
SafeStrong locked box
IronyOpposite of expected
FingerprintsMarks left by fingers

Quick revision

  • Horace stole to buy rare books.
  • The lady in red was a thief.
  • Horace was arrested.
