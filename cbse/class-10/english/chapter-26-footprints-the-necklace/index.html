Class 10 English Chapter 26: Footprints: The Necklace

NCERT First Flight & Footprints Without Feet · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Matilda borrows a necklace for a ball, loses it, and with her husband spends ten years repaying the 36,000 francs for a replacement — only to learn the original was fake, worth 500 francs.

Key concepts

💎Matilda

Matilda Loisel, pretty but unhappy with her modest life, is married to a clerk. She dreams of luxury.

Example: Her husband got an invitation to a minister's ball.

In real life: Discontent can cause harm.

💡 Did you know? Story by Guy de Maupassant.

👗The Borrowed Necklace

For the ball, she borrows a diamond necklace from her friend Mme Forestier. She enjoys the party but loses the necklace.

Example: Her husband had spent 400 francs on a dress.

In real life: Honesty avoids bigger trouble.

💡 Did you know? Pride stopped her from telling the truth.

💰Replacement

They buy a similar necklace for 36,000 francs, borrowing money, and work hard for ten years to repay the debt.

Example: Matilda did heavy housework and became old-looking.

In real life: Debt can trap families.

💡 Did you know? One mistake changed their lives.

😮The Twist

Years later Matilda tells Mme Forestier the truth. Mme Forestier reveals the original necklace was fake — worth at most 500 francs.

Example: The ending is ironic and tragic.

In real life: Appearances can be false.

💡 Did you know? Maupassant is famous for twist endings.

💭Theme

Vanity, pride, honesty, irony.

Example: Had she told the truth, ten years would have been saved.

In real life: Value truth over appearance.

💡 Did you know? Contentment brings peace.
