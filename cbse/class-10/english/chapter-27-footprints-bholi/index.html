Class 10 English Chapter 27: Footprints: Bholi

NCERT First Flight & Footprints Without Feet · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

K.A. Abbas's story shows Bholi, neglected for her looks and stammer, gaining confidence through school; she refuses to marry the greedy Bishamber who demands dowry, showing the power of education.

Key concepts

👧Sulekha (Bholi)

Sulekha, called Bholi (simpleton), fell from a cot as a baby and had smallpox at two, leaving pock-marks. She stammered and was neglected.

Example: Her father Ramlal was a numberdar.

In real life: Children with differences need care.

💡 Did you know? Story by K.A. Abbas.

🏫School

She was sent to the new primary school because no one would marry her anyway. A kind teacher encouraged her, and she gained confidence.

Example: The teacher told her she would learn to speak without stammering.

In real life: Inclusive schools help every child.

💡 Did you know? Education changes lives.

💍The Marriage

Bishamber, an older lame man, agreed to marry her but demanded 5,000 rupees dowry at the wedding because of her pock-marks.

Example: Ramlal begged and gave the money.

In real life: Speaking up against dowry.

💡 Did you know? Dowry is illegal in India.

🗣️Bholi's Courage

Bholi threw the garland away and refused to marry such a greedy, mean man. She promised to look after her parents and teach in the school.

Example: She spoke without stammering.

In real life: Women's empowerment through education.

💡 Did you know? Education gave her a voice.

💭Theme

Education empowers; dowry is evil; every child deserves respect.

Example: Her teacher was the turning point.

In real life: Beti Padhao.

💡 Did you know? Confidence changes destiny.
