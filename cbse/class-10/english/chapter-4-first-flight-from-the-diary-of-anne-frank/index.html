Class 10 English Chapter 4: First Flight: From the Diary of Anne Frank

NCERT First Flight & Footprints Without Feet · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Anne Frank writes in her diary 'Kitty' about loneliness, school and her maths teacher Mr Keesing, whose punishment essays she turned into a witty poem that won him over.

Key concepts

📔The Diary

Anne Frank, a Jewish girl, received a diary on her 13th birthday. She named it 'Kitty' and treated it as a friend.

Example: She felt she had no true friend to confide in.

In real life: Writing a diary helps express feelings.

💡 Did you know? The diary was later published worldwide.

🏫School Life

Anne writes about school, friends and exams with honesty and humour.

Example: She worried about being promoted to the next class.

In real life: Students relate to her fears.

💡 Did you know? Margot was her elder sister.

🦆Mr Keesing

Her maths teacher, Mr Keesing, punished her talkativeness with essays: 'A Chatterbox', 'An Incorrigible Chatterbox' and then 'Quack, Quack, Quack, said Mistress Chatterback'.

Example: She wrote the last one as a poem, which made him laugh and stop punishing her.

In real life: Humour can win people over.

💡 Did you know? Creativity turned punishment into success.

✍️Diary Writing

Diaries are personal, dated and written in the first person.

Example: 'Dear Kitty, …'

In real life: Diary-entry writing is a board writing task.

💡 Did you know? Anne believed paper has more patience than people.

🕯️Historical Background

Anne's family hid from the Nazis during the Second World War. She died in a concentration camp; her father published the diary.

Example: Her diary is one of the most-read books in the world.

In real life: Remembering history prevents repeating it.

💡 Did you know? It shows the cost of hatred.
