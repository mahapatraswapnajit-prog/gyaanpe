Class 10 English Chapter 5: First Flight: Glimpses of India

NCERT First Flight & Footprints Without Feet · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Three travel pieces show India's diversity: the Goan baker tradition, the land and people of Coorg, and tea plantations and legends in Assam.

Key concepts

🍞A Baker from Goa

Lucio Rodrigues recalls the Goan baker (pader), a tradition from Portuguese times. His 'jhang-jhang' bamboo announced his arrival. Bread and sweet bread (bol) were needed for marriages, and bebinca cake at Christmas.

Example: Bakers wore a 'kabai' (frock) earlier, later half pants.

In real life: Goan bakeries still exist.

💡 Did you know? Monthly accounts were written on a wall in pencil.

☕Coorg

Lokesh Abrol describes Coorg (Kodagu) in Karnataka — coffee estates, rainforests, the Kaveri river, and brave Kodavu people with a tradition of hospitality. Legends link them to Greek or Arab ancestry.

Example: The Coorg Regiment is among the most decorated; General Cariappa was a Kodava.

In real life: Bylakuppe has a Buddhist monastery.

💡 Did you know? Mahseer fish is found in the Kaveri.

🍃Tea from Assam

Pranjol and Rajvir travel to Pranjol's home at Dhekiabari Tea Estate in Assam. Rajvir tells legends of tea: Chinese emperor Shen Nung, and Bodhidharma whose eyelids became tea plants.

Example: Assam has the largest concentration of tea plantations in the world.

In real life: Tea is India's favourite drink.

💡 Did you know? The second flush (May–July) gives the best tea.

🗺️Diversity

The three pieces show India's diversity of food, landscape, culture and work.

Example: Goa (west coast), Coorg (south), Assam (north-east).

In real life: Tourism celebrates regional culture.

💡 Did you know? Travel writing builds understanding.

📝Vocabulary

Pader (baker), bol (sweet bread), kabai (frock), Kodavus (people of Coorg), flush (new tea leaves).

Example: 'The jingling thud of the bamboo' announced the baker.

In real life: Local words carry culture.

💡 Did you know? Many Portuguese words entered Konkani.
