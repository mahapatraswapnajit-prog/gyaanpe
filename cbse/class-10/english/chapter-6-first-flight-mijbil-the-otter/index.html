Class 10 English Chapter 6: First Flight: Mijbil the Otter

NCERT First Flight & Footprints Without Feet · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Gavin Maxwell describes acquiring his pet otter Mijbil in Iraq, its playful habits, the chaotic flight to London, and amusing public reactions to an animal few had seen.

Key concepts

🦦Getting an Otter

Gavin Maxwell went to Basra (Iraq) and wanted an otter as a pet. He got a young otter he named Mijbil (Mij).

Example: Mij belonged to a species unknown to science; it was later named after Maxwell (Maxwell's otter).

In real life: Wildlife writing helps conservation.

💡 Did you know? Maxwell lived at Camusfeàrna in Scotland.

🛁Mij's Habits

Mij loved water, played in the bath, juggled marbles and rolled a rubber ball.

Example: He could open the bath tap by himself.

In real life: Zoo otters love toys.

💡 Did you know? Otters are playful and intelligent.

✈️Journey to London

Airlines did not allow pets in the cabin, so Mij was packed in a box. He escaped from the box in the plane and created chaos.

Example: An air hostess helped Maxwell keep Mij on his lap.

In real life: Transporting animals needs care.

💡 Did you know? Mij tore the box lining and bled.

🗣️In London

People guessed Mij was a baby seal, a squirrel, a walrus, even a hippo!

Example: Maxwell enjoyed the funny guesses.

In real life: Rare animals attract curiosity.

💡 Did you know? Most people had never seen an otter.

📝Themes

Bond between human and animal, curiosity and responsibility of pet ownership.

Example: Maxwell cared deeply for Mij.

In real life: Animal welfare matters.

💡 Did you know? Pets need time and understanding.
