Class 10 English Chapter 8: First Flight: The Sermon at Benares

NCERT First Flight & Footprints Without Feet · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

After enlightenment, the Buddha teaches the grieving Kisa Gotami — by asking for mustard seed from a house untouched by death — that death is universal and the wise do not grieve endlessly.

Key concepts

🧘Gautama Buddha

Siddhartha Gautama was a prince who left his palace after seeing sickness, old age, death and a monk. He attained enlightenment under a peepal (Bodhi) tree at Bodh Gaya and gave his first sermon at Benares (Sarnath).

Example: He was about 25 when he left home in search of truth.

In real life: Bodh Gaya is a major pilgrimage site.

💡 Did you know? 'Buddha' means the Awakened One.

😢Kisa Gotami

Kisa Gotami's only son died. In grief, she went from house to house asking for medicine to bring him back.

Example: People thought she had lost her senses.

In real life: Support for the grieving is important.

💡 Did you know? Grief can blind us to reality.

🌼Mustard Seed

The Buddha asked her to bring a handful of mustard seed from a house where no one had lost a child, husband, parent or friend.

Example: She found no such house.

In real life: Every family has faced loss.

💡 Did you know? She realised death is common to all.

💡The Lesson

Mortals cannot avoid death; the wise do not grieve endlessly. Peace comes by giving up lamentation and selfishness.

Example: She compared human lives to flickering lamps.

In real life: Counselling helps people accept loss.

💡 Did you know? Acceptance brings peace.

📝Themes

Universality of death, acceptance, wisdom and compassion.

Example: The sermon used a simple task to teach a deep truth.

In real life: Stories teach better than lectures.

💡 Did you know? Teaching through experience is powerful.

Quick revision

  • Buddha gave his first sermon at Benares.
  • He asked for mustard seed from a deathless house.
  • Death is common to all.
