Class 10 Maths Chapter 10: Circles

NCERT Mathematics · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

The chapter proves that a tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact and that tangents from an external point are equal, and applies these to lengths and quadrilaterals around circles.

Key concepts

⭕Tangent

A tangent touches a circle at exactly one point (point of contact). A secant cuts the circle at two points.

Example: Through a point on a circle, exactly one tangent exists.

In real life: A wheel touches the road along a tangent line.

💡 Did you know? A circle can have infinitely many tangents.

📐Tangent ⟂ Radius

The tangent at any point is perpendicular to the radius through the point of contact.

Example: Radius 5 cm, point 13 cm from centre → tangent length = 12 cm.

In real life: Belt drives around pulleys use tangents.

💡 Did you know? This uses the Pythagoras theorem.

✌️Tangents from an External Point

From an external point exactly two tangents can be drawn, and their lengths are equal.

Example: PA = PB for tangents PA, PB from P.

In real life: Common 3-mark proofs.

💡 Did you know? No tangent can be drawn from a point inside the circle.

🔷Quadrilateral Around a Circle

If a quadrilateral circumscribes a circle, sums of opposite sides are equal: AB + CD = AD + BC.

Example: A parallelogram circumscribing a circle is a rhombus.

In real life: Board 'prove that' question.

💡 Did you know? Follows from equal tangent lengths.

📏Angle Results

The angle between two tangents from a point and the angle subtended by the contact points at the centre are supplementary.

Example: Tangents at 70° → angle at centre 110°.

In real life: Quick MCQ results.

💡 Did you know? Opposite angles of the quadrilateral formed add to 180°.

Quick revision

  • Tangent ⟂ radius at point of contact.
  • Tangents from an external point are equal.
  • No tangent from an inside point.

Practice questions

Q1. A tangent meets a circle at

  1. One point
  2. Two points
  3. No point
  4. Three points
Show answerA) One point

Q2. Tangent at a point is perpendicular to the

  1. Chord
  2. Another tangent
  3. Radius through the point
  4. Diameter
Show answerC) Radius through the point

Q3. Number of tangents from an external point is

  1. 0
  2. 2
  3. Infinite
  4. 1
Show answerB) 2

Q4. Number of tangents from a point inside a circle is

  1. 1
  2. 2
  3. Infinite
  4. 0
Show answerD) 0

  1. 12 cm
