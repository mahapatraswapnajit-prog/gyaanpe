Class 10 Maths Chapter 11: Areas Related to Circles

NCERT Mathematics · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

The chapter finds perimeters and areas of circles, sectors and segments, and applies them to clock hands and combinations of plane figures.

Key concepts

⭕Basics

Circumference = 2πr; area = πr². Use π = 22/7 or 3.14 as instructed.

Example: r = 7 cm: circumference 44 cm, area 154 cm².

In real life: Wheels: distance per turn = circumference.

💡 Did you know? A semicircle's perimeter = πr + 2r.

🍕Sector

Area of sector with angle θ = (θ/360) × πr². Arc length = (θ/360) × 2πr.

Example: θ = 90°, r = 14: area = 154 cm².

In real life: Pizza slices and clock-hand sweeps.

💡 Did you know? Major sector = circle − minor sector.

🌙Segment

Area of minor segment = area of sector − area of the triangle formed by the radii and chord.

Example: θ = 90°, r = 10: segment = 78.5 − 50 = 28.5 cm² (π = 3.14).

In real life: Designs in windows and tiles.

💡 Did you know? For θ = 60°, the triangle is equilateral.

🕐Clock Problems

The minute hand sweeps 6° per minute.

Example: In 5 minutes it sweeps 30°; with length 14 cm, area = (30/360) × 616 = 51.33 cm².

In real life: Common board application.

💡 Did you know? The hour hand sweeps 0.5° per minute.

🧩Combination Figures

Find shaded areas by adding or subtracting areas of squares, triangles, circles and sectors.

Example: Square of side 14 with four quarter circles of radius 7 at corners: shaded = 196 − 154 = 42 cm².

In real life: Floor designs and logos.

💡 Did you know? Draw and label parts first.

Key terms

TermMeaning
SectorRegion between two radii and an arc

Quick revision

  • Sector area = (θ/360) × πr².
  • Segment = sector − triangle.
  • Minute hand sweeps 6° per minute.

Practice questions

  1. 308 cm²
  2. 44 cm²
  3. 49 cm²
  4. 154 cm²
Show answerD) 154 cm²

Q2. Area of a 90° sector of radius 14 cm (π = 22/7) is

  1. 77 cm²
  2. 154 cm²
  3. 616 cm²
  4. 308 cm²
Show answerB) 154 cm²
