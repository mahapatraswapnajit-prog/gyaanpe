Class 10 Maths Chapter 2: Polynomials

NCERT Mathematics · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

The chapter studies zeroes of polynomials, their geometric meaning on graphs, the relationships between zeroes and coefficients for quadratic (and cubic) polynomials, and forming a quadratic from given sum and product.

Key concepts

📈Zeroes and Graphs

A zero of p(x) is a value where p(x) = 0. Graphically, it is where the curve y = p(x) meets the x-axis.

Example: p(x) = x² − 5x + 6 has zeroes 2 and 3.

In real life: Engineers find zeroes to locate balance points.

💡 Did you know? A quadratic polynomial has at most 2 zeroes; a cubic at most 3.

🔗Sum and Product of Zeroes

For ax² + bx + c with zeroes α, β: α + β = −b/a and αβ = c/a.

Example: x² − 5x + 6: sum = 5, product = 6.

In real life: Common one-mark board question.

💡 Did you know? These relations let you check zeroes quickly.

🛠️Forming a Quadratic

A quadratic with sum S and product P of zeroes is k(x² − Sx + P).

Example: Sum 4, product 1 → x² − 4x + 1.

In real life: Reverse-engineering a problem from its answer.

💡 Did you know? k can be any non-zero real number.

〰️Shape of Graphs

Graph of a quadratic is a parabola: opening upward if a > 0, downward if a < 0. It can cut the x-axis at 2, 1 or 0 points.

Example: y = −x² opens downward.

In real life: Satellite dishes have parabolic shapes.

💡 Did you know? A thrown ball follows a parabolic path.

🧊Cubic Relations

For ax³ + bx² + cx + d: α + β + γ = −b/a, αβ + βγ + γα = c/a, αβγ = −d/a.

Example: x³ − 6x² + 11x − 6 has zeroes 1, 2, 3: sum 6, product 6.

In real life: Used in advanced algebra and physics.

💡 Did you know? Signs alternate in these relations.

Practice questions

Q1. The zeroes of x² − 5x + 6 are

  1. −2 and −3
  2. 1 and 6
  3. −1 and 6
  4. 2 and 3
Show answerD) 2 and 3

Q2. For ax² + bx + c, the sum of zeroes is

  1. −c/a
  2. c/a
  3. b/a
  4. −b/a
Show answerD) −b/a

Q3. For ax² + bx + c, the product of zeroes is

  1. −b/a
  2. b/a
  3. c/a
  4. −c/a
Show answerC) c/a

Q4. A quadratic with sum of zeroes 4 and product 1 is

  1. x² + 4x + 1
  2. x² + x − 4
  3. x² − x + 4
  4. x² − 4x + 1
Show answerD) x² − 4x + 1

Q5. Maximum number of zeroes of a cubic polynomial is

  1. 1
  2. 4
  3. 3
  4. 2
Show answerC) 3
