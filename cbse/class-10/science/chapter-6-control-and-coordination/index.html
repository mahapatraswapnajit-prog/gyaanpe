Class 10 Science Chapter 6: Control and Coordination

NCERT Science · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

The chapter explains coordination by the nervous system (neurons, reflex arc, brain) and by hormones in plants (auxin, gibberellins, cytokinins, abscisic acid) and animals (thyroxine, insulin, adrenaline), including feedback control.

Key concepts

🧠Nervous System

Neurons carry electrical impulses: dendrite → cell body → axon → synapse (chemical signal) → next neuron.

Example: Touching a hot object triggers a quick response.

In real life: Helmets protect the brain.

💡 Did you know? Brain: forebrain (thinking), midbrain, hindbrain (cerebellum for balance, medulla for involuntary actions).

⚡Reflex Action

A quick, automatic response controlled by the spinal cord through a reflex arc: receptor → sensory neuron → spinal cord → motor neuron → effector.

Example: Pulling the hand away from a hot plate.

In real life: Doctors test knee-jerk reflexes.

💡 Did you know? Reflexes save time and prevent injury.

🌻Plant Hormones

Plants respond by growth movements (tropisms). Auxin causes bending towards light; gibberellins help stem growth; cytokinins promote cell division; abscisic acid inhibits growth and causes wilting of leaves.

Example: Shoots show positive phototropism; roots show positive geotropism.

In real life: Farmers use hormones to ripen fruits.

💡 Did you know? Touch-me-not (Mimosa) leaves fold quickly without growth.

🧪Human Hormones

Endocrine glands release hormones into blood: thyroxine (thyroid, needs iodine), insulin (pancreas, controls blood sugar), adrenaline (adrenal, emergency), growth hormone (pituitary), testosterone and oestrogen.

Example: Lack of iodine causes goitre.

In real life: Iodised salt prevents goitre.

💡 Did you know? Pituitary is called the master gland.

🔁Feedback Mechanism

Hormone levels are controlled by feedback: when blood sugar rises, insulin is released; when it falls, release reduces.

Example: Thermostat-like control of the body.

In real life: Regular exercise helps sugar control.

💡 Did you know? Diabetes involves problems with insulin.

Quick revision

  • Insulin controls blood sugar.
  • Iodine is needed for thyroxine.
  • Cerebellum maintains balance.

Practice questions

Q1. The gap between two neurons is a

  1. Nephron
  2. Axon
  3. Synapse
  4. Dendrite
Show answerC) Synapse

Q2. Reflex actions are controlled by the

  1. Spinal cord
  2. Liver
  3. Cerebrum
  4. Heart
Show answerA) Spinal cord

Q3. Balance of the body is maintained by the

  1. Cerebellum
  2. Cerebrum
  3. Pons only
  4. Medulla
Show answerA) Cerebellum

Q4. Hormone that controls blood sugar is

  1. Insulin
  2. Auxin
  3. Adrenaline
  4. Thyroxine
Show answerA) Insulin

Q5. Iodine is needed to make

  1. Insulin
  2. Growth hormone
  3. Thyroxine
  4. Adrenaline
