Class 10 Science Chapter 9: Light – Reflection and Refraction

NCERT Science · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

The chapter covers reflection by spherical mirrors and refraction through glass and lenses, image formation rules, mirror and lens formulas, magnification, refractive index and power of lenses.

Key concepts

🪞Spherical Mirrors

Concave mirrors converge light; convex mirrors diverge it. Focal length f = R/2. Mirror formula: 1/v + 1/u = 1/f; magnification m = −v/u.

Example: Concave: torches, shaving mirrors. Convex: rear-view mirrors (wider view, always diminished virtual image).

In real life: Dentists use concave mirrors.

💡 Did you know? New Cartesian sign convention: distances measured from the pole.

🔎Images by Concave Mirror

Object beyond C → real, inverted, diminished; at C → same size; between C and F → magnified; at F → at infinity; between F and P → virtual, erect, magnified.

Example: Object between F and P gives a virtual, erect, enlarged image.

In real life: Make-up mirrors are concave.

💡 Did you know? A plane mirror has magnification +1.

🌊Refraction

Light bends when going from one medium to another. Snell's law: sin i / sin r = constant (refractive index). n = c/v.

Example: A pencil in water looks bent.

In real life: Swimming pools look shallower.

💡 Did you know? Refractive index of water ≈ 1.33, glass ≈ 1.5, diamond ≈ 2.42.

🔍Lenses

Convex lens converges; concave lens diverges. Lens formula: 1/v − 1/u = 1/f; m = v/u. Power P = 1/f (in metres), unit dioptre (D).

Example: f = +0.5 m → P = +2 D.

In real life: Spectacles are prescribed in dioptres.

💡 Did you know? Convex lens power is positive; concave negative.

🖼️Images by Convex Lens

Object beyond 2F₁ → real, inverted, diminished; at 2F₁ → same size; between F₁ and 2F₁ → magnified; between F₁ and O → virtual, erect, magnified (magnifying glass).

Example: A concave lens always forms a virtual, erect, diminished image.

In real life: Projectors and cameras use convex lenses.

💡 Did you know? Combined power of lenses in contact = P₁ + P₂.

  • Mirror formula: 1/v + 1/u = 1/f.
  • Lens formula: 1/v − 1/u = 1/f.
  • P = 1/f (m).

Practice questions

Q1. Rear-view mirrors in vehicles are

  1. Convex
  2. Concave
  3. Plane
  4. Cylindrical
Show answerA) Convex

Q2. Focal length of a mirror of radius 20 cm is

  1. 40 cm
  2. 5 cm
  3. 10 cm
  4. 20 cm
Show answerC) 10 cm

Q3. Power of a lens of focal length +0.5 m is

  1. −2 D
  2. +2 D
  3. +0.5 D
  4. +5 D
Show answerB) +2 D

Q4. Unit of power of a lens is

  1. Joule
  2. Dioptre
  3. Metre
  4. Watt
Show answerB) Dioptre

Q5. A concave lens always forms an image that is

  1. Magnified
  2. Real and erect
  3. Virtual, erect and diminished
  4. Real and inverted
Show answerC) Virtual, erect and diminished
