Class 10 Social Science Chapter 20: Economics: Money and Credit

NCERT Social Science · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

The chapter explains money as a medium of exchange, modern forms of money, banks and the RBI, terms of credit, formal and informal credit sources and Self-Help Groups.

Key concepts

🔄Money as Medium

Barter requires a double coincidence of wants. Money solves this by acting as a medium of exchange.

Example: A shoe maker wanting wheat must find a wheat farmer who wants shoes.

In real life: UPI is a digital form of money transfer.

💡 Did you know? Modern currency is accepted because the government authorises it.

🏦Banks and the RBI

The Reserve Bank of India issues currency notes and supervises banks. Banks keep a small part of deposits as cash and lend the rest. Demand deposits can be withdrawn on demand (cheques).

Example: Interest on loans is higher than on deposits — that difference is banks' income.

In real life: Savings accounts earn interest.

💡 Did you know? Cheques transfer money from one account to another.

📝Terms of Credit

Interest rate, collateral (assets pledged as security), documentation and mode of repayment together are terms of credit.

Example: A farmer may pledge land as collateral.

In real life: Kisan Credit Cards give farm loans.

💡 Did you know? Credit can help or trap — debt-trap happens when one cannot repay.

🏛️Formal vs Informal Sources

Formal: banks and cooperatives (regulated by RBI). Informal: moneylenders, traders, relatives (not regulated, often high interest).

Example: Rich households use mostly formal credit; poor depend more on informal.

In real life: Expanding bank branches in villages.

💡 Did you know? Cheap credit is important for development.

👩‍👩‍👧Self-Help Groups

SHGs of 15–20 members, mostly women, pool savings and give small loans. Banks lend to SHGs without collateral. Grameen Bank of Bangladesh was founded by Muhammad Yunus (Nobel Peace Prize 2006).

Example: SHGs help women become self-reliant.

In real life: Many SHGs run small businesses.

💡 Did you know? Members repay because of group responsibility.
