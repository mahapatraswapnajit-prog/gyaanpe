Class 10 Social Science Chapter 21: Economics: Globalisation and the Indian Economy

NCERT Social Science · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

The chapter explains MNCs, how foreign trade and investment integrate markets, liberalisation and the WTO, the role of technology, and the benefits and problems of globalisation, calling for fair globalisation.

Key concepts

🌐MNCs

A multinational corporation (MNC) owns or controls production in more than one country, setting up where costs are low and markets are near.

Example: Ford Motors set up a plant near Chennai.

In real life: Many phone brands assemble in India.

💡 Did you know? Cargill Foods bought the Indian company Parakh Foods.

🔗Integration of Markets

Foreign trade and foreign investment connect markets across countries. Globalisation is the rapid integration of countries through trade, investment, technology and people.

Example: Chinese toys sold in Indian markets.

In real life: Call centres serve foreign customers.

💡 Did you know? Information technology made services globally tradeable.

🚪Liberalisation

Removing barriers on trade and investment is liberalisation. India began major economic reforms in 1991.

Example: Trade barriers like import taxes were reduced.

In real life: More choices for consumers.

💡 Did you know? SEZs (Special Economic Zones) attract foreign companies.

🏛️WTO

The World Trade Organization (1995) aims to liberalise international trade, but developed countries have often protected their own farmers.

Example: Developing countries argue for fair rules.

In real life: Trade disputes are settled at the WTO.

💡 Did you know? Free and fair trade is the goal.

⚖️Impact

Benefits: more choice, better quality, investment, new jobs for skilled workers, growth of Indian MNCs (Tata Motors, Infosys). Problems: small producers and workers face tough competition and insecure jobs. Fair globalisation is needed.

Example: Battery and toy makers faced Chinese competition.

In real life: Governments protect small producers.

💡 Did you know? Flexible labour laws led to temporary jobs.
