Class 2 English Chapter 10: The Crow

NCERT Mridang · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Crows are clever black birds. The chapter talks about bird sounds and parts, describing words, and the well-known idea of a clever crow solving a problem by thinking.

Key concepts

🐦‍⬛The Crow

Crows are clever black birds found everywhere.

Example: Crows say caw-caw.

In real life: Crows near our houses.

💡 Did you know? Crows are very smart birds.

💡Clever Crow

A well-known story tells how a thirsty crow dropped pebbles into a pot to raise the water and drink.

Example: Thinking helps solve problems.

In real life: Puzzle solving.

💡 Did you know? Never give up!

🔊Bird Sounds

crow – caw, cuckoo – coo, parrot – talk, sparrow – chirp

Example: Birds have their own sounds.

In real life: Bird walks.

💡 Did you know? Listen to birds in the morning.

🪶Bird Parts

beak, feathers, wings, claws, tail

Example: Birds fly with wings.

In real life: Drawing birds.

💡 Did you know? Feathers keep birds warm.

🔤Describing Birds

black, clever, small, noisy

Example: The black crow is clever.

In real life: Bird posters.

💡 Did you know? Describing words come before naming words.

Key terms

Practice questions

Q1. A crow is usually

  1. White
  2. Black
  3. Pink
  4. Blue
Show answerB) Black

Q2. A crow says

  1. Caw caw
  2. Quack
  3. Moo
  4. Bark
Show answerA) Caw caw

Q3. Birds fly with their

  1. Beaks
  2. Legs
  3. Wings
  4. Tails only
Show answerC) Wings

Q4. A bird's mouth is its

  1. Nose
  2. Beak
  3. Lip
  4. Trunk
Show answerB) Beak

Q5. A small stone is a

  1. Pebble
  2. Rock mountain
  3. Brick
  4. Ball
Show answerA) Pebble
