Class 2 English Chapter 12: Little Drops of Water

NCERT Mridang · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

This famous poem shows that little drops of water and grains of sand make the mighty ocean and land. Small things, like saving water and kind words, make a big difference.

Key concepts

💧Little Things Matter

This poem says little drops of water and little grains of sand make the mighty ocean and the land.

Example: Small things together make big things.

In real life: Saving one rupee daily.

💡 Did you know? Small good deeds make a big difference.

🌊Big and Small

drop–ocean, grain–land, minute–hour

Example: Many minutes make an hour.

In real life: Piggy bank savings.

💡 Did you know? Little by little, things grow.

🚰Saving Water

Every drop of water counts.

Example: Close the tap while brushing.

In real life: Water saving.

💡 Did you know? Fix leaking taps.

🎵Rhyme

sand–land, water–ocean (idea)

Example: Read the poem with rhythm.

In real life: Recitation.

💡 Did you know? This is an old, famous poem.

❤️Kind Words

Little kind words make people happy.

Example: Thank you, please, sorry.

In real life: Kindness wall.

💡 Did you know? Small kindness spreads.

Key terms

TermMeaning
DropTiny amount of liquid
GrainTiny bit of sand or rice
OceanVery big sea
MightyVery big and strong
SaveKeep and not waste

Quick revision

  • Little drops make the ocean.
  • Every drop counts.
  • sand rhymes with land.
