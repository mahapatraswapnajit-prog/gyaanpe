Class 2 English Chapter 13: We Are All Indians

NCERT Mridang · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

India has many states, languages and festivals, but we are all Indians. We respect our flag and live together in harmony.

Key concepts

🇮🇳One Country

India has many states, languages and festivals, but we are all Indians.

Example: We live in Odisha, Punjab, Kerala and more.

In real life: Republic Day.

💡 Did you know? Unity in diversity.

🗣️Many Languages

Odia, Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, English and many more are spoken in India.

Example: Namaskar, Vanakkam, Sat Sri Akal.

In real life: Pen pals.

💡 Did you know? Learning a greeting in another language is fun.

🎉Many Festivals

Diwali, Eid, Christmas, Pongal, Rath Yatra, Bihu.

Example: We celebrate together.

In real life: School celebrations.

💡 Did you know? Festivals bring joy.

🏳️Our Flag

Our flag has saffron, white and green with a blue wheel.

Example: The wheel is the Ashoka Chakra.

In real life: Flag hoisting.

💡 Did you know? We respect our flag.

🤝Harmony

Living together peacefully is harmony.

Example: Share, care and respect everyone.

In real life: Class of many states.

💡 Did you know? Friends from every state.

Key terms

  • We are all Indians.
  • Our flag has saffron, white and green.
