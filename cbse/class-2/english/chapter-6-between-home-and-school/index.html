Class 2 English Chapter 6: Between Home and School

NCERT Mridang · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

On the way between home and school we see many places. The chapter practises position words, simple maps and road safety.

Key concepts

🏫My Way to School

On the way to school we see shops, trees, people and vehicles.

Example: I walk past the temple to reach school.

In real life: School bus.

💡 Did you know? Some children walk, some take a bus.

📍Position Words

near, far, left, right, opposite, between

Example: The shop is between the bank and the park.

In real life: Giving directions.

💡 Did you know? Position words tell where things are.

🚦Road Safety

Walk on the footpath; cross at zebra crossings.

Example: Red means stop, green means go.

In real life: Traffic lights.

💡 Did you know? Hold an adult's hand while crossing.

🗺️Simple Map

Draw your way from home to school.

Example: Mark your house, school and shops.

In real life: Route drawing.

💡 Did you know? A map shows places from the top.

🔤Naming Places

school, park, shop, hospital, temple

Example: Places are naming words.

In real life: Neighbourhood walk.

💡 Did you know? Every place has a name.

Key terms

TermMeaning
BetweenIn the middle of two things
NearClose
FarNot close
FootpathPath for walkers
MapDrawing of places

Quick revision

  • Red means stop.
  • Walk on the footpath.
  • between = in the middle.

Practice questions

Q1. The shop is in the middle of the bank and the park. It is ___ them.

  1. under
  2. inside
  3. between
  4. above
Show answerC) between

Q2. Opposite of 'near' is

  1. close
  2. by

Show answerC) far

Q3. We should walk on the

  1. Middle of the road
  2. Railway track
  3. Footpath
  4. Wall
Show answerC) Footpath

Q4. Red light means

  1. Stop
  2. Go
  3. Dance
  4. Run
Show answerA) Stop

Q5. Green light means

  1. Go
  2. Wait forever
  3. Stop
  4. Sleep
