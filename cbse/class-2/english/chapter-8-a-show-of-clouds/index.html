Class 2 English Chapter 8: A Show of Clouds

NCERT Mridang · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Clouds float, change shapes and bring rain. We imagine animals in the clouds and use describing words like fluffy, white and grey.

Key concepts

☁️Clouds

Clouds float in the sky and change shapes.

Example: A cloud may look like an elephant or a rabbit!

In real life: Cloud watching.

💡 Did you know? Clouds are made of tiny drops of water.

🌧️Rain Clouds

Dark clouds often bring rain.

Example: White fluffy clouds come on sunny days.

In real life: Monsoon clouds.

💡 Did you know? Rain helps plants grow.

💭Imagination

Imagine shapes in the clouds.

Example: I see a ship in the sky!

In real life: Story making.

💡 Did you know? Imagination makes the world fun.

🎨Describing Words

fluffy, white, grey, dark, soft

Example: Fluffy white clouds.

In real life: Sky drawing.

💡 Did you know? Describing words tell what kind.

🌬️Wind

Wind moves the clouds across the sky.

Example: Clouds change shape as they move.

In real life: Flying kites.

💡 Did you know? Wind can be gentle or strong.

Key terms

TermMeaning
CloudTiny water drops in the sky
FluffySoft and light
ImaginePicture in the mind
WindMoving air
RainWater falling from clouds

Quick revision

  • Dark clouds bring rain.
  • Clouds change shapes.
  • Wind moves clouds.
