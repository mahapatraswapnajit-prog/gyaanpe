Class 2 Maths Chapter 2: Shapes Around Us

NCERT Joyful Mathematics · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Things around us have solid shapes like cubes, cuboids, cylinders, cones and spheres. Curved shapes roll, flat ones slide, and flat-faced shapes stack easily.

Key concepts

🧊Solid Shapes

Things around us have solid shapes: cube, cuboid, cylinder, cone and sphere.

Example: A dice is a cube; a ball is a sphere.

In real life: Toys and boxes.

💡 Did you know? These shapes are 3D.

🎲Faces

Flat surfaces of a solid are faces.

Example: A cube has 6 square faces.

In real life: Dice faces.

💡 Did you know? A sphere has no flat face.

🛞Roll or Slide

Curved shapes roll; flat-faced shapes slide.

Example: A ball rolls; a box slides.

In real life: Pushing a drum.

💡 Did you know? A cylinder can roll and slide.

🍦Cone

A cone has a round base and a pointed top.

Example: Ice cream cone, birthday cap.

In real life: Traffic cones.

💡 Did you know? A cone can roll in a circle.

🏗️Stacking

Shapes with flat faces stack easily.

Example: Bricks and boxes stack; balls don't.

In real life: Building blocks.

💡 Did you know? Builders choose shapes that stack.
