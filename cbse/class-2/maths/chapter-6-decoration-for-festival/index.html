Class 2 Maths Chapter 6: Decoration for Festival

NCERT Joyful Mathematics · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Decorating for a festival, we add to find totals and subtract to find what is left, carrying and borrowing when needed, and decide which to use in word problems.

Key concepts

➕Adding

We add to find the total of decorations.

Example: 24 diyas + 13 diyas = 37 diyas.

In real life: Counting decorations.

💡 Did you know? Add ones first, then tens.

➖Subtracting

We subtract to find what is left.

Example: 45 balloons, 12 burst: 33 left.

In real life: Sweets left after sharing.

💡 Did you know? Subtract ones first, then tens.

🔁Carrying

If ones add to 10 or more, carry 1 ten.

Example: 28 + 15 = 43.

In real life: Making change.

💡 Did you know? 10 ones = 1 ten.

🔄Borrowing

If ones are too few, borrow 1 ten.

Example: 42 − 17 = 25.

In real life: Fair sharing.

💡 Did you know? Check by adding back.

🎁Word Problems

Read and decide: add or subtract.

Example: 'In all' → add; 'left' → subtract.

In real life: Festival shopping.

💡 Did you know? Draw pictures to help.

Key terms

TermMeaning
AddPut together
SubtractTake away
CarryMove 1 ten to the tens place
BorrowTake 1 ten as 10 ones
TotalAll together

Quick revision

  • 24 + 13 = 37.
  • 42 − 17 = 25.
  • 'Left' means subtract.
