Class 2 Maths Chapter 8: Grouping and Sharing

NCERT Joyful Mathematics · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

We make equal groups and multiply to count quickly, share things equally, and sometimes have leftovers. Grouping and sharing are linked.

Key concepts

👥Making Groups

Put equal numbers in each group.

Example: 12 sweets in groups of 3 = 4 groups.

In real life: Packing laddoos.

💡 Did you know? Equal groups help count fast.

✖️Times

3 groups of 4 = 3 × 4 = 12.

Example: × means groups of.

In real life: Counting wheels.

💡 Did you know? It is quick adding.

🍬Sharing Equally

Sharing means giving equal parts.

Example: 10 sweets for 2 friends = 5 each.

In real life: Sharing tiffin.

💡 Did you know? Equal sharing is fair.

➗Leftovers

Sometimes some are left over.

Example: 7 sweets for 2 friends: 3 each, 1 left.

In real life: Extra sweets.

💡 Did you know? The leftover can't be shared equally.

🔁Link

Grouping and sharing are linked.

Example: 4 × 3 = 12 and 12 shared by 3 = 4.

In real life: Party planning.

💡 Did you know? They undo each other.

Quick revision

  • 3 × 4 = 12.
  • 10 shared by 2 = 5.
  • 7 shared by 2: 3 each, 1 left.
