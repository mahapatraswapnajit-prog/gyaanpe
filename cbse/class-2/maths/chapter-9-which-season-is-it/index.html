Class 2 Maths Chapter 9: Which Season is it?

NCERT Joyful Mathematics · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

India has summer, rainy and winter seasons. A year has 12 months, a week 7 days, a day 24 hours and an hour 60 minutes. Farming, food and festivals follow the seasons.

Key concepts

☀️Seasons

India has summer, rainy season and winter.

Example: Summer is hot; winter is cold; rainy season is wet.

In real life: Clothes change with seasons.

💡 Did you know? Seasons change through the year.

📅Months

A year has 12 months.

Example: January is the first month; December the last.

In real life: Calendars.

💡 Did you know? Rainy season comes around June to September.

🗓️Days and Weeks

7 days make a week.

Example: Today is Monday; tomorrow is Tuesday.

In real life: School timetable.

💡 Did you know? Yesterday was Sunday.

🕐Hours

A day has 24 hours. A clock shows hours.

Example: School starts at 8 o'clock.

In real life: Bus times.

💡 Did you know? 1 hour = 60 minutes.

🌱Seasons and Life

Farmers, festivals and food follow seasons.

Example: Rice is sown in the rainy season.

In real life: Seasonal fruits.

💡 Did you know? Mangoes come in summer.

Key terms
