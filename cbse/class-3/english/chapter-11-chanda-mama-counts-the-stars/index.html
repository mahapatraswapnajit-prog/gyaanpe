Class 3 English Chapter 11: Chanda Mama Counts the Stars

NCERT Santoor · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

In this imaginative story, Chanda Mama tries to count the countless stars. The chapter practises counting, moon shapes and question words like 'how many'.

Key concepts

🌕Theme: The Moon and Stars

In this imaginative story, Chanda Mama (the Moon) tries to count the stars.

Example: There are too many stars to count!

In real life: Moon-gazing with grandparents.

💡 Did you know? In Indian stories the Moon is lovingly called Chanda Mama.

🔢Counting

We count things one by one.

Example: 1, 2, 3 … 10 stars.

In real life: Counting games.

💡 Did you know? Counting in tens is faster.

🌓Moon Shapes

The Moon seems to change shape through the month.

Example: Full moon (Purnima), new moon (Amavasya).

In real life: Festival calendars.

💡 Did you know? The Moon has no light of its own.

❓Question Words

how many, how much, where, when

Example: How many stars are there?

In real life: Quiz time.

💡 Did you know? 'How many' is for things we can count.

💭Imagination

Stories can make the Moon talk.

Example: Chanda Mama talks to the stars.

In real life: Bedtime tales.

💡 Did you know? Imagination makes stories fun.

  • The Moon reflects sunlight.
