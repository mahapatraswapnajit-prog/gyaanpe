Class 3 English Chapter 12: Chandrayaan

NCERT Santoor · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Chandrayaan is India's Moon mission by ISRO. Chandrayaan-3 landed near the Moon's south pole on 23 August 2023, a first for any country. The chapter teaches space words and writing dates.

Key concepts

🚀Theme: India's Moon Mission

This chapter introduces Chandrayaan, India's mission to the Moon by ISRO.

Example: Chandrayaan-3 landed near the Moon's south pole on 23 August 2023.

In real life: 23 August is National Space Day in India.

💡 Did you know? India was the first country to land near the Moon's south pole.

🛰️Space Words

rocket, lander, rover, orbit, astronaut

Example: The rover Pragyan moved on the Moon.

In real life: Space museums.

💡 Did you know? The lander was named Vikram.

🇮🇳ISRO

ISRO is the Indian Space Research Organisation.

Example: It launches rockets from Sriharikota.

In real life: Satellite TV and weather.

💡 Did you know? ISRO also sent Mangalyaan to Mars.

📅Dates

Write dates with day, month and year.

Example: 23 August 2023

In real life: Calendars.

💡 Did you know? Months begin with capital letters.

🌟Dreams

Big missions start with big dreams and hard work.

Example: Scientists worked for years.

In real life: Science clubs.

💡 Did you know? Many women scientists worked on Chandrayaan.

Key terms

TermMeaning
ChandrayaanIndia's Moon mission

Quick revision

  • Chandrayaan-3 landed on 23 Aug 2023.
  • Lander Vikram, rover Pragyan.
  • National Space Day: 23 August.
