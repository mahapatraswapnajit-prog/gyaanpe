Class 3 English Chapter 4: Out in the Garden

NCERT Santoor · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

This poem enjoys a garden full of flowers, bees and butterflies. The chapter practises rhyming words, garden words, action words and our five senses.

Key concepts

🌻Theme: Playing in the Garden

This poem enjoys the beauty and fun of a garden.

Example: Flowers, butterflies, bees and trees.

In real life: School gardens.

💡 Did you know? Gardens are full of life.

🎵Rhyming Words

Rhyming words sound alike at the end.

Example: tree–bee, flower–shower, play–day

In real life: Nursery rhymes.

💡 Did you know? Poems often rhyme.

🦋Garden Words

bud, petal, leaf, butterfly, bee

Example: A bud opens into a flower.

In real life: Garden walks.

💡 Did you know? Bees make honey from nectar.

🏃Action Words

Action words are verbs.

Example: run, jump, smell, play

In real life: Games.

💡 Did you know? Every sentence has a verb.

👃Our Senses

We see, hear, smell, touch and taste.

Example: We smell flowers; we hear birds.

In real life: Nature walk.

💡 Did you know? We have five senses.

Practice questions

  2. bee

  4. tea pot
Show answerB) bee

Q2. Which rhymes with 'play'?

  1. pot
  2. pull

  4. plate
Show answerC) day

Q3. A flower before it opens is a

  1. Bud
  2. Root

Q4. Bees make

  1. Silk
  2. Honey
  3. Wool
  4. Milk

  2. jump
