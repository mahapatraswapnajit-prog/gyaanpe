Class 3 EVS Chapter 10: This World of Things

NCERT Our Wondrous World · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Things around us are made of materials like wood, metal, plastic, glass, clay and cloth. Materials can be hard or soft, can float or sink, and can be see-through or not. We choose materials based on their use.

Key concepts

🪑Things Around Us

Things are made of materials like wood, metal, plastic, glass, clay and cloth.

Example: A chair can be wooden or plastic.

In real life: Look around your classroom.

💡 Did you know? The same thing can be made of different materials.

🔨Hard and Soft

Some materials are hard, some soft.

Example: Stone is hard; cotton is soft.

In real life: Pillows are soft.

💡 Did you know? Hard materials are used for tools.

💧Float or Sink

Some things float on water, some sink.

Example: A leaf floats; a stone sinks.

In real life: Life jackets help people float.

💡 Did you know? Boats are made to float.

🪟See-through or Not

We can see through glass but not through wood.

Example: Windows have glass.

In real life: Spectacles use glass or plastic.

💡 Did you know? Some things let a little light pass, like butter paper.

🧲Why Materials Matter

We choose materials based on what we need.

Example: Cooking pots are metal because they get hot.

In real life: Umbrellas keep us dry.

💡 Did you know? Raincoats are waterproof plastic.
