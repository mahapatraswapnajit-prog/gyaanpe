Class 3 EVS Chapter 12: Taking Charge of Waste

NCERT Our Wondrous World · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Waste is what we throw away. Separating wet waste (which rots) from dry waste (which doesn't) helps make compost and recycle. Reducing, reusing and recycling, and never littering, keep our surroundings clean and safe for animals.

Key concepts

🗑️What is Waste?

Things we throw away are waste.

Example: Peels, paper, plastic, broken toys.

In real life: Cities collect waste daily.

💡 Did you know? Too much waste harms the Earth.

🟢Wet and Dry Waste

Wet waste rots, like vegetable peels; dry waste does not rot, like plastic and glass.

Example: Keep green bin for wet, blue bin for dry.

In real life: Many towns ask for separated waste.

💡 Did you know? Separating waste helps recycling.

🌱Compost

Wet waste can become compost, a natural plant food.

Example: Peels in a compost pit become manure.

In real life: Gardeners love compost.

💡 Did you know? Compost makes soil rich.

♻️Reduce, Reuse, Recycle

Use less, use again, and turn waste into new things.

Example: Use cloth bags; reuse jars; recycle paper.

In real life: Kabadiwalas buy recyclable waste.

💡 Did you know? Recycled paper saves trees.

🚯Don't Litter

Throw waste in dustbins, not on roads or in rivers.

Example: Carry waste home if no dustbin.

In real life: Swachh Bharat keeps India clean.

💡 Did you know? Plastic harms animals that eat it.

Key terms
