Class 3 EVS Chapter 2: Going to the Mela

NCERT Our Wondrous World · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

A mela is a fair where people shop, eat and enjoy rides. Artisans sell handmade crafts like clay toys and baskets. In crowds we stay close to elders and know whom to ask for help.

Key concepts

🎡What is a Mela?

A mela is a fair where people gather to shop, eat and have fun.

Example: Rides, toys, bangles and sweets are found at melas.

In real life: Rath Yatra brings a big mela to Puri.

💡 Did you know? Many melas happen during festivals.

🧺Things at the Mela

Craftspeople sell pots, toys, baskets and cloth.

Example: Clay toys, bamboo baskets, handloom cloth.

In real life: Buying handmade things supports artisans.

💡 Did you know? Many crafts are made by hand.

👨‍👩‍👧Staying Safe

Stay close to elders in crowds.

Example: Remember your parent's phone number.

In real life: Melas have help desks.

💡 Did you know? If lost, ask a police person or stall owner for help.

🚌Getting There

People go to melas by bus, bullock cart, bicycle or on foot.

Example: Villagers walk together to nearby melas.

In real life: Special buses run during big melas.

💡 Did you know? Travelling together is safe and fun.

🍭Mela Fun

Melas have rides, music, magic shows and food.

Example: Giant wheel, merry-go-round.

In real life: Kite festivals are melas too.

💡 Did you know? Melas bring communities together.
