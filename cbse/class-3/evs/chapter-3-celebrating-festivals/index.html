Class 3 EVS Chapter 3: Celebrating Festivals

NCERT Our Wondrous World · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

India celebrates many festivals like Diwali, Eid, Christmas and Guru Purab. Harvest festivals such as Pongal, Bihu, Onam and Nuakhai thank nature for crops. Special food, decorations and sharing with people of all faiths make festivals joyful.

Key concepts

🪔Many Festivals

India celebrates many festivals with joy.

Example: Diwali, Eid, Christmas, Guru Purab, Pongal.

In real life: Schools celebrate festivals too.

💡 Did you know? Festivals bring families and friends together.

🌾Harvest Festivals

Some festivals celebrate new crops.

Example: Pongal, Bihu, Onam, Baisakhi and Nuakhai.

In real life: Farmers thank nature for crops.

💡 Did you know? Nuakhai is celebrated in Odisha with new rice.

🍲Festival Food

Special foods are made at festivals.

Example: Pitha in Odisha, sewaiyan at Eid, cake at Christmas.

In real life: Neighbours exchange sweets.

💡 Did you know? Sharing food spreads happiness.

🎨Decorations

Homes are cleaned and decorated with lights, rangoli and flowers.

Example: Diyas at Diwali, stars at Christmas.

In real life: Rangoli competitions in schools.

💡 Did you know? Use eco-friendly colours and diyas.

🤝Celebrating Together

People of all faiths join each other's festivals.

Example: Friends visit each other on Eid and Diwali.

In real life: Community feasts are held.

💡 Did you know? Festivals teach sharing and kindness.

Key terms

Quick revision

  • Nuakhai: Odisha harvest festival.
  • Pongal: Tamil Nadu.
  • Festivals teach sharing.
