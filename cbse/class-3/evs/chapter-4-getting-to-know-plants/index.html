Class 3 EVS Chapter 4: Getting to Know Plants

NCERT Our Wondrous World · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Plants have roots, stems, leaves, flowers and fruits. They can be herbs, shrubs or trees, with leaves of many shapes. We eat different plant parts, and plants need water, sunlight, air and soil to grow.

Key concepts

🌱Parts of a Plant

A plant has roots, stem, leaves, flowers and fruits.

Example: The roots grow under the soil.

In real life: We eat many plant parts.

💡 Did you know? Leaves make food for the plant using sunlight.

🌳Big and Small Plants

Plants can be small herbs, bushy shrubs or tall trees.

Example: Grass is small; rose is a shrub; mango is a tree.

In real life: Trees give shade.

💡 Did you know? The banyan is India's national tree.

🍃Leaves

Leaves come in many shapes, sizes and colours.

Example: Peepal leaf is heart-shaped; neem leaves are small.

In real life: Leaf prints make nice art.

💡 Did you know? Most leaves are green.

🥕Plants as Food

We eat roots, stems, leaves, flowers, fruits and seeds of plants.

Example: Carrot (root), potato (stem), spinach (leaf), cauliflower (flower).

In real life: Our plate has many plant parts.

💡 Did you know? Potato is a stem that grows underground.

🌸Caring for Plants

Plants need water, sunlight, air and soil.

Example: Water plants regularly but not too much.

In real life: Children can grow plants in pots.

💡 Did you know? Planting trees keeps air clean.
