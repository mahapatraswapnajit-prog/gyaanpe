Class 3 EVS Chapter 6: Living in Harmony

NCERT Our Wondrous World · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Humans, plants and animals share the Earth. Animals need forests and rivers, and people like the Chipko villagers and the Bishnois protected trees. Being kind to animals and using resources wisely helps us live in harmony with nature.

Key concepts

🌍Sharing the Earth

Humans, animals and plants all share the Earth.

Example: Forests are homes for elephants, tigers and birds.

In real life: Wildlife sanctuaries protect animals.

💡 Did you know? We should not harm animals or their homes.

🐘Animals Need Space

Animals need forests, rivers and grasslands to live.

Example: Elephants travel long distances for food and water.

In real life: Similipal in Odisha is home to tigers and elephants.

💡 Did you know? When forests are cut, animals come into villages.

🌳Protecting Trees

People have protected trees for centuries.

Example: The Chipko movement: villagers hugged trees to save them.

In real life: Planting trees on birthdays is a good habit.

💡 Did you know? Bishnoi people protect trees and blackbucks.

🦚Kindness to Animals

Be kind to animals: give water in summer, don't throw stones.

Example: Keep a bowl of water for birds.

In real life: Animal shelters care for hurt animals.

💡 Did you know? The peacock is India's national bird.

♻️Using Wisely

Use water, food and paper carefully so nature can last.

Example: Turn off taps, finish your food.

In real life: Cloth bags save plastic.

💡 Did you know? Small actions help the Earth.
