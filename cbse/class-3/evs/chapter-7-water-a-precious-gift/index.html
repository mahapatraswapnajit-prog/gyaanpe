Class 3 EVS Chapter 7: Water — A Precious Gift

NCERT Our Wondrous World · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Water is precious because all living things need it. It comes from rain, rivers, lakes, wells and taps and is used for drinking, cooking, cleaning and farming. We must save water and keep it clean to stay healthy.

Key concepts

💧Water is Life

All living things need water to live.

Example: We drink water; plants need water to grow.

In real life: We cannot live many days without water.

💡 Did you know? Our body is mostly water.

🏞️Sources of Water

Water comes from rain, rivers, lakes, ponds, wells and taps.

Example: Rain fills ponds and rivers.

In real life: Villages use wells and tube wells.

💡 Did you know? Mahanadi is the biggest river of Odisha.

🚿Uses of Water

We use water for drinking, cooking, bathing, washing and farming.

Example: Farmers need water for crops.

In real life: Washing hands keeps us healthy.

💡 Did you know? Factories also use water.

🚰Saving Water

Turn off taps, fix leaks, and reuse water.

Example: Water plants with water used to wash vegetables.

In real life: Rainwater harvesting saves rainwater.

💡 Did you know? A dripping tap wastes many litres a day.

🧼Clean Water

Drink clean water. Boil or filter water if unsure.

Example: Don't throw garbage in ponds or rivers.

In real life: Water purifiers clean water at home.

💡 Did you know? Dirty water causes diseases.
