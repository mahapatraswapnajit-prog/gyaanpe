Class 3 EVS Chapter 8: Food We Eat

NCERT Our Wondrous World · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

We eat different foods in different places, coming from plants and animals grown by farmers. Healthy meals include grains, pulses, fruits, vegetables and milk. Some foods are eaten raw and some cooked, and we should never waste food.

Key concepts

🍛Different Foods

People eat different foods in different places.

Example: Rice and fish in Odisha; roti and dal in Punjab.

In real life: Each state has special dishes.

💡 Did you know? Food depends on what grows nearby.

🌾Where Food Comes From

Food comes from plants and animals.

Example: Rice, wheat and vegetables from plants; milk, eggs and fish from animals.

In real life: Thank farmers for our meals.

💡 Did you know? Farmers grow our food.

🥗Healthy Food

Eat fruits, vegetables, grains, pulses and milk to grow strong.

Example: Dal gives protein; fruits give vitamins.

In real life: A colourful plate is healthy.

💡 Did you know? Too much junk food is not good.

🍳Cooking Food

Some food is eaten raw, some cooked.

Example: Fruits raw; rice and dal cooked.

In real life: Grandmothers' recipes are special.

💡 Did you know? Cooking makes some food soft and safe.

🙏Do Not Waste Food

Take only what you can eat.

Example: Leftover food can be shared.

In real life: Food banks help the hungry.

💡 Did you know? Many people do not get enough food.

Key terms

TermMeaning
GrainSeed like rice or wheat
PulseDal like moong or arhar
RawNot cooked
HealthyGood for the body
WasteThrowing away useful things

Quick revision

  • Food comes from plants and animals.
  • Eat fruits and vegetables daily.
  • Don't waste food.
