Class 3 Maths Chapter 11: Filling and Lifting

NCERT Maths Mela · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Capacity tells how much a container holds and is measured in litres and millilitres, with 1 litre = 1000 mL. Weight tells how heavy something is, measured in kilograms and grams, with 1 kg = 1000 g. A balance shows which object is heavier.

Key concepts

🫙Capacity

Capacity is how much a container can hold.

Example: A bucket holds more water than a glass.

In real life: Milk is sold in litre packets.

💡 Did you know? We measure liquids in litres (L) and millilitres (mL).

💧Litres and Millilitres

1 litre = 1000 millilitres.

Example: Half a litre = 500 mL.

In real life: Water bottles show mL.

💡 Did you know? Small amounts like medicine are in mL.

⚖️Weight

Weight tells how heavy something is. We use kilograms (kg) and grams (g).

Example: A bag of rice is 5 kg; a pencil is a few grams.

In real life: Vegetables are weighed in kg.

💡 Did you know? 1 kilogram = 1000 grams.

🔍Heavier and Lighter

A balance shows which side is heavier.

Example: The heavier side goes down.

In real life: Shopkeepers use balances.

💡 Did you know? Big things are not always heavier: a balloon is big but light.

🥛Comparing Containers

We can compare by filling with glasses of water.

Example: A jug fills 4 glasses; a mug fills 2.

In real life: Cooking uses measuring cups.

💡 Did you know? Using the same small glass makes it fair.
