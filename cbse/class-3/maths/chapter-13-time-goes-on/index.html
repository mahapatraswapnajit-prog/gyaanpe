Class 3 Maths Chapter 13: Time Goes On

NCERT Maths Mela · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Time is measured in seconds, minutes, hours, days, weeks, months and years. A week has 7 days, a year 12 months, and an hour 60 minutes. Clocks show hours and minutes, and calendars show days and months.

Key concepts

📅Days and Weeks

A week has 7 days: Monday to Sunday.

Example: After Friday comes Saturday.

In real life: School timetables use days.

💡 Did you know? Sunday is a holiday in many places.

🗓️Months and Year

A year has 12 months and about 365 days.

Example: January is the first month; December is the last.

In real life: Birthdays come once a year.

💡 Did you know? February has 28 days, or 29 in a leap year.

🕐Reading the Clock

The short hand shows hours; the long hand shows minutes.

Example: At 3 o'clock, the short hand is at 3 and the long hand at 12.

In real life: We wake up by the clock.

💡 Did you know? 1 hour = 60 minutes.

🌅Times of the Day

Morning, afternoon, evening and night.

Example: We have breakfast in the morning.

In real life: Trains run on time-tables.

💡 Did you know? A day has 24 hours.

⏳How Long?

We can measure how long things take.

Example: A cricket match lasts many hours; brushing takes 2 minutes.

In real life: Races are timed.

💡 Did you know? Seconds are smaller than minutes.

  • 1 year = 12 months.
