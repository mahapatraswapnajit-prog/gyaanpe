Class 3 Maths Chapter 4: Vacation with My Nani Maa

NCERT Maths Mela · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Through stories of a vacation, we practise adding (putting together) and subtracting (taking away). Adding tens first helps, and the difference tells how many more. A week has 7 days.

Key concepts

🚂Adding in Stories

Adding means putting together.

Example: Nani gave 12 laddoos and Mama gave 15: 12 + 15 = 27.

In real life: We add when counting gifts.

💡 Did you know? Adding in any order gives the same answer.

🍬Taking Away

Subtracting means taking away.

Example: 25 sweets, 8 eaten: 25 − 8 = 17 left.

In real life: Counting change uses subtraction.

💡 Did you know? We can check by adding back: 17 + 8 = 25.

🔢Using Tens

Add tens first, then ones.

Example: 34 + 23 = 30 + 20 and 4 + 3 = 57.

In real life: Mental maths at the market.

💡 Did you know? Breaking numbers makes adding easy.

🎒How Many More?

Find the difference to know how many more.

Example: Ravi has 15 marbles, Sita 9: Ravi has 6 more.

In real life: Comparing heights uses difference.

💡 Did you know? Difference is found by subtracting.

📅Counting Days

We count days of a vacation on a calendar.

Example: From 1st to 10th May is 10 days.

In real life: Holidays are planned with calendars.

💡 Did you know? A week has 7 days.
