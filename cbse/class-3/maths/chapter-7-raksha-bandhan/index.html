Class 3 Maths Chapter 7: Raksha Bandhan

NCERT Maths Mela · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

When we have equal groups, we can multiply instead of adding again and again. Arrays and tables of 2, 3, 4 and 5 help us multiply quickly, like counting rakhis and sweets on Raksha Bandhan.

Key concepts

🎀Equal Groups

When groups have the same number, we can multiply.

Example: 3 sisters each tie 2 rakhis: 3 groups of 2 = 6.

In real life: Packets of biscuits have equal numbers.

💡 Did you know? Multiplication is quick repeated adding.

➕Repeated Addition

4 + 4 + 4 = 12 is the same as 3 × 4 = 12.

Example: 5 + 5 = 2 × 5 = 10.

In real life: Counting legs of animals uses multiplication.

💡 Did you know? × means 'times' or 'groups of'.

📊Arrays

Objects in rows and columns form an array.

Example: 3 rows of 5 sweets = 15.

In real life: Chairs arranged in rows.

💡 Did you know? Turning the array gives 5 rows of 3, still 15.

🔢Tables of 2, 3, 4, 5

Tables help multiply quickly.

Example: 2 × 6 = 12; 5 × 4 = 20.

In real life: Shopkeepers use tables.

💡 Did you know? Multiplying by 1 keeps the number same.

🎁Gift Sharing

Multiplication helps count gifts and sweets.

Example: 4 boxes with 3 laddoos each = 12 laddoos.

In real life: Festival shopping uses it.

💡 Did you know? Order does not change the product: 3 × 4 = 4 × 3.

Quick revision

  • 3 × 4 = 4 × 3.
  • Any number × 1 = same number.
  • Multiplication is repeated addition.
