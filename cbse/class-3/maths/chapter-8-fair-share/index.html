Class 3 Maths Chapter 8: Fair Share

NCERT Maths Mela · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

A fair share gives everyone the same amount. Sharing equally is division. A whole cut into 2 equal parts gives halves, and into 4 equal parts gives quarters.

Key concepts

🍬Sharing Equally

Fair sharing means everyone gets the same amount.

Example: 12 sweets shared by 3 children: 4 each.

In real life: Sharing food fairly with friends.

💡 Did you know? Sharing equally is called division.

➗Equal Groups

Making equal groups helps divide.

Example: 15 pencils in groups of 5 make 3 groups.

In real life: Packing fruits in boxes.

💡 Did you know? Division is the opposite of multiplication.

🍕Halves

Cutting a whole into 2 equal parts gives halves.

Example: Half of a roti is 1/2.

In real life: Sharing an apple with a friend.

💡 Did you know? Both halves must be equal.

🥧Quarters

Cutting a whole into 4 equal parts gives quarters.

Example: A quarter is 1/4.

In real life: Cutting a cake into 4.

💡 Did you know? 2 quarters make a half.

🤝Is it Fair?

If parts are not equal, it is not a fair share.

Example: A big piece and a small piece are not halves.

In real life: Elders divide prasad equally.

💡 Did you know? Fairness matters in games too.

Quick revision

  • 2 halves make 1 whole.
  • 4 quarters make 1 whole.
  • 12 ÷ 3 = 4.
