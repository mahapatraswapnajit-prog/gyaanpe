Class 4 English Chapter 1: Together We Can

NCERT Santoor · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

This chapter shows that people working together can achieve more. It practises collective nouns, joining words and teamwork vocabulary.

Key concepts

🤝Theme: Teamwork

This chapter in 'My Land' shows how people working together can do what one person cannot.

Example: Many hands make light work.

In real life: Village clean-up drives.

💡 Did you know? Teamwork builds villages, schools and nations.

👥Collective Nouns

Words for groups: a team of players, a flock of birds, a herd of cows.

Example: a bunch of grapes

In real life: Sports teams.

💡 Did you know? Collective nouns name groups.

🔗Joining Words

and, but, or join sentences.

Example: We cleaned the park and planted trees.

In real life: Writing longer sentences.

💡 Did you know? 'and' adds; 'but' shows difference.

💪Teamwork Words

cooperate, help, share, together

Example: Cooperate means work together.

In real life: Group projects.

💡 Did you know? Teams need good listening.

🗣️We and Us

Use we/us for a group including yourself.

Example: We can do it together.

In real life: Class pledges.

💡 Did you know? 'We' is a pronoun.
