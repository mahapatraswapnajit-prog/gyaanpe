Class 4 English Chapter 11: A Journey to the Magical Mountains

NCERT Santoor · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

This chapter takes us on a magical journey to the mountains with snow, valleys and waterfalls. It practises travel and mountain words, prepositions of direction and writing a travel diary.

Key concepts

🏔️Theme: Travel to the Mountains

This chapter takes us on a journey to beautiful mountains.

Example: Mountains have snow, pine trees, waterfalls and cool air.

In real life: Family trips to hill stations.

💡 Did you know? The Himalayas are the highest mountains in the world.

🎒Travel Words

journey, trip, luggage, ticket, guide

Example: We packed our luggage.

In real life: Planning holidays.

💡 Did you know? A guide shows the way.

❄️Mountain Words

peak, valley, slope, snow, glacier

Example: The peak is the top of a mountain.

In real life: Hill stations.

💡 Did you know? Rivers start from glaciers.

📍Prepositions

up, down, across, through, over

Example: We walked up the hill.

In real life: Directions.

💡 Did you know? They show direction and place.

✍️Travel Diary

Writing about a trip: where, when, what we saw.

Example: Day 1: We saw snow!

In real life: Holiday homework.

💡 Did you know? Diaries use past tense.

Key terms

TermMeaning
JourneyTravel from one place to another
PeakTop of a mountain
ValleyLow land between hills
GlacierRiver of ice
LuggageBags for travel

Quick revision

  • Peak = top of a mountain.
  • The Himalayas are the highest.
  • Rivers start from glaciers.

Practice questions

Q1. The top of a mountain is the

  1. Peak
  2. Valley
  3. River
  4. Base
Show answerA) Peak

Q2. Low land between hills is a

  1. Plateau
  2. Desert
  3. Peak
  4. Valley
Show answerD) Valley

Q3. The highest mountains in the world are the

  1. Eastern Ghats
  2. Aravallis
  3. Western Ghats
  4. Himalayas
Show answerD) Himalayas

Q4. A river of ice is a

  1. Pond
  2. Lake
  3. Well
  4. Glacier
Show answerD) Glacier

Q5. Bags for travel are

  1. Luggage
  2. Furniture
  3. Toys
  4. Groceries
Show answerA) Luggage
