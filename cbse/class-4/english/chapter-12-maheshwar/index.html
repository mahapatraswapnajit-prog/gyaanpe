Class 4 English Chapter 12: Maheshwar

NCERT Santoor · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Maheshwar is a heritage town in Madhya Pradesh on the Narmada river, once the capital of Queen Ahilyabai Holkar and famous for handwoven Maheshwari sarees. The chapter practises describing a place and heritage vocabulary.

Key concepts

🏯Theme: A Heritage Town

Maheshwar is a historic town in Madhya Pradesh on the banks of the Narmada river.

Example: It was the capital of Queen Ahilyabai Holkar.

In real life: Ghats along the Narmada.

💡 Did you know? Maheshwar is famous for Maheshwari sarees.

👑Ahilyabai Holkar

Ahilyabai Holkar was a wise and kind queen of the Malwa region in the 18th century.

Example: She built temples, ghats and roads across India.

In real life: Her fort overlooks the river.

💡 Did you know? She supported weavers in Maheshwar.

🧵Handloom

Maheshwari sarees are woven by hand on looms.

Example: Handloom supports artisans' families.

In real life: Craft fairs.

💡 Did you know? National Handloom Day: 7 August.

🗺️Describing a Place

Use describing words: ancient, peaceful, beautiful.

Example: Maheshwar is a peaceful town.

In real life: Travel brochures.

💡 Did you know? Good descriptions help readers imagine.

🏛️Heritage Words

fort, ghat, temple, heritage, artisan

Example: A ghat is a set of steps to a river.

In real life: Heritage walks.

💡 Did you know? Heritage is what we inherit from the past.
