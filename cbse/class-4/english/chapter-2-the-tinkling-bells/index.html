Class 4 English Chapter 2: The Tinkling Bells

NCERT Santoor · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

This chapter enjoys the sound of tinkling bells in everyday life. It practises sound words, rhythm and adjectives that describe sounds.

Key concepts

🔔Theme: Sounds of My Land

This chapter is about the cheerful sound of bells and the life around them.

Example: Bells ring in temples, schools and on cattle.

In real life: School bell timings.

💡 Did you know? Bells are made of metal because metal rings.

🔊Sound Words

tinkle, ring, ding-dong, jingle, clang

Example: Small bells tinkle; big bells clang.

In real life: Bicycle bells.

💡 Did you know? Sound words make writing lively.

🎵Rhyme and Rhythm

Repeating sounds give rhythm.

Example: jingle–tingle

In real life: Songs.

💡 Did you know? Read aloud to enjoy sounds.

🎨Describing Sounds

soft, loud, sweet, sharp

Example: The bell made a sweet sound.

In real life: Music class.

💡 Did you know? Describing words are adjectives.

🐄Bells in Village Life

Cattle wear bells so owners can find them.

Example: Bullock carts with bells.

In real life: Temple aarti.

💡 Did you know? Bells signal time and events.

Key terms

  • Bells are made of metal.

Practice questions

Q1. A light ringing sound is a

  1. Bark
  2. Tinkle
  3. Roar
  4. Moo
Show answerB) Tinkle

Q2. Bells are usually made of

  1. Wood only
  2. Cloth
  3. Paper
  4. Metal
Show answerD) Metal

Q3. Which is a sound word?

  1. sweet
  2. bell
  3. school
  4. jingle
Show answerD) jingle

Q4. Cattle wear bells so that

  1. They look funny
  2. They run faster
  3. Owners can find them
  4. They sleep
Show answerC) Owners can find them

Q5. Which describes a sound?

  1. loud
  2. ring
  3. bell
  4. school
Show answerA) loud
