Class 4 English Chapter 3: Be Smart, Be Safe

NCERT Santoor · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

This chapter teaches being smart and safe on roads, at home and with strangers. It practises safety words, command sentences and saying no to unsafe situations.

Key concepts

🛡️Theme: Staying Safe

This chapter teaches safety at home, on roads and with strangers.

Example: Don't go with strangers; tell a trusted adult.

In real life: Road safety week.

💡 Did you know? Emergency number in India: 112.

🚦Road Safety

Cross at zebra crossings; look right, left, right.

Example: Red means stop; green means go.

In real life: Traffic lights.

💡 Did you know? Wear a helmet on two-wheelers.

🔥Home Safety

Don't touch switches with wet hands; keep away from fire.

Example: Ask adults to handle gas stoves.

In real life: Safe homes.

💡 Did you know? Medicines only from adults.

📢Imperatives

Safety rules are often commands.

Example: Stop. Look. Walk carefully.

In real life: Sign boards.

💡 Did you know? Commands begin with verbs.

🙅Saying No

It's okay to say 'No' to unsafe things.

Example: No to strangers' gifts or rides.

In real life: Good touch, bad touch awareness.

💡 Did you know? Tell parents or teachers.

Practice questions

Q1. At a traffic light, red means

  1. Stop
  2. Dance
  3. Go
  4. Run
Show answerA) Stop

Q2. We should cross roads at a

  1. Corner bend
  2. Bridge rail
  3. Middle of traffic
  4. Zebra crossing
Show answerD) Zebra crossing

Q3. If a stranger offers a ride, you should

  1. Go with them
  2. Take sweets
  3. Say no and tell an adult
  4. Hide
Show answerC) Say no and tell an adult

Q4. India's emergency number is

  1. 123
  2. 000
  3. 112
  4. 999
Show answerC) 112

Q5. On a two-wheeler, wear a

  1. Cap only
  2. Helmet
  3. Garland
  4. Scarf
Show answerB) Helmet
