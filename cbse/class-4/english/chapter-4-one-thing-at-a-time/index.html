Class 4 English Chapter 4: One Thing at a Time

NCERT Santoor · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

This chapter teaches that focusing on one thing at a time gives better results than rushing. It practises to-do lists, order words and the simple present tense.

Key concepts

🎯Theme: Focus

This chapter shows that doing one thing at a time, carefully, is better than rushing to do many things together.

Example: Focus helps us finish work well.

In real life: Doing homework without TV.

💡 Did you know? Rushing causes mistakes.

📋Making a List

A to-do list helps do things one by one.

Example: 1. Homework 2. Play 3. Read

In real life: Planning the day.

💡 Did you know? Tick each task when done.

⏱️Time Words

first, then, after that, finally

Example: First I wash my hands, then I eat.

In real life: Morning routine.

💡 Did you know? Order words show sequence.

🔤Present Tense

Use present tense for daily habits.

Example: I brush my teeth every morning.

In real life: Daily routine.

💡 Did you know? He/she adds -s: She reads.

🧘Calm Mind

A calm mind works better.

Example: Take a deep breath before starting.

In real life: Study breaks.

💡 Did you know? Breaks help focus.

Key terms

TermMeaning
FocusPaying full attention

Quick revision

  • first, then, finally show order.
  • She reads (adds -s).
  • Focus avoids mistakes.
