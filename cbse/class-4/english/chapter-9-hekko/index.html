Class 4 English Chapter 9: Hekko

NCERT Santoor · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

This chapter enjoys playing a game together, practising taking turns, following rules, ordinal numbers and fun describing words.

Key concepts

🎲Theme: Fun with Games

This chapter in the 'Fun with Games' unit is about playing and enjoying a game together.

Example: Games teach rules, turns and patience.

In real life: Playground fun.

💡 Did you know? Many Indian games are played with simple things.

🔁Taking Turns

Players wait for their turn.

Example: 'It's your turn now.'

In real life: Board games.

💡 Did you know? Taking turns is fair.

📜Rules

Rules tell how to play.

Example: Don't step on the line.

In real life: Game cards.

💡 Did you know? Instructions use command sentences.

🔢Ordinal Numbers

first, second, third, fourth

Example: She came first in the race.

In real life: Race results.

💡 Did you know? Ordinal numbers show position.

😄Fun Words

exciting, thrilling, joyful

Example: The game was exciting.

In real life: Game day stories.

💡 Did you know? Describing words make stories lively.

  1. Nothing
  2. How to play
  3. What to eat

  2. second

  2. Unfair
  3. Bad
