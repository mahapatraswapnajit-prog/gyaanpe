Class 4 EVS Chapter 1: Living Together

NCERT Our Wondrous World · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

A community is a group of people who live near each other and help one another. Workers like farmers, doctors and sanitation workers keep it running. Rules help everyone live peacefully, and respecting differences makes communities strong.

Key concepts

🏘️Our Community

People living near each other and helping one another form a community.

Example: Neighbours, shopkeepers, doctors and teachers are part of our community.

In real life: Village meetings decide on common needs.

💡 Did you know? Communities share parks, schools and water sources.

🤝Helping Each Other

Community members help during festivals, illness or disasters.

Example: During floods, people share food and shelter.

In real life: Odisha's people help each other during cyclones.

💡 Did you know? Working together makes hard tasks easy.

👷People Who Help Us

Many workers keep our community running.

Example: Farmers, sanitation workers, postmen, doctors, police.

In real life: Thank workers who help us.

💡 Did you know? Every job is important.

📏Rules

Rules help everyone live together peacefully.

Example: Queue at the ration shop; don't litter.

In real life: Traffic rules prevent accidents.

💡 Did you know? Rules keep us safe and fair.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑Respecting Differences

People in a community may speak different languages and follow different customs.

Example: Celebrating each other's festivals.

In real life: Friends from different states.

💡 Did you know? Diversity makes communities rich.
