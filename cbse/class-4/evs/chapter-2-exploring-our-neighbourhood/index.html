Class 4 EVS Chapter 2: Exploring Our Neighbourhood

NCERT Our Wondrous World · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Our neighbourhood has places like shops, schools, hospitals and parks. Landmarks and maps help us find the way. Knowing emergency services and keeping the area clean and green makes our neighbourhood safe and pleasant.

Key concepts

🗺️My Neighbourhood

A neighbourhood is the area around our home.

Example: Shops, school, temple, park, hospital, post office.

In real life: Draw a map of your area.

💡 Did you know? Each place has a purpose.

🧭Finding the Way

Landmarks help us give directions.

Example: Turn left after the temple and the school is on the right.

In real life: Auto drivers use landmarks.

💡 Did you know? A landmark is an easy-to-see place.

🏥Important Places

Hospitals, police stations and fire stations help in emergencies.

Example: Emergency number in India: 112.

In real life: Ambulance number 108 in many states.

💡 Did you know? Know where the nearest hospital is.

🚌Getting Around

People travel by walking, cycling, buses and autos.

Example: Buses connect villages and towns.

In real life: Cycle lanes in cities.

💡 Did you know? Walking and cycling keep air clean.

🌳Caring for Our Area

Keep the neighbourhood clean and green.

Example: Plant trees, use dustbins.

In real life: Neighbourhood cleanliness drives.

💡 Did you know? Clean areas reduce diseases.
