Class 4 EVS Chapter 5: Food for Health

NCERT Our Wondrous World · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

A balanced meal includes energy foods like rice and millets, body-building foods like dal, milk and eggs, and protective foods like fruits and vegetables. Too much junk food harms our health, so we should choose healthy foods.

Key concepts

🍽️Balanced Meal

A balanced meal has grains, pulses, vegetables, fruits and milk.

Example: Rice, dal, sabzi, curd and a fruit.

In real life: School mid-day meals are planned to be balanced.

💡 Did you know? Eating a variety gives all nutrients.

⚡Energy Foods

Rice, wheat, potatoes and millets give energy.

Example: Ragi is a healthy millet.

In real life: Athletes eat energy foods.

💡 Did you know? Energy helps us play and study.

💪Body-Building Foods

Pulses, milk, eggs, fish and nuts help us grow.

Example: Dal and eggs give protein.

In real life: Growing children need protein.

💡 Did you know? Protein repairs our body.

🍎Protective Foods

Fruits and vegetables protect us from illness.

Example: Oranges and amla give vitamin C.

In real life: A colourful plate is healthy.

💡 Did you know? Green leafy vegetables give iron.

🚫Junk Food

Too much junk food like chips and cold drinks harms health.

Example: Choose fruit instead of chips.

In real life: Read labels on packets.

💡 Did you know? Too much sugar harms teeth.
