Class 4 EVS Chapter 6: Happy and Healthy Living

NCERT Our Wondrous World · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Being healthy and happy needs clean habits, exercise, enough rest and good food. It is okay to have different feelings, and talking to trusted adults helps. When unwell, tell elders, see a doctor, and take medicine only as advised.

Key concepts

🧼Cleanliness

Clean habits keep germs away.

Example: Wash hands, bathe daily, wear clean clothes.

In real life: Hand washing stops many illnesses.

💡 Did you know? Germs are too small to see.

🏃Exercise and Play

Exercise keeps the body strong and the mind fresh.

Example: Yoga, running, kabaddi, kho-kho.

In real life: Sports days in school.

💡 Did you know? Outdoor play is better than long screen time.

😴Rest

Enough sleep helps the body grow and the brain learn.

Example: Children need about 9–10 hours of sleep.

In real life: Rest after hard work.

💡 Did you know? Sleeping early helps wake up fresh.

💬Feelings

It is okay to feel happy, sad, angry or scared. Talking helps.

Example: Tell a parent or teacher if something worries you.

In real life: Friends support each other.

💡 Did you know? Kind words make others happy.

🩺When Sick

Tell elders when you feel unwell and see a doctor.

Example: Take medicine only as the doctor says.

In real life: Health check-ups in school.

💡 Did you know? Vaccines protect us from diseases.
