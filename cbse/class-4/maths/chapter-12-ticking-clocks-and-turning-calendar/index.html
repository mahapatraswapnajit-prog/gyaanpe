Class 4 Maths Chapter 12: Ticking Clocks and Turning Calendar

NCERT Maths Mela · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

We read clocks using the hour and minute hands, use a.m. and p.m., and find how long activities take. Calendars show 12 months, 365 or 366 days, and help us find days and dates.

Key concepts

🕐Reading Time

The hour hand is short; the minute hand is long. Each number on a clock means 5 minutes for the minute hand.

Example: Minute hand on 6 = 30 minutes.

In real life: Bus timetables show times.

💡 Did you know? 1 hour = 60 minutes.

🌞a.m. and p.m.

a.m. is from midnight to noon; p.m. is from noon to midnight.

Example: School starts at 8 a.m.; dinner at 8 p.m.

In real life: Train tickets show a.m./p.m.

💡 Did you know? 12 noon is midday.

⏱️How Long?

Find time taken by counting from start to end.

Example: From 2:00 to 3:30 is 1 hour 30 minutes.

In real life: Cricket match durations.

💡 Did you know? Count hours first, then minutes.

📅Calendar

A year has 12 months and 365 days (366 in a leap year).

Example: 30 days: April, June, September, November.

In real life: Festival dates on calendars.

💡 Did you know? A leap year comes every 4 years.

🗓️Days and Dates

Calendars show which day a date falls on.

Example: If 1st is Monday, 8th is also Monday.

In real life: Planning holidays.

💡 Did you know? A week has 7 days.
