Class 4 Maths Chapter 13: The Transport Museum

NCERT Maths Mela · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

At the transport museum we see how travel changed from carts to trains and planes. We use money for tickets, measure distances in kilometres, find journey times, and multiply to count wheels and seats.

Key concepts

🚂Transport Through Time

People travelled by foot, animals, carts, then trains, cars and planes.

Example: Bullock carts to Vande Bharat trains.

In real life: Rail museums show old engines.

💡 Did you know? India has one of the world's largest railway networks.

🎟️Tickets and Money

We add and subtract money for tickets.

Example: 3 tickets at ₹40 each = ₹120.

In real life: Museum entry tickets.

💡 Did you know? Check change after paying.

📏Distances

Distances between cities are measured in kilometres.

Example: Bhubaneswar to Puri is about 60 km.

In real life: Signboards show distances.

💡 Did you know? Longer trips take more time.

⏰Travel Time

Find how long a journey takes.

Example: Train leaves 9:00, arrives 11:30: 2 hours 30 minutes.

In real life: Railway timetables.

💡 Did you know? Faster vehicles take less time.

🔢Counting Wheels and Seats

Multiply to count wheels and seats.

Example: 5 cars × 4 wheels = 20 wheels.

In real life: Parking lots count vehicles.

💡 Did you know? A bicycle has 2 wheels; an auto has 3.

Key terms

TermMeaning
TransportWays of travelling

Quick revision

  • Auto rickshaw: 3 wheels.
  • Distance between cities in km.
  • Arrival − departure = travel time.
