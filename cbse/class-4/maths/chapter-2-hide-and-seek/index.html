Class 4 Maths Chapter 2: Hide and Seek

NCERT Maths Mela · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Objects look different from the top, front and side. Position words, maps, directions and grids help us describe where things are and find our way.

Key concepts

👀Views of Objects

Objects look different from the top, front and side.

Example: A glass from the top looks like a circle; from the side, like a rectangle.

In real life: Drones show top views of fields.

💡 Did you know? This is how architects draw buildings.

📍Position Words

Words like left, right, above, below, behind and in front tell position.

Example: The ball is behind the chair.

In real life: Giving directions uses position words.

💡 Did you know? Your left is the person facing you's right.

🗺️Simple Maps

A map shows places from the top.

Example: A classroom map shows desks and the door.

In real life: School route maps help new students.

💡 Did you know? Maps use symbols and directions.

🧭Directions

North, south, east and west are the main directions.

Example: The Sun rises in the east.

In real life: Puri is south-east of Bhubaneswar.

💡 Did you know? On most maps, north is at the top.

🎯Grids

A grid of rows and columns helps find positions.

Example: Square B3 is in column B, row 3.

In real life: Seats in a hall use row and number.

💡 Did you know? Games like chess use grids.

Key terms

Quick revision

  • Maps usually have north at the top.
  • Top view of a glass is a circle.
