Class 4 Maths Chapter 5: Sharing and Measuring

NCERT Maths Mela · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Fractions show equal parts of a whole or a group. We find fractions of groups, compare fractions, notice equal fractions like 2/4 = 1/2, and use fractions in measuring length, weight and time.

Key concepts

🍕Fractions

A fraction shows equal parts of a whole.

Example: 1/2 is one of 2 equal parts; 3/4 is three of 4 equal parts.

In real life: Cutting a cake fairly.

💡 Did you know? The parts must be equal.

🧮Fraction of a Group

We can find a fraction of a group of things.

Example: 1/2 of 10 sweets = 5 sweets.

In real life: Sharing marbles among friends.

💡 Did you know? 1/4 of 12 = 3.

⚖️Comparing Fractions

With the same top number, a bigger bottom number means smaller parts.

Example: 1/2 > 1/4.

In real life: A half roti is more than a quarter roti.

💡 Did you know? Same denominator: larger numerator is bigger: 3/4 > 1/4.

📏Measuring with Fractions

We use fractions in measuring.

Example: Half a metre = 50 cm; quarter kilogram = 250 g.

In real life: Shopkeepers sell 1/2 kg packets.

💡 Did you know? Half an hour = 30 minutes.

🍫Equal Fractions

Some fractions show the same amount.

Example: 2/4 = 1/2.

In real life: Two quarters of a chocolate equal a half.

💡 Did you know? Folding paper shows equal fractions.
