Class 4 Maths Chapter 8: Weigh it, Pour it

NCERT Maths Mela · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Weight is measured in grams and kilograms, and capacity in millilitres and litres. 1 kg = 1000 g and 1 L = 1000 mL. We add measures in the same unit, count small containers that fill a big one, and estimate before measuring.

Key concepts

⚖️Weight

Weight tells how heavy something is, in grams (g) and kilograms (kg).

Example: 1 kg = 1000 g.

In real life: Grocery shops use kg.

💡 Did you know? Light things like a pencil are weighed in grams.

🥛Capacity

Capacity is how much liquid a container holds, in millilitres (mL) and litres (L).

Example: 1 L = 1000 mL.

In real life: Milk packets are 500 mL or 1 L.

💡 Did you know? A spoon holds about 5 mL.

➕Adding Weights

Add weights in the same unit.

Example: 500 g + 750 g = 1250 g = 1 kg 250 g.

In real life: Packing a bag of vegetables.

💡 Did you know? Convert to one unit before adding.

🫗Pouring and Filling

Count how many small containers fill a big one.

Example: A 1 L bottle fills 4 glasses of 250 mL.

In real life: Serving juice at a party.

💡 Did you know? Smaller cups need more pours.

🔍Estimating

Guess weight or capacity before measuring.

Example: A bucket holds about 10 L.

In real life: Cooks estimate quantities.

💡 Did you know? Practice makes estimates better.

Quick revision

  • 1 kg = 1000 g.
  • 1 L = 1000 mL.
  • 4 × 250 mL = 1 L.
