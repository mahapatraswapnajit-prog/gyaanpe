Class 4 Maths Chapter 9: Equal Groups

NCERT Maths Mela · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Equal groups can be counted quickly by multiplication, and division splits a number into equal groups. Tables help us calculate fast, the order of multiplying does not matter, and sometimes sharing leaves a remainder.

Key concepts

👥Multiplication

Equal groups can be counted by multiplying.

Example: 6 groups of 4 = 6 × 4 = 24.

In real life: Packets of 6 eggs.

💡 Did you know? Multiplication is repeated addition.

➗Division

Division splits a number into equal groups.

Example: 24 ÷ 4 = 6.

In real life: Sharing laddoos equally.

💡 Did you know? Division undoes multiplication.

📊Tables up to 10

Knowing tables makes multiplying fast.

Example: 7 × 8 = 56; 9 × 6 = 54.

In real life: Shopkeepers multiply prices.

💡 Did you know? Any number × 0 = 0.

🔁Order Doesn't Matter

3 × 5 = 5 × 3 = 15.

Example: Arrays show this when turned.

In real life: Rows and columns of chairs.

💡 Did you know? This helps remember tables.

🍬Remainders

Sometimes things are left over after sharing.

Example: 17 sweets among 5 children: 3 each, 2 left.

In real life: Packing items in boxes.

💡 Did you know? The leftover is called the remainder.

Key terms

TermMeaning
MultiplyFind total of equal groups

Quick revision

  • Division undoes multiplication.
  • 3 × 5 = 5 × 3.
