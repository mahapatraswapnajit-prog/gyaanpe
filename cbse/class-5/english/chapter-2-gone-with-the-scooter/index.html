Class 5 English Chapter 2: Gone with the Scooter

NCERT Santoor · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

This story shows the value of responsibility and careful decision-making. It practises the past continuous tense and road safety vocabulary.

Key concepts

🛵Theme: Responsibility

This story in 'Let's Have Fun' shows the importance of being responsible and careful while making decisions.

Example: Thinking before acting avoids trouble.

In real life: Locking the scooter and keeping the key safe.

💡 Did you know? Responsibility means taking care of our duties.

🔑Responsible Actions

Return things, keep promises, take care of belongings.

Example: Keep your keys in a safe place.

In real life: Class monitor duties.

💡 Did you know? Owning mistakes is responsible.

⏪Past Continuous

Shows an action going on in the past.

Example: 'He was riding the scooter when it rained.'

In real life: Telling stories.

💡 Did you know? was/were + verb-ing.

🚦Road Safety

Riders must wear helmets and follow traffic rules.

Example: Children should not ride scooters.

In real life: Traffic police.

💡 Did you know? Helmets save lives.

💭Decisions

Good decisions come from thinking about results.

Example: 'What will happen if…?'

In real life: Choices every day.

💡 Did you know? Ask an elder when unsure.
