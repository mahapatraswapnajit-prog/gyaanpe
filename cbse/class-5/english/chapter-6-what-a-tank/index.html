Class 5 English Chapter 6: What a Tank!

NCERT Santoor · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

This chapter is about storing and saving water. It practises exclamation sentences, water-saving habits and the idea of rainwater harvesting.

Key concepts

🚰Theme: Saving Water

This chapter in the 'Water' unit is about water storage and using water wisely.

Example: Tanks store water for daily use and dry days.

In real life: Village water tanks.

💡 Did you know? Rainwater can be collected in tanks.

💧Saving Water

Close taps, fix leaks, reuse water.

Example: Use a bucket instead of a shower.

In real life: Water-saving posters.

💡 Did you know? Every drop counts.

❗Exclamations

Exclamations show strong feeling and end with !

Example: What a tank! How big it is!

In real life: Surprise moments.

💡 Did you know? They often start with What or How.

📏Big Numbers

Tanks hold litres of water.

Example: A tank may hold 1,000 litres.

In real life: Water bills.

💡 Did you know? 1 litre = 1,000 millilitres.

🌧️Rainwater Harvesting

Collecting rainwater in tanks or pits.

Example: Rooftop rainwater harvesting.

In real life: School campuses.

💡 Did you know? It refills groundwater.

Key terms

Quick revision

  • Exclamations end with !
  • 1 L = 1,000 mL.
  • Every drop counts.
