Class 5 English Chapter 9: Vocation

NCERT Santoor · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

In Tagore's poem 'Vocation', a child wishes to be a hawker, gardener or watchman because their lives seem free. The chapter discusses kinds of work, 'I wish I were' sentences and the dignity of labour.

Key concepts

🔔Theme: Work is Worship

'Vocation' is a poem by Rabindranath Tagore. A child, tired of school rules, wishes to be a hawker, a gardener or a watchman, who seem free.

Example: The poem shows how children see grown-ups' work.

In real life: Watching the hawker on your street.

💡 Did you know? Tagore won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1913.

👷Kinds of Work

hawker, gardener, watchman, farmer, teacher

Example: A hawker sells things on the street.

In real life: Community helpers.

💡 Did you know? Every job is valuable.

💭Wishes

Use 'I wish I were…' for imaginary wishes.

Example: I wish I were a gardener.

In real life: Dream jobs.

💡 Did you know? 'were' is used for all subjects in wishes.

🎵Poem Features

Repetition and a child's voice make it musical.

Example: The child repeats his wish.

In real life: Recitation.

💡 Did you know? Tagore wrote in Bengali and English.

🙏Dignity of Labour

All honest work deserves respect.

Example: Gardeners and watchmen serve us.

In real life: Thanking helpers.

💡 Did you know? Respect workers in our community.

Key terms
