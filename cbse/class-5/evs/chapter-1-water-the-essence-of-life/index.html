Class 5 EVS Chapter 1: Water — The Essence of Life

NCERT Our Wondrous World · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Water is essential for all life. Rain fills rivers, lakes and groundwater, while most of Earth's water is salty. Traditional systems like stepwells stored water, and today we must save water and keep it clean to prevent diseases.

Key concepts

💧Water for Life

All living things need water. Our body is about two-thirds water.

Example: Plants wilt without water.

In real life: Drink enough water every day.

💡 Did you know? Water helps digest food and keeps the body cool.

🌧️Sources of Water

Rain is the main source of fresh water. It fills rivers, lakes, ponds and groundwater.

Example: Wells and tube wells take groundwater.

In real life: Monsoon rains fill reservoirs.

💡 Did you know? Most of Earth's water is salty sea water.

🏺Traditional Water Systems

People built stepwells, tanks and canals to store water.

Example: Stepwells (baolis) in Rajasthan and Gujarat.

In real life: Old systems still teach water saving.

💡 Did you know? Some temple tanks in Odisha are centuries old.

🚰Saving Water

Save water by fixing leaks, reusing water and harvesting rainwater.

Example: Water plants with water used for washing vegetables.

In real life: Rooftop rainwater harvesting.

💡 Did you know? A leaking tap can waste many litres a day.

🧪Clean Water

Dirty water spreads diseases like cholera and diarrhoea.

Example: Boil or filter water before drinking if unsure.

In real life: Water purifiers at home.

💡 Did you know? ORS helps during diarrhoea.
