Class 5 EVS Chapter 3: The Mystery of Food

NCERT Our Wondrous World · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Food contains nutrients like carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins and minerals. Iodine shows starch by turning blue-black. Food spoils due to microbes, heat and moisture, and can be preserved by drying, salting, pickling or cooling. Millets like ragi are very nutritious.

Key concepts

🔬What's in Food?

Food has nutrients: carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins and minerals.

Example: Rice gives carbohydrates; dal gives proteins.

In real life: Food labels list nutrients.

💡 Did you know? We need a mix of nutrients every day.

🧪Testing for Starch

Iodine turns blue-black where starch is present.

Example: Potato and rice turn blue-black with iodine.

In real life: Food scientists test food.

💡 Did you know? This is an easy kitchen experiment with an adult.

🦠Food Spoilage

Food spoils because of tiny microbes, heat and moisture.

Example: Bread gets fungus; milk turns sour.

In real life: Check expiry dates.

💡 Did you know? Spoilt food can make us sick.

🧂Preserving Food

Drying, salting, refrigerating, and making pickles or jams keep food longer.

Example: Mango pickle, dried fish, papad.

In real life: Refrigerators slow spoilage.

💡 Did you know? Sugar and salt stop microbes from growing.

🌾Millets

Millets like ragi, jowar and bajra are nutritious and need less water.

Example: Ragi is rich in calcium.

In real life: Ragi porridge is healthy.

💡 Did you know? Odisha's Millet Mission promotes ragi.

Quick revision

  • Iodine + starch → blue-black.
  • Salt and sugar preserve food.
  • Ragi is rich in calcium.
