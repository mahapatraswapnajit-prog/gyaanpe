Class 5 EVS Chapter 4: Our School — A Happy Place

NCERT Our Wondrous World · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

A school is a happy place to learn, play and make friends. Teachers, students, cooks, cleaners and guards all help. Kindness, respect and saying no to bullying, keeping the school clean and green, and learning through clubs and games make school joyful.

Key concepts

🏫Our School

A school is where we learn, play and make friends.

Example: Classrooms, library, playground, garden.

In real life: School assemblies start the day.

💡 Did you know? Everyone in school has a role.

👩‍🏫People in School

Teachers, students, principal, cooks, cleaners and guards keep the school running.

Example: Mid-day meal cooks prepare food.

In real life: Parents and teachers meet in PTMs.

💡 Did you know? Thank everyone who helps.

🤝Kindness and Respect

A happy school has kind and respectful people.

Example: Help a new student; never bully anyone.

In real life: Buddy systems in schools.

💡 Did you know? Bullying hurts; tell a teacher if you see it.

🧹Clean and Green

Keep classrooms and grounds clean and plant trees.

Example: Use dustbins; save water and electricity.

In real life: Cleanliness drives.

💡 Did you know? School gardens teach growing food.

🎭Learning by Doing

Clubs, games, art and projects make learning fun.

Example: Science fair, sports day, drama.

In real life: Annual day celebrations.

💡 Did you know? Everyone has different talents.
