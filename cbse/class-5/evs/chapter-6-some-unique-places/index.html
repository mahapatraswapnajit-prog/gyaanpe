Class 5 EVS Chapter 6: Some Unique Places

NCERT Our Wondrous World · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

India has unique places: Ladakh, a cold desert; Mawsynram, one of the wettest places with living root bridges; the white salt Rann of Kutch; coral islands like Lakshadweep; and Odisha's Chilika Lake with migratory birds and dolphins. People adapt their lives to each place.

Key concepts

🏔️Ladakh

Ladakh is a cold desert with very little rain and freezing winters.

Example: Houses have thick walls and flat roofs.

In real life: Tourists visit Pangong Lake.

💡 Did you know? Yaks and double-humped camels live there.

🌧️Mawsynram

Mawsynram in Meghalaya is one of the wettest places on Earth.

Example: People use bamboo umbrellas called knups.

In real life: Root bridges are tourist spots.

💡 Did you know? Living root bridges are made from tree roots.

🏜️The Rann of Kutch

The Rann of Kutch in Gujarat is a huge white salt desert.

Example: It floods in the monsoon and dries into salt flats.

In real life: Salt is collected here.

💡 Did you know? The Rann Utsav festival is held there.

🏝️Islands

India's islands have unique life: Andaman & Nicobar and Lakshadweep.

Example: Lakshadweep has coral islands.

In real life: Ships and planes connect islands.

💡 Did you know? Coral reefs are home to colourful fish.

🦩Chilika Lake

Chilika in Odisha is Asia's largest brackish water lagoon.

Example: Thousands of migratory birds visit in winter.

In real life: Boat rides to see dolphins.

💡 Did you know? Irrawaddy dolphins live in Chilika.

Key terms
