Class 5 EVS Chapter 7: Energy — How Things Work

NCERT Our Wondrous World · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Energy lets us do work and comes from sources like the Sun, wind, water, coal and petrol. Renewable sources don't run out, but coal and petrol can. Electricity is made in power plants, including hydroelectric dams like Hirakud, and we should save energy.

Key concepts

⚡What is Energy?

Energy is the ability to do work. We need energy to move, grow and play.

Example: Food gives our body energy.

In real life: Cooking uses heat energy.

💡 Did you know? Energy changes from one form to another.

☀️Sources of Energy

Energy comes from the Sun, wind, water, coal, petrol and gas.

Example: Solar panels use sunlight; windmills use wind.

In real life: Solar lamps in villages.

💡 Did you know? The Sun is the main source of energy on Earth.

♻️Renewable and Non-renewable

Sun, wind and water are renewable; coal and petrol can run out.

Example: Coal takes millions of years to form.

In real life: Solar parks in India.

💡 Did you know? Using renewable energy reduces pollution.

🔌Electricity

Electricity powers lights, fans and machines.

Example: It is made in power plants using coal, water, wind or sun.

In real life: Switch off when not in use.

💡 Did you know? Hirakud Dam makes hydroelectricity.

💡Saving Energy

Save energy by switching off lights, using LED bulbs and walking or cycling.

Example: LED bulbs use less electricity.

In real life: Energy-saving stars on appliances.

💡 Did you know? Saving energy saves money and nature.
