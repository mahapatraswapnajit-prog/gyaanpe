Class 5 EVS Chapter 8: Clothes — How Things are Made

NCERT Our Wondrous World · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Clothes are made from natural fibres like cotton, wool, silk and jute, or man-made ones like polyester. Fibres are spun into yarn and woven or knitted into fabric. We wear clothes suited to the weather, India has famous handlooms like Sambalpuri ikat, and we should mend and reuse clothes.

Key concepts

🌱Fibres

Clothes are made from fibres: natural (cotton, wool, silk, jute) or man-made (polyester, nylon).

Example: Cotton comes from cotton plants; wool from sheep.

In real life: Labels show fibre type.

💡 Did you know? Silk comes from silkworm cocoons.

🧶From Fibre to Fabric

Fibres are spun into yarn, then woven or knitted into fabric.

Example: Spinning wheel (charkha) makes yarn.

In real life: Handloom weaving.

💡 Did you know? Gandhiji promoted khadi made on the charkha.

🌡️Clothes for Weather

Cotton keeps us cool in summer; wool keeps us warm in winter.

Example: Raincoats keep us dry.

In real life: Seasonal wardrobes.

💡 Did you know? Light colours feel cooler in the sun.

🎨Indian Textiles

India has famous handloom textiles.

Example: Sambalpuri ikat, Bomkai and Kotpad from Odisha; Banarasi; Kanjeevaram.

In real life: Handloom Day is on 7 August.

💡 Did you know? Ikat is dyed before weaving.

♻️Caring for Clothes

Mend, reuse and donate clothes instead of throwing them.

Example: Old clothes become rags or quilts.

In real life: Clothes donation drives.

💡 Did you know? Making clothes uses a lot of water.
