Class 5 Maths Chapter 1: We the Travellers — I

NCERT Maths Mela · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Travelling involves big numbers. We read and write numbers up to one lakh using place value, add distances to find totals, and subtract to find how far is left.

Key concepts

🚆Big Numbers in Travel

Travel uses big numbers: distances, fares and passengers.

Example: Bhubaneswar to Delhi is about 1,700 km by train.

In real life: Train tickets show distance and fare.

💡 Did you know? Indian Railways carries crores of passengers.

🔢Numbers up to 1,00,000

After 99,999 comes 1,00,000 (one lakh).

Example: 45,320 = forty-five thousand three hundred twenty.

In real life: Bike prices are in ten thousands.

💡 Did you know? 5 digits: ten thousands, thousands, hundreds, tens, ones.

🏠Place Value

In 63,458, 6 is in the ten thousands place: 60,000.

Example: 1 ten thousand = 10 thousands.

In real life: Cheques use number places.

💡 Did you know? Commas make big numbers easy to read.

➕Adding Distances

Add distances of trip parts to get the total.

Example: 320 km + 485 km = 805 km.

In real life: Planning a road trip.

💡 Did you know? Line up places before adding.

➖How Far Still?

Subtract to find the remaining distance.

Example: Total 1,200 km, travelled 750 km: 450 km left.

In real life: Maps show distance left.

💡 Did you know? Check by adding back.

Quick revision

  • 99,999 + 1 = 1,00,000.
  • 1 ten thousand = 10 thousands.
  • Remaining = total − travelled.
