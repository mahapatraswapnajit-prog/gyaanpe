Class 5 Maths Chapter 14: Maps and Locations

NCERT Maths Mela · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Maps show places from above using symbols and a key. Directions and grids help us locate places, and routes can be described with directions and distances. Odisha lies on India's east coast along the Bay of Bengal, with Bhubaneswar as its capital.

Key concepts

🗺️Reading Maps

Maps show places, roads and landmarks from above.

Example: A school map shows rooms, playground and gate.

In real life: City maps help visitors.

💡 Did you know? Symbols and a key explain the map.

🧭Directions

North, south, east and west help describe location.

Example: The library is east of the office.

In real life: Compasses point north.

💡 Did you know? North-east is between north and east.

🔲Grids

A grid divides a map into squares named by letters and numbers.

Example: The temple is in square C4.

In real life: Atlas index uses grids.

💡 Did you know? Grids help find places quickly.

🛤️Following Routes

Describe a route with directions and distances.

Example: Walk 200 m north, turn east, walk 100 m.

In real life: Delivery apps show routes.

💡 Did you know? Landmarks make routes easy to follow.

📍Our State on the Map

Odisha lies on the east coast of India along the Bay of Bengal.

Example: Bhubaneswar is the capital of Odisha.

In real life: School atlases show states.

💡 Did you know? Odisha borders West Bengal, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh and Andhra Pradesh.
