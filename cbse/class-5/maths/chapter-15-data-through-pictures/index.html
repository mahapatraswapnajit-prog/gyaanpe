Class 5 Maths Chapter 15: Data Through Pictures

NCERT Maths Mela · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

We collect data through surveys and show it in tables, pictographs and bar graphs. Reading the key or scale carefully lets us find the most, least, totals and differences, and use data to make good decisions.

Key concepts

📋Collecting Data

Data answers questions like 'Which fruit do classmates like?'

Example: Survey your class and record answers.

In real life: Census collects data about people.

💡 Did you know? Organise data in a table.

🖼️Pictographs

Pictographs use pictures with a key.

Example: If 🍎 = 5 children, 🍎🍎🍎 = 15 children.

In real life: Posters use pictographs.

💡 Did you know? Half a picture shows half the value.

📊Bar Graphs

Bar graphs use bars; the scale shows value.

Example: Scale 1 box = 2 children: 6 boxes = 12 children.

In real life: Report cards and charts.

💡 Did you know? Bars have equal widths and gaps.

🔍Reading Graphs

Find most, least, totals and differences.

Example: Mango 15, banana 9: mango has 6 more.

In real life: Shops read sales graphs.

💡 Did you know? Always check the key or scale first.

🧠Making Conclusions

Data helps make decisions.

Example: If most children like mango, buy more mangoes for the party.

In real life: Governments plan using data.

💡 Did you know? Good data leads to good decisions.
