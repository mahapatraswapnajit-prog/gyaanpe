Class 5 Maths Chapter 9: Coconut Farm

NCERT Maths Mela · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

At a coconut farm we multiply to count coconuts and earnings, multiply quickly by 10 and 100, divide to pack in groups, and use factors and multiples to plan equal rows.

Key concepts

🥥Counting Coconuts

Coconut farms count trees and coconuts using multiplication.

Example: 40 trees × 60 coconuts = 2,400 coconuts.

In real life: Coconut water is healthy.

💡 Did you know? Kerala and Odisha's coast grow coconuts.

🔢Multiplying by 10 and 100

Add zeros to multiply by 10 or 100.

Example: 35 × 10 = 350; 35 × 100 = 3,500.

In real life: Counting crates.

💡 Did you know? Easy multiplication saves time.

📦Packing in Groups

Divide to pack coconuts in bags or crates.

Example: 2,400 coconuts in bags of 50 = 48 bags.

In real life: Market packing.

💡 Did you know? Division tells how many groups.

💵Selling

Multiply quantity by price to find earnings.

Example: 100 coconuts × ₹25 = ₹2,500.

In real life: Farmers' income.

💡 Did you know? Profit = selling amount − cost.

🔍Factors and Multiples

Multiples come from multiplying; factors divide exactly.

Example: Multiples of 6: 6, 12, 18; factors of 12: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12.

In real life: Planting in neat rows.

💡 Did you know? Arranging trees in equal rows uses factors.
