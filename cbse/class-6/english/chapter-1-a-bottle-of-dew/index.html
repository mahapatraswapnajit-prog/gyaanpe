Class 6 English Chapter 1: A Bottle of Dew

NCERT Poorvi · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

This folk tale shows that patience and hard work matter more than shortcuts. The chapter practises nouns: common and proper nouns, and singular and plural forms.

Key concepts

💧Theme: Hard Work

This folk tale is about hard work and patience versus looking for shortcuts.

Example: Real success comes from steady effort.

In real life: Farmers work daily for a good harvest.

💡 Did you know? Folk tales pass values from one generation to the next.

🔤Nouns

A noun names a person, place, animal, thing or idea.

Example: boy, village, dew, bottle, patience

In real life: Every sentence needs naming words.

💡 Did you know? Ideas like patience and courage are also nouns.

🏷️Common and Proper Nouns

Common nouns are general names; proper nouns are particular names and begin with capital letters.

Example: river (common), Mahanadi (proper)

In real life: Names on maps are proper nouns.

💡 Did you know? Days and months are proper nouns: Monday, June.

👥Singular and Plural

Plural nouns usually add -s or -es; some change form.

Example: bottle → bottles, box → boxes, child → children

In real life: Shopping lists use plurals.

💡 Did you know? Some nouns stay the same: sheep, deer.

📖Reading Folk Tales

Folk tales often have a lesson at the end, called a moral.

Example: 'Hard work pays' is a moral.

In real life: Grandparents tell folk tales.

💡 Did you know? Folk tales come from many cultures.

Key terms

NounNaming word

Quick revision

  • Proper nouns start with capital letters.
  • child → children.
  • Folk tales usually have a moral.
