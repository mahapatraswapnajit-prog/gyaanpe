Class 6 English Chapter 10: Change of Heart

NCERT Poorvi · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

The chapter shows a change of heart and the value of sportsmanship and teamwork. It practises adverbs of manner, time and place.

Key concepts

🏅Theme: Sportsmanship

The chapter is about how a player's attitude changes, showing that being a good sport matters more than winning.

Example: Shaking hands after a match shows respect.

In real life: Fair play awards in tournaments.

💡 Did you know? Good sportsmanship means playing fair.

🏃Adverbs

Adverbs describe verbs, telling how, when or where.

Example: She ran quickly. He came early. They played outside.

In real life: Coaches say 'run faster!'

💡 Did you know? Many adverbs end in -ly.

⚖️Adverbs of Manner, Time, Place

Manner: how (slowly). Time: when (today). Place: where (here).

Example: 'He spoke politely.'

In real life: Instructions use adverbs.

💡 Did you know? Not all adverbs end in -ly: fast, well.

💭Change of Heart

A change of heart means changing one's feelings or opinion.

Example: From jealousy to friendship.

In real life: Saying sorry after a mistake.

💡 Did you know? People can grow and change.

🤝Teamwork

Teams win when members support each other.

Example: Passing the ball, cheering teammates.

In real life: Kabaddi needs teamwork.

💡 Did you know? Together Everyone Achieves More (TEAM).

Quick revision

  • Many adverbs end in -ly.
  • 'well' is the adverb of 'good'.
  • Fair play matters more than winning.
