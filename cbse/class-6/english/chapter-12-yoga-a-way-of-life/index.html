Class 6 English Chapter 12: Yoga — A Way of Life

NCERT Poorvi · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Yoga is an ancient Indian way of life combining postures, breathing and meditation for a healthy body and calm mind. The chapter practises imperative sentences and writing clear instructions.

Key concepts

🧘Theme: Yoga

Yoga is an ancient Indian practice for a healthy body and calm mind.

Example: Asanas (postures), pranayama (breathing) and meditation.

In real life: Many schools begin with yoga.

💡 Did you know? International Yoga Day is on 21 June.

🫁Pranayama

Pranayama is controlled breathing that calms the mind.

Example: Deep breathing before exams.

In real life: Doctors recommend breathing exercises.

💡 Did you know? Breathing exercises help focus.

📢Imperative Sentences

Imperatives give instructions or commands.

Example: 'Sit straight. Breathe slowly. Close your eyes.'

In real life: Recipes and yoga guides use imperatives.

💡 Did you know? They often begin with a verb.

📋Instructions

Good instructions are clear, short and in order.

Example: First stand straight, then raise your arms.

In real life: Exercise charts.

💡 Did you know? Numbering steps helps.

🌞Healthy Habits

Yoga with good food and sleep keeps us healthy.

Example: Surya Namaskar has 12 steps.

In real life: Morning routine.

💡 Did you know? Regular practice matters more than difficult poses.
