Class 6 English Chapter 3: Rama to the Rescue

NCERT Poorvi · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

The story celebrates presence of mind and courage. The chapter practises verbs, especially the simple past tense and irregular verbs, and putting events in sequence.

Key concepts

🦸Theme: Presence of Mind

The story celebrates quick thinking and courage in a tricky situation.

Example: Staying calm helps solve problems.

In real life: Children who call 112 in emergencies show presence of mind.

💡 Did you know? Presence of mind means thinking clearly when something unexpected happens.

🏃Verbs

Verbs are action or being words.

Example: run, think, shout, is, was

In real life: Instructions use verbs: 'Close the door.'

💡 Did you know? Every sentence has a verb.

⏪Simple Past Tense

Simple past tells actions that already happened. Many verbs add -ed.

Example: help → helped, call → called

In real life: Diaries use the past tense.

💡 Did you know? Irregular verbs change: run → ran, go → went.

🔁Irregular Verbs

Some verbs don't follow the -ed rule.

Example: see → saw, think → thought, catch → caught

In real life: Story telling uses them.

💡 Did you know? Learn them by practice.

🗺️Sequencing

Good stories have a beginning, middle and end.

Example: Use words like first, then, finally.

In real life: Recipe steps.

💡 Did you know? Sequencing helps retell stories.
