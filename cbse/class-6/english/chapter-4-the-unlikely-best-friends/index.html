Class 6 English Chapter 4: The Unlikely Best Friends

NCERT Poorvi · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

The chapter shows that friendship can grow between very different beings. It practises pronouns: personal, possessive and reflexive pronouns.

Key concepts

🤝Theme: Unlikely Friendship

The chapter shows that friends can be very different from each other and still care deeply.

Example: Friendship can grow across differences.

In real life: Classmates from different places become friends.

💡 Did you know? Real friends accept each other as they are.

👤Pronouns

Pronouns replace nouns to avoid repeating them.

Example: he, she, it, they, we, you, I

In real life: Pronouns make writing smooth.

💡 Did you know? 'Ravi is kind. He helps everyone.'

🔁Possessive Pronouns

Possessive pronouns show ownership.

Example: mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs

In real life: Labelling belongings.

💡 Did you know? 'This book is mine.'

🪞Reflexive Pronouns

Reflexive pronouns refer back to the subject.

Example: myself, herself, themselves

In real life: Self-help tasks.

💡 Did you know? 'She made it herself.'

💬Friendly Words

Kind words strengthen friendships.

Example: please, thank you, sorry, well done

In real life: Writing a thank-you note.

💡 Did you know? Listening is part of friendship.

Key terms

TermMeaning
PronounWord used instead of a noun
Possessive pronounShows ownership
Reflexive pronounRefers back to the subject
FriendshipBond of care and trust
UnlikelyNot expected

Quick revision

  • he/she/it replace nouns.
  • mine, yours, ours show ownership.
  • myself, herself are reflexive.
