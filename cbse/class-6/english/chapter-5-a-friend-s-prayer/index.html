Class 6 English Chapter 5: A Friend's Prayer

NCERT Poorvi · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

This poem is a wish to be a good and faithful friend. The chapter practises rhyme, stanzas, words describing friendship and reading poems aloud with feeling.

Key concepts

🙏Theme: What Makes a Good Friend

This poem expresses the wish to be a good, caring and faithful friend.

Example: A good friend is loyal, kind and honest.

In real life: Being there for a friend in hard times.

💡 Did you know? Prayers in poems express deep wishes.

🎵Rhyme

Rhyming words end with the same sound.

Example: friend–end, true–you, care–share

In real life: Songs use rhyme.

💡 Did you know? Rhyme makes poems musical.

📜Stanza

A stanza is a group of lines in a poem.

Example: A poem may have three stanzas.

In real life: Song verses are stanzas.

💡 Did you know? A stanza is like a paragraph in prose.

💖Qualities of Friendship

Words describing a good friend: loyal, honest, caring, patient.

Example: 'A loyal friend stands by you.'

In real life: Choosing friends wisely.

💡 Did you know? Opposites: loyal–disloyal, honest–dishonest.

🗣️Reading Poems Aloud

Read with feeling, pause at commas and full stops.

Example: Practise rhythm by clapping.

In real life: Poetry recitation in school.

💡 Did you know? Poems are meant to be heard.

Key terms

  • A stanza is a group of lines.
