Class 6 English Chapter 8: What a Bird Thought

NCERT Poorvi · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

In this poem, a young bird thinks the world is only as big as what it has seen, and its idea changes as it grows. The chapter explores point of view, similes and rhythm, and shows how learning widens our world.

Key concepts

🐣Theme: A Growing World

In this poem, a young bird's idea of the world keeps changing as it grows and sees more.

Example: What we see shapes what we think the world is.

In real life: Each new class shows us more of the world.

💡 Did you know? Learning more widens our view.

👁️Point of View

A poem can be told from a character's point of view.

Example: Here we see the world through a bird's eyes.

In real life: Imagining how others see things.

💡 Did you know? Changing the point of view changes the story.

🔁Similes

A simile compares using 'like' or 'as'.

Example: as blue as the sky; soft like a feather

In real life: Everyday talk: 'as cool as ice'.

💡 Did you know? Similes make writing vivid.

🎵Rhythm and Rhyme

Poems have a beat (rhythm) and often rhyme.

Example: Clap to feel the rhythm.

In real life: Nursery rhymes.

💡 Did you know? Short lines can give a quick beat.

🌍Growing Up

As we grow, our understanding grows.

Example: A baby knows only home; a student learns about the world.

In real life: Travel opens our minds.

💡 Did you know? Curiosity helps us grow.

Key terms

Quick revision

  • Similes use 'like' or 'as'.
  • Our view grows as we learn.
  • Poems have rhythm.

Practice questions

Q1. The poem shows that the bird's idea of the world

  1. Changes as it grows
  2. Never changes
  3. Is about the sea only
  4. Is always correct
Show answerA) Changes as it grows

Q2. A comparison using 'like' or 'as' is a

  1. Verb
  2. Simile
  3. Noun
  4. Rhyme
Show answerB) Simile

Q3. 'as soft as a feather' is a

  1. Pronoun
  2. Question
  3. Simile
  4. Command
Show answerC) Simile

Q4. The poem is told from the point of view of a

  1. King
  2. Teacher
  3. Bird
  4. Fish
Show answerC) Bird

Q5. As we learn more, our view of the world

  1. Gets smaller
  2. Grows wider
  3. Stays the same
  4. Disappears
Show answerB) Grows wider
