Class 6 Science Chapter 1: The Wonderful World of Science

NCERT Curiosity · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Science is a way of exploring the world by asking questions, observing carefully, guessing explanations and testing them with experiments. It is everywhere, from the kitchen to space. Scientific discoveries improve our lives, and using them wisely is part of being a good citizen.

Key concepts

🔍Science is Everywhere

Science helps us understand things around us, from cooking to rainbows.

Example: Why does milk boil over but water does not?

In real life: Cooking, farming and medicine all use science.

💡 Did you know? Every child is a natural scientist who asks 'why?'.

❓Asking Questions

Science begins with curiosity and questions.

Example: Why do leaves change colour?

In real life: Doctors ask questions to find causes of illness.

💡 Did you know? Good questions can be tested.

👀Observing

Scientists observe carefully and record what they see.

Example: Noting the Moon's shape each night.

In real life: Birdwatchers keep careful records.

💡 Did you know? Writing observations in a notebook helps later.

🧪Testing Ideas

A guess is tested with an experiment, changing one thing at a time.

Example: Does a plant grow without sunlight? Test with two pots.

In real life: New medicines are tested carefully.

💡 Did you know? Results may surprise us and lead to new questions.

🌏Science and Society

Science and technology improve lives but must be used wisely.

Example: Vaccines protect us from diseases.

In real life: Satellites help forecast weather.

💡 Did you know? Indian scientists like J.C. Bose studied plants and radio waves.
