Class 6 Science Chapter 10: Living Creatures: Exploring their Characteristics

NCERT Curiosity · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Living things share characteristics: they need food, grow, respire, respond to stimuli, excrete waste and reproduce. Seeds need water, air and suitable warmth to germinate. Living things go through life cycles, like the frog's egg-tadpole-adult stages. These features help us tell living from non-living things.

Key concepts

🌱What Makes Something Alive?

Living things grow, need food, breathe, respond to changes, get rid of waste and reproduce.

Example: A puppy grows into a dog; a stone does not grow.

In real life: Doctors check breathing to see if someone is alive.

💡 Did you know? Plants also respond, like sunflowers turning towards light.

🫁Respiration

All living things respire to get energy from food.

Example: Plants and animals both take in and give out gases.

In real life: Fish breathe dissolved oxygen through gills.

💡 Did you know? Plants respire day and night.

⚡Response to Stimuli

Living things react to changes around them, called stimuli.

Example: Touch-me-not (Mimosa) leaves fold when touched.

In real life: Animals run from danger.

💡 Did you know? Our eyes blink when light is too bright.

🌰Seeds and Germination

Seeds need water, air and suitable warmth to germinate.

Example: Moong seeds sprout when kept moist.

In real life: Farmers soak seeds before sowing.

💡 Did you know? Sunlight is not needed for germination but is needed later to make food.

🔁Life Cycles

Living things go through stages from birth to death, called a life cycle.

Example: Frog: egg → tadpole → froglet → adult frog.

In real life: Removing stagnant water prevents mosquito breeding.

💡 Did you know? Mosquitoes breed in stagnant water.

Key terms

StimulusA change that causes a response
RespirationGetting energy from food

  • Frog: egg → tadpole → adult.

Q3. Seeds need which of these to germinate?

  1. Only sunlight
  2. Water, air and warmth
