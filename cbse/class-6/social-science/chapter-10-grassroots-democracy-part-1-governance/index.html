Class 6 Social Science Chapter 10: Grassroots Democracy — Part 1: Governance

NCERT Exploring Society: India and Beyond · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Governance is how decisions are made and implemented. India's government has three organs: the legislature makes laws, the executive implements them and the judiciary interprets them. Government works at the Union, state and local levels. Grassroots democracy lets people participate directly in local decisions.

Key concepts

🏛️What is Governance?

Governance is how decisions are made and carried out for a society.

Example: Deciding where to build a school is governance.

In real life: Citizens can give feedback on government work.

💡 Did you know? Good governance is fair, transparent and accountable.

⚖️Three Organs

The legislature makes laws, the executive carries them out, and the judiciary interprets laws and settles disputes.

Example: Parliament is the legislature at the national level.

In real life: Courts protect citizens' rights.

💡 Did you know? This separation of powers prevents misuse of power.

🗺️Levels of Government

India has governments at the national (Union), state and local levels.

Example: The Odisha government runs state matters like police and health.

In real life: Your panchayat or municipality is closest to you.

💡 Did you know? Local governments handle villages and towns.

🗳️Democracy

In a democracy, people choose representatives through elections.

Example: Adults aged 18 and above can vote.

In real life: Voting is both a right and a duty.

💡 Did you know? Elections are held by the Election Commission.

🌱Grassroots Democracy

Grassroots democracy means people taking part in decisions at the local level.

Example: Village meetings decide on local needs like wells and roads.

In real life: Gram Sabhas meet regularly in villages.

💡 Did you know? Local government brings democracy close to people.
