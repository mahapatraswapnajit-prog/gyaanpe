Class 6 Social Science Chapter 2: Oceans and Continents

NCERT Exploring Society: India and Beyond · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

About 71% of Earth's surface is water. There are seven continents, with Asia the largest and Australia the smallest, and five oceans, with the Pacific the largest. Oceans support rich life, influence weather like the monsoon, and have long connected people through trade, as Kalinga's sailors did.

Key concepts

🌍Land and Water

About 71% of Earth's surface is covered by water; the rest is land.

Example: That is why Earth looks blue from space.

In real life: Astronauts call Earth the 'Blue Planet'.

💡 Did you know? Most of Earth's water is in oceans.

🗺️Seven Continents

The seven continents are Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Antarctica, Europe and Australia.

Example: Asia is the largest continent; Australia is the smallest.

In real life: India is in Asia.

💡 Did you know? Antarctica is covered with ice and has no permanent population.

🌊Five Oceans

The five oceans are the Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Southern and Arctic.

Example: The Pacific is the largest and deepest ocean.

In real life: Ships carry goods across oceans.

💡 Did you know? The Indian Ocean is named after India.

🐋Life in Oceans

Oceans are home to fish, whales, corals and many other living things.

Example: Coral reefs are very rich in life.

In real life: Fishing supports coastal communities like those in Odisha.

💡 Did you know? The Mariana Trench in the Pacific is the deepest place on Earth.

🛳️Oceans Connect Us

Oceans affect weather and connect countries through trade.

Example: Monsoon winds come from the Indian Ocean.

In real life: Odisha celebrates Bali Jatra remembering these voyages.

💡 Did you know? Ancient Kalinga traders sailed to Southeast Asia.
