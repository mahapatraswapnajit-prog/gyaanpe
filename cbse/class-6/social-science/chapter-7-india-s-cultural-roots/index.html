Class 6 Social Science Chapter 7: India's Cultural Roots

NCERT Exploring Society: India and Beyond · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

India's cultural roots include the Vedas, of which the Rig Veda is the oldest, and the Upanishads that explore deep questions about life. Gautama Buddha and Vardhamana Mahavira taught paths of right conduct and non-violence. Folk and tribal traditions add rich art, music and respect for nature.

Key concepts

📖The Vedas

The Vedas are among the oldest texts in the world. The Rig Veda is the oldest.

Example: Vedic hymns were passed down orally for centuries.

In real life: UNESCO recognises Vedic chanting as heritage.

💡 Did you know? Careful chanting methods preserved them accurately.

🤔The Upanishads

The Upanishads discuss big questions about life, the self and the universe.

Example: They often use dialogues between teachers and students.

In real life: Many thinkers still read them.

💡 Did you know? They are part of the later Vedic texts.

☸️Buddhism

Gautama Buddha taught that suffering can end through the Middle Path and right conduct.

Example: He gave his first sermon at Sarnath.

In real life: Dhauli in Odisha has a Shanti Stupa.

💡 Did you know? Buddhism spread across Asia.

🙏Jainism

Vardhamana Mahavira taught ahimsa (non-violence) and simple living.

Example: Jains are careful not to harm any living being.

In real life: Many Jains avoid eating after sunset.

💡 Did you know? Udayagiri-Khandagiri caves near Bhubaneswar were used by Jain monks.

🥁Folk and Tribal Traditions

Folk and tribal communities have rich traditions of nature worship, art and songs.

Example: Odisha's tribal communities have unique dances and arts.

In real life: Folk songs keep history alive.

💡 Did you know? Many traditions respect forests, rivers and animals.
