Class 6 Social Science Chapter 8: Unity in Diversity, or 'Many in the One'

NCERT Exploring Society: India and Beyond · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

India has great diversity in languages, festivals, food and clothing, yet shared stories, values and national symbols bind us together. Harvest festivals like Pongal, Bihu, Onam and Nuakhai, epics told in many languages, and common values show the idea of 'many in the one'.

Key concepts

🗣️Many Languages

India has hundreds of languages and dialects. 22 languages are listed in the Constitution's Eighth Schedule.

Example: Odia, Hindi, Tamil, Bengali and Marathi are some of them.

In real life: Many Indians speak more than one language.

💡 Did you know? Odia is a classical language of India.

🎉Many Festivals

Festivals celebrate harvests, seasons and faiths across India.

Example: Pongal, Bihu, Onam, Baisakhi and Nuakhai are harvest festivals.

In real life: People of all faiths join in local festivals.

💡 Did you know? Nuakhai celebrates the new rice crop in western Odisha.

🍛Food and Clothing

Food and clothes differ by region but share common ideas.

Example: Rice is common in the east and south; wheat in the north.

In real life: Sambalpuri and Bomkai sarees are famous in Odisha.

💡 Did you know? The saree is draped in many regional styles.

🔗Shared Threads

Common stories, values and practices connect people across regions.

Example: The Ramayana and Mahabharata are told in many languages.

In real life: National festivals unite everyone.

💡 Did you know? Respect for elders and hospitality are shared values.

🤝Many in the One

Diversity is our strength, and unity holds us together.

Example: The national flag and anthem belong to all Indians.

In real life: Exchange programmes connect students from different states.

💡 Did you know? Learning about other cultures builds friendship.
