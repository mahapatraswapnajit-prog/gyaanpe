Class 7 English Chapter 10: The Tunnel

NCERT Poorvi · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

This suspenseful adventure story set around a tunnel shows courage. The chapter explores how writers build suspense and practises conjunctions for joining ideas.

Key concepts

🚇Theme: Travel and Adventure

This suspenseful story is set around a tunnel and shows courage in an adventure.

Example: Suspense keeps readers wanting to know what happens next.

In real life: Railway tunnels in hilly areas.

💡 Did you know? Tunnels connect places through mountains.

😮Suspense

Suspense is the feeling of excitement or worry about what will happen.

Example: Short sentences and questions build suspense.

In real life: Mystery stories.

💡 Did you know? Writers hide information to create suspense.

🔗Conjunctions

Conjunctions join words or sentences.

Example: and, but, or, because, so, although, when

In real life: Joining ideas in writing.

💡 Did you know? 'It was dark, but he kept walking.'

🦁Courage

Courage is facing fear to do the right thing.

Example: Staying calm in danger.

In real life: Rescue workers.

💡 Did you know? Courage doesn't mean no fear.

🚆Tunnels in India

India has long tunnels in hills and mountains.

Example: Atal Tunnel in Himachal Pradesh is one of the world's longest high-altitude highway tunnels.

In real life: Konkan Railway tunnels.

💡 Did you know? Tunnels make travel faster and safer.

Quick revision

  • 'but' shows contrast.
  • 'because' gives a reason.
  • Courage is acting despite fear.
