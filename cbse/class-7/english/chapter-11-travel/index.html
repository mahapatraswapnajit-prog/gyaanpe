Class 7 English Chapter 11: Travel

NCERT Poorvi · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

In Millay's poem 'Travel', the speaker hears train whistles and longs to travel anywhere. The chapter explores sound imagery, repetition, mood and the joy of discovering new places.

Key concepts

🚂Theme: Longing to Travel

In this poem by Edna St. Vincent Millay, the speaker hears train whistles and longs to travel anywhere.

Example: The pull of new places is called wanderlust.

In real life: Watching trains pass by.

💡 Did you know? Millay was an American poet who won the Pulitzer Prize.

🔊Sound Imagery

Poets use sounds to create mood.

Example: A distant train whistle at night.

In real life: Listening to rain.

💡 Did you know? Sound imagery makes poems feel real.

💭Longing

Longing means a strong wish for something.

Example: Longing for holidays or faraway places.

In real life: Missing home when away.

💡 Did you know? Poems often express longing.

🔁Repetition

Repeating words or ideas adds emphasis.

Example: Coming back again and again to the idea of taking any train shows strong desire.

In real life: Chants and songs.

💡 Did you know? Repetition gives rhythm.

🌍Benefits of Travel

Travel teaches us about people, food and cultures.

Example: Visiting Konark, Darjeeling or Kerala.

In real life: School excursions.

💡 Did you know? Travel broadens the mind.

  • Millay was an American poet.
  • Wanderlust = desire to travel.
