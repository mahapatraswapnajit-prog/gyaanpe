Class 7 English Chapter 12: Conquering the Summit

NCERT Poorvi · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

The chapter celebrates the courage, teamwork and determination needed to reach a mountain summit. It shares facts about Mount Everest and its climbers and practises superlative adjectives.

Key concepts

🏔️Theme: Determination in Adventure

The chapter is about the courage and determination needed to climb a great mountain summit.

Example: Mountaineers train hard and work as a team.

In real life: Mountaineering institutes in India.

💡 Did you know? A summit is the highest point of a mountain.

🗻Mount Everest

Mount Everest is the highest mountain in the world, about 8,849 m.

Example: Tenzing Norgay and Edmund Hillary first reached the top in 1953.

In real life: Everest lies on the Nepal–China border.

💡 Did you know? Bachendri Pal was the first Indian woman to climb Everest (1984).

📏Superlatives

Superlatives show the highest degree.

Example: highest, tallest, coldest, most difficult

In real life: Records and facts.

💡 Did you know? Use 'the' before superlatives.

🤝Teamwork and Safety

Climbers depend on guides, teammates and careful planning.

Example: Sherpas are expert mountain guides.

In real life: Trekking groups.

💡 Did you know? Respecting nature keeps climbers safe.

💪Never Giving Up

Climbing teaches patience and resilience.

Example: Step by step, camp by camp.

In real life: Life goals.

💡 Did you know? Resilience means bouncing back from difficulty.
