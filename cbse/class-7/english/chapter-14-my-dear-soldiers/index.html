Class 7 English Chapter 14: My Dear Soldiers

NCERT Poorvi · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

This poem speaks directly to soldiers with love and gratitude for guarding us in harsh conditions. The chapter explores tone, the poetic device of addressing someone directly, and writing a letter.

Key concepts

💌Theme: Gratitude to Soldiers

This poem expresses love and gratitude to soldiers who protect us while we live in peace.

Example: Soldiers stand guard in heat, cold and rain.

In real life: Writing letters and rakhis to soldiers.

💡 Did you know? Gratitude means feeling thankful.

🗣️Apostrophe in Poetry

Addressing someone directly in a poem is called apostrophe.

Example: 'My dear soldiers, we thank you.'

In real life: Letters and speeches.

💡 Did you know? The poem speaks directly to soldiers.

❄️Hard Conditions

Soldiers serve in tough places like Siachen, among the highest battlefields in the world.

Example: Temperatures there can fall far below zero.

In real life: Documentaries about soldiers.

💡 Did you know? Their sacrifice lets us live safely.

🎵Tone of a Poem

Tone shows the poet's feeling: grateful, proud, emotional.

Example: This poem's tone is respectful and loving.

In real life: Patriotic songs.

💡 Did you know? Word choice sets tone.

✉️Writing a Letter

A letter has date, greeting, body and closing.

Example: 'Dear Soldier, … Yours gratefully, Riya.'

In real life: Letters to soldiers on special days.

💡 Did you know? Informal letters can be warm and personal.
