Class 7 English Chapter 5: A Funny Man

NCERT Poorvi · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

This light-hearted poem describes a funny man who does things in silly ways, showing how rhyme, rhythm and opposites create humour. The chapter practises descriptive adjectives and reminds us to enjoy humour kindly.

Key concepts

🤪Theme: Humour in Poetry

This light-hearted poem describes a funny man who does things in silly, upside-down ways.

Example: Nonsense poems play with silly ideas for fun.

In real life: Comic poems in children's magazines.

💡 Did you know? Laughter is good for health.

🎵Rhyme and Rhythm

Funny poems use strong rhyme and bouncy rhythm.

Example: Clap along to feel the beat.

In real life: Jingles in ads.

💡 Did you know? Rhyme makes lines easy to remember.

🔄Opposites for Fun

Doing things the opposite way creates humour.

Example: Wearing a shoe on the head instead of the foot.

In real life: Comedy shows.

💡 Did you know? Exaggeration also makes things funny.

🎨Adjectives of Quality

Adjectives describe what kind.

Example: funny, silly, strange, cheerful

In real life: Character sketches.

💡 Did you know? Use vivid adjectives in descriptions.

😊Enjoying Humour Kindly

Good humour makes everyone laugh, not hurts anyone.

Example: Laugh with others, not at them.

In real life: Class fun time.

💡 Did you know? Kind jokes build friendship.

  • Laugh with others, not at them.
  • Opposites can create humour.

Practice questions

Q1. The poem is mainly

  1. Scary
  2. Sad
  3. Funny
  4. Angry
Show answerC) Funny

Q2. A silly, playful poem is a

  1. Prayer
  2. Nonsense poem
  3. Report
  4. War poem
Show answerB) Nonsense poem

Q3. Wearing a shoe on the head is funny because it is

  1. The opposite of normal
  2. Expensive
  3. Safe
  4. Normal
Show answerA) The opposite of normal

Q4. Which is an adjective of quality?

  1. quickly
  2. man
  3. run
  4. silly
Show answerD) silly

Q5. Making something bigger than real is

  1. Plural
  2. Reduction
  3. Exaggeration
  4. Rhyme
Show answerC) Exaggeration
