Class 7 English Chapter 7: My Brother's Great Invention

NCERT Poorvi · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

This humorous story about a brother's invention shows curiosity and the fun of trying new ideas, even when they go wrong. The chapter practises the past continuous tense and introduces Indian scientists.

Key concepts

💡Theme: Dreams and Discoveries

This humorous story is about a brother's enthusiastic attempt at an invention.

Example: Curiosity drives invention, even when ideas go wrong.

In real life: School science fairs.

💡 Did you know? Many great inventions began as funny failures.

⏳Past Continuous Tense

Shows an action going on in the past: was/were + verb-ing.

Example: 'My brother was building a machine when the bell rang.'

In real life: Storytelling.

💡 Did you know? Often used with 'when' and 'while'.

🔬Indian Inventors and Scientists

India has many great scientists and innovators.

Example: C.V. Raman won the Nobel Prize for the Raman Effect (1930).

In real life: Science exhibitions.

💡 Did you know? National Science Day is on 28 February.

😂Humour in Stories

Humour can come from unexpected results.

Example: An invention that does the opposite of what was planned.

In real life: Funny family stories.

💡 Did you know? Laughing at our own mistakes helps us learn.

🛠️Trying and Improving

Inventors test, fail, fix and test again.

Example: Prototype → test → improve.

In real life: Startups improve products.

💡 Did you know? Patience is needed in invention.
