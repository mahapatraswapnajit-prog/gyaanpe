Class 7 Maths Chapter 2: Arithmetic Expressions

NCERT Ganita Prakash · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

An arithmetic expression combines numbers with operations, and its value is found by following an order: brackets first, then multiplication and division, then addition and subtraction. Expressions can be broken into terms. Swapping, grouping and the distributive property help calculate quickly.

Key concepts

🧮What is an Expression?

An arithmetic expression is a combination of numbers and operations, like 13 + 2 × 6.

Example: The value of 30 − 5 × 4 is 10.

In real life: A shop bill is an expression: items' prices added together.

💡 Did you know? Two different expressions can have the same value, like 10 + 2 and 6 × 2.

➕Terms in an Expression

Terms are the parts of an expression that are added. A minus sign belongs to the term after it.

Example: In 25 − 6 × 2 + 4, the terms are 25, −6 × 2 and 4.

In real life: Splitting a bill into parts uses terms.

💡 Did you know? Adding terms in any order gives the same result.

🎛️Brackets

Brackets tell us which part to calculate first.

Example: (5 + 3) × 2 = 16, but 5 + 3 × 2 = 11.

In real life: Calculators follow the same order rules.

💡 Did you know? Brackets can change the value of an expression completely.

🔁Swapping and Grouping

Addition and multiplication can be done in any order and grouped in any way without changing the value.

Example: 27 + 48 + 73 = (27 + 73) + 48 = 148.

In real life: Adding prices that make round hundreds is quicker.

💡 Did you know? Smart grouping makes mental maths faster.

🧩Distributive Property

Multiplying a sum is the same as multiplying each part and adding: a × (b + c) = a × b + a × c.

Example: 6 × 104 = 6 × 100 + 6 × 4 = 624.

In real life: Shopkeepers use it to multiply quickly in their heads.

💡 Did you know? This is why 99 × 7 = 700 − 7 = 693.

Quick revision

  • Multiplication and division are done before addition and subtraction.
  • Brackets are solved first.
  • Changing the order of addition does not change the sum.
