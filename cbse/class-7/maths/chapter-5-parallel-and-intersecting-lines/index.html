Class 7 Maths Chapter 5: Parallel and Intersecting Lines

NCERT Ganita Prakash · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

When two lines intersect, vertically opposite angles are equal and adjacent angles form linear pairs that add up to 180°. Parallel lines never meet. A transversal crossing parallel lines makes equal corresponding angles and equal alternate angles.

Key concepts

✖️Intersecting Lines

Two lines that cross each other meet at one point and form four angles.

Example: The two hands of scissors form intersecting lines.

In real life: Road crossings are examples of intersecting lines.

💡 Did you know? At the point of intersection, the four angles add up to 360°.

🔄Vertically Opposite Angles

When two lines intersect, the angles opposite each other are equal.

Example: If one angle is 50°, the angle opposite it is also 50°.

In real life: Carpenters use this to check cuts.

💡 Did you know? Neighbouring angles at the crossing add up to 180°.

➖Linear Pair

Two adjacent angles on a straight line add up to 180°.

Example: If one angle is 120°, the other is 60°.

In real life: A door opening forms a linear pair with the wall.

💡 Did you know? A straight angle measures 180°.

🛤️Parallel Lines

Lines on a plane that never meet, however far extended, are parallel.

Example: Railway tracks and opposite edges of a ruler are parallel.

In real life: Notebook ruled lines are parallel.

💡 Did you know? The distance between two parallel lines stays the same everywhere.

📐Transversal and Angles

A line crossing two parallel lines is a transversal. Corresponding angles and alternate angles formed are equal.

Example: If a corresponding angle is 70°, the matching angle is also 70°.

In real life: Zebra crossings show a transversal across parallel lines.

💡 Did you know? If corresponding angles are equal, the lines are parallel.

Key terms

TermMeaning
Intersecting linesLines that cross at a point
Vertically opposite anglesOpposite angles at an intersection, always equal
Linear pairAdjacent angles on a line adding to 180°
Parallel linesLines that never meet
TransversalA line that crosses two or more lines

Quick revision

  • Vertically opposite angles are equal.
  • A linear pair adds up to 180°.
  • With parallel lines, corresponding angles are equal.

Practice questions

Q1. When two lines intersect, vertically opposite angles are

  1. Supplementary
  2. Unequal
  3. Equal
  4. Always 90°
Show answerC) Equal

Q2. Two angles of a linear pair add up to

  1. 180°
  2. 360°
  3. 90°
  4. 270°
Show answerA) 180°

Q3. If one angle of a linear pair is 120°, the other is

  1. 240°
  2. 120°
  3. 80°
  4. 60°
Show answerD) 60°

Q4. Two lines intersect and one angle is 50°. The angle opposite to it is

  1. 40°
  2. 310°
  3. 130°
  4. 50°
Show answerD) 50°

Q5. Lines that never meet are called

  1. Perpendicular
  2. Transversal
  3. Parallel
  4. Intersecting
Show answerC) Parallel
