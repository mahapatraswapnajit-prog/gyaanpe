Class 7 Maths Chapter 6: Number Play

NCERT Ganita Prakash · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Number play explores patterns such as parity rules for adding even and odd numbers, magic squares where every line has the same sum, and Virahanka numbers where each term is the sum of the two before it. Digit sums help test divisibility and solve puzzles.

Key concepts

⚫Parity: Even and Odd

Even + even = even, odd + odd = even, and even + odd = odd.

Example: 7 + 9 = 16 is even; 4 + 5 = 9 is odd.

In real life: Pairing objects quickly tells if a count is odd or even.

💡 Did you know? The sum of an odd count of odd numbers is odd.

🔲Magic Squares

In a magic square, every row, column and diagonal has the same sum, called the magic sum.

Example: In a 3 × 3 magic square using 1 to 9, the magic sum is 15.

In real life: Magic squares appear in old Indian temples and books.

💡 Did you know? The centre of that square is always 5.

🌀Virahanka Numbers

Each number is the sum of the two before it: 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21...

Example: After 13 and 21 comes 34.

In real life: They appear in flower petals and pinecone spirals.

💡 Did you know? Virahanka described these numbers centuries before Fibonacci, while studying poetry rhythms.

🔢Digit Sums

Adding the digits of a number can reveal patterns, like divisibility by 3 and 9.

Example: For 729, the digit sum is 18, so 729 is divisible by 9.

In real life: Shopkeepers check bills using digit tricks.

💡 Did you know? A number is divisible by 3 if its digit sum is divisible by 3.

🧩Grid Puzzles

Grid puzzles use parity and sums to find missing numbers.

Example: If a row must sum to 12 and has 5 and 3, the missing number is 4.

In real life: Sudoku-style puzzles use the same logic.

💡 Did you know? Thinking about odd and even can rule out answers fast.

Quick revision

  • odd + odd = even.
  • 3 × 3 magic square with 1 to 9 has magic sum 15.
  • Divisible by 9 if the digit sum is divisible by 9.

Practice questions

Q1. What is the parity of 7 + 9?

  1. Even
  2. Neither
  3. Both
  4. Odd
Show answerA) Even

Q2. Even + odd always gives

  1. An even number
  2. A multiple of 4
  3. Zero
  4. An odd number
Show answerD) An odd number

Q3. In a 3 × 3 magic square using numbers 1 to 9, the magic sum is

  1. 15
  2. 12
  3. 18
  4. 45
Show answerA) 15

Q4. What comes next: 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, ...?

  1. 21
  2. 18
  3. 20
  4. 26
Show answerA) 21

Q5. The sum of three odd numbers is

  1. Sometimes even
  2. Always even
  3. Always odd
  4. Always zero
Show answerC) Always odd
