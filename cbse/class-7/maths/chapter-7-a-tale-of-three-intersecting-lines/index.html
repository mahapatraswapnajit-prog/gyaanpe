Class 7 Maths Chapter 7: A Tale of Three Intersecting Lines

NCERT Ganita Prakash · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

A triangle is formed by three intersecting lines and has three sides, angles and vertices. Triangles are classified by sides (equilateral, isosceles, scalene) and by angles (acute, right, obtuse). The angles always add up to 180°, and any two sides together must be longer than the third.

Key concepts

🔺Triangles

A triangle is formed by three line segments; it has 3 sides, 3 angles and 3 vertices.

Example: Triangle ABC has sides AB, BC and CA.

In real life: Roof trusses use triangles for strength.

💡 Did you know? The triangle is the strongest shape, which is why bridges use it.

📏Types by Sides

Equilateral: all sides equal; isosceles: two sides equal; scalene: no sides equal.

Example: A triangle with sides 5, 5 and 7 cm is isosceles.

In real life: Road signs often use equilateral triangles.

💡 Did you know? Every equilateral triangle has all angles equal to 60°.

📐Types by Angles

Acute: all angles less than 90°; right: one angle 90°; obtuse: one angle more than 90°.

Example: Angles 30°, 60° and 90° make a right triangle.

In real life: A set square is a right triangle.

💡 Did you know? A triangle can have only one right or one obtuse angle.

➕Angle Sum Property

The three angles of any triangle add up to 180°.

Example: If two angles are 50° and 60°, the third is 70°.

In real life: Surveyors use it to find unknown angles.

💡 Did you know? Tearing the corners of a paper triangle and joining them makes a straight line.

⚖️Triangle Inequality

The sum of any two sides of a triangle must be greater than the third side.

Example: Sides 3, 4 and 8 cm cannot make a triangle because 3 + 4 < 8.

In real life: Shortest path rules in maps use this idea.

💡 Did you know? This helps check if a triangle is possible before drawing.

Quick revision

  • Angles of a triangle add up to 180°.
  • Each angle of an equilateral triangle is 60°.
  • Any two sides together must be longer than the third.

Practice questions

Q1. The sum of the angles of a triangle is

  1. 270°
  2. 360°
  3. 180°
  4. 90°
Show answerC) 180°

Q2. Two angles of a triangle are 50° and 60°. The third angle is

  1. 80°
  2. 90°
  3. 70°
  4. 60°
Show answerC) 70°

Q3. A triangle with all sides equal is called

  1. Equilateral
  2. Scalene
  3. Isosceles
  4. Right
Show answerA) Equilateral

Q4. Each angle of an equilateral triangle measures

  1. 60°
  2. 30°
  3. 90°
  4. 45°
Show answerA) 60°

Q5. A triangle with sides 5 cm, 5 cm and 7 cm is

  1. Equilateral
  2. Right
  3. Scalene
  4. Isosceles
Show answerD) Isosceles
