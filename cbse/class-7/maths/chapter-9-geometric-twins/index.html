Class 7 Maths Chapter 9: Geometric Twins

NCERT Ganita Prakash · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Congruent figures have the same shape and size. Two triangles are congruent when their corresponding sides and angles match, which can be shown using the SSS, SAS, ASA or RHS rules. Equal angles alone (AAA) only show the same shape, not the same size.

Key concepts

👯Congruent Figures

Figures with exactly the same shape and size are congruent. One fits exactly on the other.

Example: Two ₹10 coins of the same type are congruent.

In real life: Machine-made parts like bottle caps are congruent.

💡 Did you know? The symbol ≅ means 'is congruent to'.

🔺Congruent Triangles

Two triangles are congruent if their matching sides and angles are all equal.

Example: If △ABC ≅ △PQR, then AB = PQ and ∠A = ∠P.

In real life: Bridge trusses use congruent triangles for balance.

💡 Did you know? Matching parts are called corresponding parts.

📏SSS Rule

If three sides of one triangle equal three sides of another, the triangles are congruent.

Example: Triangles with sides 3, 4, 5 cm and 4, 5, 3 cm are congruent.

In real life: A triangle frame with fixed sides cannot change shape.

💡 Did you know? SSS means Side-Side-Side.

📐SAS and ASA Rules

SAS: two sides and the angle between them are equal. ASA: two angles and the side between them are equal.

Example: AB = PQ, ∠B = ∠Q, BC = QR gives △ABC ≅ △PQR by SAS.

In real life: Surveyors use ASA to measure distances across rivers.

💡 Did you know? The angle in SAS must be the one between the two sides.

⬛RHS Rule

In right triangles, if the hypotenuse and one side are equal, the triangles are congruent.

Example: Two right triangles with hypotenuse 10 cm and a side 6 cm are congruent.

In real life: Ladders leaning the same way on a wall make congruent right triangles.

💡 Did you know? RHS means Right angle-Hypotenuse-Side.

Key terms

TermMeaning
CongruentSame shape and same size
Corresponding partsMatching sides and angles
SSSSide-Side-Side rule
SASTwo sides and included angle
RHSRight angle, hypotenuse and a side

Quick revision

  • ≅ means congruent.
  • SSS, SAS, ASA and RHS prove triangles congruent.
  • AAA does not prove congruence.

Practice questions

Q1. Two figures with the same shape and size are called

  1. Congruent
  2. Equal angled
  3. Similar
  4. Parallel
Show answerA) Congruent

Q2. The symbol for 'is congruent to' is

  1. =
  2. ≅
  3. ≈
  4. ~
Show answerB) ≅

Q3. If △ABC ≅ △PQR, which side is equal to AB?

  1. PR
  2. QR
  3. BC
  4. PQ
Show answerD) PQ

Q4. SSS stands for

  1. Side-Sum-Side
  2. Same-Shape-Size
  3. Side-Side-Side
  4. Side-Shape-Side
Show answerC) Side-Side-Side

Q5. In the SAS rule, the angle must be

  1. The angle between the two sides
  2. A right angle
  3. Any angle
  4. The largest angle
Show answerA) The angle between the two sides
