Class 7 Science Chapter 10: Life Processes in Plants

NCERT Curiosity · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Green plants make food by photosynthesis using sunlight, water and carbon dioxide, trapped by chlorophyll, and release oxygen. Stomata allow gas exchange and water loss by transpiration. Xylem carries water and minerals up, while phloem carries food. Starch stored in leaves can be tested with iodine, which turns blue-black.

Key concepts

🌿Photosynthesis

Green plants make their own food using sunlight, water and carbon dioxide. Chlorophyll in leaves traps sunlight.

Example: Carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen (in sunlight).

In real life: Indoor plants grow better near windows.

💡 Did you know? Oxygen we breathe comes from photosynthesis.

🍃Stomata

Tiny pores on leaves called stomata let carbon dioxide in and oxygen and water vapour out.

Example: Stomata are mostly on the underside of leaves.

In real life: Plants lose water through stomata on hot days.

💡 Did you know? Guard cells open and close the stomata.

🚰Transport in Plants

Xylem carries water and minerals from roots to leaves; phloem carries food from leaves to other parts.

Example: Water rises from roots to the top of a tall tree through xylem.

In real life: Fruits get sugar through phloem.

💡 Did you know? Celery in coloured water shows how water moves up.

💨Transpiration

Loss of water vapour from leaves is called transpiration. It helps pull water up and cools the plant.

Example: Covering a plant with a plastic bag shows water drops inside.

In real life: Areas with many trees feel cooler.

💡 Did you know? Trees add moisture to the air through transpiration.

🧪Testing for Starch

Glucose made in leaves is stored as starch. Iodine turns blue-black in the presence of starch.

Example: A leaf kept in sunlight shows starch with iodine.

In real life: Potatoes store starch.

💡 Did you know? A leaf kept in the dark for days may show no starch.

Key terms

Quick revision

  • Photosynthesis releases oxygen.
  • Iodine turns blue-black with starch.
  • Xylem: water; phloem: food.

Practice questions

Q1. Plants make food by

  1. Transpiration
  2. Digestion
  3. Photosynthesis
  4. Respiration
Show answerC) Photosynthesis

Q2. The green pigment that traps sunlight is

  1. Haemoglobin
  2. Iodine
  3. Chlorophyll
  4. Starch
Show answerC) Chlorophyll

Q3. Which gas is released during photosynthesis?

  1. Nitrogen
  2. Oxygen
  3. Carbon dioxide
  4. Hydrogen
Show answerB) Oxygen

Q4. Tiny pores on leaves are called

  1. Xylem
  2. Stomata
  3. Spiracles
  4. Villi
Show answerB) Stomata

Q5. Water is carried from roots to leaves by

  1. Phloem
  2. Xylem
  3. Stomata
  4. Chlorophyll
