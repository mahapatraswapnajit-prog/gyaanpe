Class 7 Science Chapter 11: Light: Shadows and Reflections

NCERT Curiosity · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Light travels in straight lines, so opaque objects block it and form shadows, while transparent and translucent objects let light through fully or partly. Light bounces off shiny surfaces in reflection, with the angle of incidence equal to the angle of reflection. A plane mirror forms an upright, same-size, laterally inverted image.

Key concepts

➡️Light Travels in Straight Lines

Light travels in straight lines, which is why we cannot see around corners.

Example: A pinhole camera forms an upside-down image.

In real life: Torch beams show light going straight.

💡 Did you know? Light from the Sun takes about 8 minutes to reach Earth.

🪟Transparent, Translucent, Opaque

Transparent objects let almost all light pass, translucent ones let some pass, and opaque ones block it.

Example: Clear glass is transparent, butter paper is translucent, wood is opaque.

In real life: Frosted glass in bathrooms is translucent.

💡 Did you know? Only opaque objects form clear, dark shadows.

🌑Shadows

A shadow forms when an opaque object blocks light. Its size and direction depend on the light source's position.

Example: Shadows are longest in early morning and evening.

In real life: Shadow puppets use this idea.

💡 Did you know? A shadow is always dark, whatever the object's colour.

🪞Reflection

When light falls on a shiny surface, it bounces back. Angle of incidence equals angle of reflection.

Example: A mirror reflects light to form an image.

In real life: Rear-view mirrors help drivers see behind.

💡 Did you know? Smooth surfaces give clear reflections; rough ones scatter light.

🔁Image in a Plane Mirror

A plane mirror forms an image that is upright, the same size, and laterally inverted (left and right swapped).

Example: The word AMBULANCE is written reversed on ambulances.

In real life: A periscope uses mirrors to see over walls.

💡 Did you know? The image appears as far behind the mirror as the object is in front.

Key terms

Quick revision

  • Light travels in straight lines.
  • Angle of incidence = angle of reflection.
  • Plane mirror images are upright and laterally inverted.

Practice questions

Q1. Light travels in

  1. Straight lines
  2. Zig-zag lines
  3. Circles
  4. Curved lines
Show answerA) Straight lines

Q2. Which object forms a dark shadow?

  1. Wooden board
  2. Butter paper
  3. Clear glass
  4. Clean water
Show answerA) Wooden board

Q3. Butter paper is

  1. Opaque
  2. Translucent
  3. Luminous
  4. Transparent
Show answerB) Translucent

Q4. A pinhole camera forms an image that is

  1. Upside down
  2. Coloured only
  3. Upright
  4. Invisible
Show answerA) Upside down

Q5. The angle of incidence is equal to the angle of

  1. Shadow
  2. Reflection
  3. Rotation
  4. Refraction
Show answerB) Reflection
