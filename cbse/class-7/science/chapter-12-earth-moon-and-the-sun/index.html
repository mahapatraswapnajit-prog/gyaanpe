Class 7 Science Chapter 12: Earth, Moon, and the Sun

NCERT Curiosity · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Earth rotates on its axis in about 24 hours, causing day and night, and revolves around the Sun in about 365¼ days. The tilt of Earth's axis causes seasons. The Moon shines by reflected sunlight and shows phases in a cycle of about 29.5 days. Solar and lunar eclipses happen when the Sun, Earth and Moon line up.

Key concepts

🌍Rotation

Earth spins on its axis from west to east once in about 24 hours, causing day and night.

Example: The side facing the Sun has day; the other side has night.

In real life: Our daily routine follows day and night.

💡 Did you know? Because Earth rotates west to east, the Sun appears to rise in the east.

🔄Revolution

Earth goes around the Sun once in about 365¼ days, which is one year.

Example: The extra ¼ day adds up to a leap year every 4 years.

In real life: Summer and winter holidays follow the seasons.

💡 Did you know? Earth's tilted axis, along with revolution, causes seasons.

🌙The Moon

The Moon has no light of its own; it reflects sunlight.

Example: The Moon revolves around Earth in about 27 days.

In real life: Chandrayaan missions explored the Moon.

💡 Did you know? The same side of the Moon always faces Earth.

🌗Phases of the Moon

The changing shapes of the Moon we see are its phases, from new moon to full moon and back in about 29.5 days.

Example: Amavasya is the new moon; Purnima is the full moon.

In real life: Many festivals follow the Moon's phases.

💡 Did you know? Phases happen because we see different amounts of the Moon's lit side.

🌑Eclipses

A solar eclipse happens when the Moon comes between the Sun and Earth. A lunar eclipse happens when Earth comes between the Sun and the Moon.

Example: A lunar eclipse can occur only on a full moon night.

In real life: Eclipses are predicted years in advance.

💡 Did you know? Never look at the Sun directly during a solar eclipse.

Practice questions

Q1. Day and night are caused by Earth's

  1. Moon
  2. Tilt only
  3. Revolution
  4. Rotation
Show answerD) Rotation

Q2. Earth takes about how long to revolve around the Sun?

  1. 24 hours
  2. 365¼ days
  3. 29.5 days
  4. 27 days
Show answerB) 365¼ days

Q3. The Sun appears to rise in the east because Earth rotates

  1. East to west
  2. West to east
  3. North to south
  4. It does not rotate
Show answerB) West to east

Q4. The Moon shines because it

  1. Reflects sunlight
  2. Burns gas
  3. Makes its own light
  4. Is very hot
Show answerA) Reflects sunlight

Q5. A solar eclipse happens when

  1. It is a full moon
  2. Sun is between Earth and Moon
  3. Moon is between Sun and Earth
  4. Earth is between Sun and Moon
Show answerC) Moon is between Sun and Earth
