Class 7 Science Chapter 3: Electricity: Circuits and their Components

NCERT Curiosity · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

An electric cell provides electricity, and several cells form a battery. A bulb glows only when it is part of a complete, closed circuit. Switches open or close the circuit. Conductors like metals let current pass, while insulators like plastic and rubber do not, which keeps us safe.

Key concepts

🔋Electric Cell

An electric cell has a positive (+) and a negative (−) terminal and supplies electricity.

Example: A torch cell has a metal cap (+) and a flat base (−).

In real life: TV remotes use cells.

💡 Did you know? Two or more cells joined together make a battery.

💡Bulb

A bulb glows when current flows through its thin filament.

Example: If the filament breaks, the bulb is 'fused' and won't glow.

In real life: Street lights now use LEDs to save energy.

💡 Did you know? LEDs use much less electricity than filament bulbs.

🔁Closed Circuit

Current flows only in a complete path from + terminal through components back to − terminal.

Example: Cell → wire → bulb → wire → cell makes a closed circuit.

In real life: A broken wire stops a fan from working.

💡 Did you know? If any gap exists, it is an open circuit and the bulb won't glow.

🔘Switch

A switch opens or closes a circuit.

Example: Switch ON closes the circuit; OFF opens it.

In real life: Every room switch controls a circuit.

💡 Did you know? A simple switch can be made with a safety pin and drawing pins.

🧲Conductors and Insulators

Materials that let current pass are conductors; those that don't are insulators.

Example: Copper and aluminium conduct; rubber and plastic insulate.

In real life: Electricians wear rubber gloves.

💡 Did you know? Wires are covered in plastic to keep us safe.

Key terms

TermMeaning
Electric cellSource of electricity with + and − terminals
BatteryCombination of two or more cells
CircuitComplete path for current
ConductorMaterial that allows current to pass

Quick revision

  • Current flows only in a closed circuit.
  • Copper is a good conductor; rubber is an insulator.
  • A switch opens or closes a circuit.
