Class 7 Science Chapter 6: Adolescence: A Stage of Growth and Change

NCERT Curiosity · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Adolescence is the stage of growth between childhood and adulthood, roughly between 10 and 19 years. Hormones bring physical changes like growth spurts and voice changes, along with emotional changes. Everyone changes at their own pace. A balanced diet, exercise, hygiene, rest and safe choices help adolescents grow healthily.

Key concepts

🌱What is Adolescence?

Adolescence is the period of growth between childhood and adulthood, roughly from 10 to 19 years of age.

Example: Many changes in the body and mind happen during these years.

In real life: Classmates of the same age may look very different in height.

💡 Did you know? These changes happen at different times for different people, and that is normal.

📈Physical Changes

The body grows quickly in height and strength. Voice may change and new hair growth appears.

Example: A sudden increase in height is called a growth spurt.

In real life: Clothes and shoes may need changing more often.

💡 Did you know? Hormones, chemical messengers in the body, cause these changes.

💭Emotional Changes

Adolescents may feel new emotions, want more independence and care about how they look.

Example: Feeling unsure or moody sometimes is common.

In real life: Sharing worries with a friend or family member can make them lighter.

💡 Did you know? Talking to parents, teachers or trusted adults helps.

🥗Healthy Habits

A balanced diet, exercise, enough sleep and personal hygiene support healthy growth.

Example: Milk, pulses, green vegetables and fruits help growing bodies.

In real life: Playing outdoors keeps body and mind healthy.

💡 Did you know? Iron-rich foods help prevent anaemia.

🚫Making Safe Choices

Saying no to tobacco, alcohol and drugs protects health and future.

Example: Peer pressure should not decide our choices.

In real life: Schools run awareness programmes on healthy choices.

💡 Did you know? It is okay to ask trusted adults for help and information.

  • Adolescence is about 10 to 19 years.
  • Hormones cause body changes.

Practice questions

Q1. Adolescence is roughly the age range of

  1. 10 to 19 years
  2. 2 to 6 years
  3. 60 to 70 years
  4. 25 to 40 years
Show answerA) 10 to 19 years

Q2. The chemical messengers that cause body changes are

  1. Enzymes
  2. Minerals
  3. Hormones
  4. Vitamins
Show answerC) Hormones

Q3. A sudden increase in height is called

  1. Balanced diet
  2. Growth spurt
  3. Hygiene
  4. Puberty
Show answerB) Growth spurt

Q4. Body changes during adolescence happen

  1. At the same time for everyone
  2. Only in boys
  3. Only in girls
  4. At different times for different people
Show answerD) At different times for different people

Q5. Which helps healthy growth in adolescence?

  1. Sleeping very little
  2. Balanced diet and exercise
  3. Junk food daily
  4. Skipping meals
Show answerB) Balanced diet and exercise
