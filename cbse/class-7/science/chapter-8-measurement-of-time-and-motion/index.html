Class 7 Science Chapter 8: Measurement of Time and Motion

NCERT Curiosity · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

People have measured time with sundials, water clocks and pendulums; a pendulum of fixed length has a nearly constant time period. Speed is distance divided by time, with the SI unit metre per second. Motion is uniform when equal distances are covered in equal times, and non-uniform otherwise.

Key concepts

⏳Measuring Time

People measured time using the Sun, sundials, water clocks and sand clocks before modern clocks.

Example: A sundial shows time from the shadow of a stick.

In real life: Mechanical and digital clocks now measure time very accurately.

💡 Did you know? In ancient India, a ghati was a unit of about 24 minutes.

🕰️Simple Pendulum

A simple pendulum is a small weight (bob) hung from a thread. The time for one complete swing is its time period.

Example: For a pendulum of a fixed length, the time period stays nearly the same.

In real life: Old wall clocks used pendulums to keep time.

💡 Did you know? A longer pendulum has a longer time period.

🏃Speed

Speed is the distance covered in unit time: speed = distance ÷ time.

Example: A car covering 120 km in 2 hours has a speed of 60 km/h.

In real life: Speedometers show a vehicle's speed.

💡 Did you know? The SI unit of speed is metre per second (m/s).

📏Uniform and Non-uniform Motion

In uniform motion, equal distances are covered in equal times; otherwise the motion is non-uniform.

Example: A train at constant speed on a straight track shows uniform motion.

In real life: A bus in city traffic moves non-uniformly.

💡 Did you know? Most motion around us is non-uniform.

🔢Units of Time

The SI unit of time is the second. 60 seconds = 1 minute; 60 minutes = 1 hour.

Example: 1 hour = 3600 seconds.

In real life: Races are timed in seconds and fractions of a second.

💡 Did you know? A day has 24 hours, the time Earth takes to rotate once.

Key terms

Time periodTime for one complete oscillation
SpeedDistance covered per unit time

  • SI unit of speed is m/s.
