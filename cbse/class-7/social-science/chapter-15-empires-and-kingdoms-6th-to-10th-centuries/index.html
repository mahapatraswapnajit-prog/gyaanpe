Class 7 Social Science Chapter 15: Empires and Kingdoms: 6th to 10th Centuries

NCERT Exploring Society: India and Beyond · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

From the 6th to 10th centuries, many kingdoms shaped India: Harsha in the north, the Chalukyas of Badami in the Deccan, the Pallavas of Kanchipuram in the south, and later the Palas, Pratiharas and Rashtrakutas who fought over Kannauj. It was a great age of temples, learning and regional languages.

Key concepts

👑Harsha

Harshavardhana ruled north India in the 7th century from Kannauj.

Example: The Chinese traveller Xuanzang visited his court.

In real life: Xuanzang also studied at Nalanda.

💡 Did you know? Banabhatta wrote the Harshacharita about him.

⛰️Chalukyas

The Chalukyas of Badami ruled the Deccan. Pulakeshin II stopped Harsha from moving south.

Example: Badami, Aihole and Pattadakal have fine temples.

In real life: Tourists visit these temples in Karnataka.

💡 Did you know? Pattadakal is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

🏖️Pallavas

The Pallavas ruled from Kanchipuram in the south.

Example: The Shore Temple and rock-cut rathas are at Mamallapuram.

In real life: Mamallapuram monuments are UNESCO heritage.

💡 Did you know? Mamallapuram was also a busy port.

⚔️The Tripartite Struggle

The Palas, Pratiharas and Rashtrakutas fought for control of Kannauj.

Example: Palas ruled Bengal and supported Nalanda and Vikramashila.

In real life: Ellora caves are UNESCO heritage.

💡 Did you know? The Rashtrakutas built the Kailasa temple at Ellora from a single rock.

🛕Temples and Culture

This period saw great temple building, literature and the growth of regional languages.

Example: Bhakti saints composed devotional songs in Tamil.

In real life: Temples were also centres of learning and art.

💡 Did you know? Odisha's Parashurameshvara temple at Bhubaneswar dates from this time.
