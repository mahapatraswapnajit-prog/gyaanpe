Class 7 Social Science Chapter 16: Turning Tides: 11th and 12th Centuries

NCERT Exploring Society: India and Beyond · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

In the 11th and 12th centuries, the Cholas under Rajaraja I and Rajendra I built a strong empire with grand temples and village assemblies. In the north, Mahmud of Ghazni raided many times, and Rajput rulers like Prithviraj Chauhan faced Muhammad Ghori at Tarain, leading to new rulers in Delhi.

Key concepts

⚓The Cholas at Their Height

The Chola kings Rajaraja I and Rajendra I built a powerful empire in south India.

Example: Rajaraja I built the Brihadeeswara Temple at Thanjavur.

In real life: The Brihadeeswara Temple is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

💡 Did you know? Rajendra I led a naval expedition to Southeast Asia.

🏘️Chola Villages

Chola villages had local assemblies that managed tanks, temples and land.

Example: Inscriptions at Uthiramerur describe village assembly elections.

In real life: Local self-government in India has old roots.

💡 Did you know? Members were chosen by drawing lots (kudavolai) in some places.

🐎Raids from the North-west

Mahmud of Ghazni raided north India many times in the early 11th century.

Example: He attacked the Somnath temple in 1025.

In real life: Somnath temple was rebuilt and stands today.

💡 Did you know? Al-Biruni, who came with Mahmud, wrote a detailed book about India.

🛡️Rajput Kingdoms

Rajput kingdoms like the Chauhans ruled parts of north India.

Example: Prithviraj Chauhan ruled from Ajmer and Delhi.

In real life: Many forts in Rajasthan date from these times.

💡 Did you know? He defeated Muhammad Ghori in the First Battle of Tarain (1191).

🔁Changing Times

In the Second Battle of Tarain (1192), Ghori defeated Prithviraj, opening the way for new rulers in Delhi.

Example: This led to the start of the Delhi Sultanate in 1206.

In real life: Kingdoms rose and fell, changing India's map.

💡 Did you know? Odisha's Lingaraja Temple was completed in this era.

  • Somnath was raided in 1025.
