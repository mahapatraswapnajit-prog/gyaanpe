Class 7 Social Science Chapter 17: India, a Home to Many

NCERT Exploring Society: India and Beyond · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

India is home to many languages, faiths, foods, dresses and traditions, shaped over centuries by migration and exchange. Twenty-two languages, including Odia, are listed in the Eighth Schedule. Shared values, the Constitution and national symbols hold this diversity together as unity in diversity.

Key concepts

🗣️Many Languages

India has hundreds of languages and dialects. 22 languages are listed in the Eighth Schedule of the Constitution.

Example: Odia, Hindi, Bengali, Tamil and Telugu are among them.

In real life: Signboards in many states show more than one language.

💡 Did you know? Odia was declared a classical language in 2014.

🕌Many Faiths

People of many religions live in India, including Hindus, Muslims, Christians, Sikhs, Buddhists, Jains and Parsis.

Example: Festivals like Diwali, Eid, Christmas and Gurpurab are celebrated widely.

In real life: Rath Yatra in Puri attracts people from all over.

💡 Did you know? Many people join in the festivals of their neighbours.

🍛Food and Dress

Food and clothing vary by region, climate and tradition.

Example: Pakhala in Odisha, dosa in the south, and roti in the north.

In real life: Handlooms like Sambalpuri sarees are famous.

💡 Did you know? Spices from India have travelled the world.

🚶Migration

People have moved across India for work, trade and safety, adding to its diversity.

Example: Many workers from Odisha work in other states.

In real life: Cities like Mumbai have people from every state.

💡 Did you know? Migration has shaped Indian food, music and languages.

🇮🇳Unity in Diversity

Despite differences, Indians share common values, a Constitution and national symbols.

Example: The national anthem and flag unite all Indians.

In real life: Sports teams bring people from all regions together.

💡 Did you know? Respecting others' customs keeps diversity strong.

Key terms

TermMeaning
DiversityVariety of people, languages and cultures
Eighth ScheduleList of 22 official languages
MigrationMoving from one place to another
Classical languageLanguage with ancient literary tradition
Unity in diversityTogetherness despite differences

Quick revision

  • 22 languages are in the Eighth Schedule.
  • Odia became a classical language in 2014.
  • Diversity is India's strength.
