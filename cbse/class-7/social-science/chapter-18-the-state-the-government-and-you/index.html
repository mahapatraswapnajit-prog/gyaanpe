Class 7 Social Science Chapter 18: The State, the Government, and You

NCERT Exploring Society: India and Beyond · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

The government has three organs: the legislature makes laws, the executive carries them out and the judiciary interprets them. It works at three levels: Union, state and local, with panchayats and municipalities closest to people. Citizens shape government by voting, paying taxes, following laws and taking part in public life.

Key concepts

🏛️Three Organs

The government has three organs: the legislature makes laws, the executive implements them, and the judiciary interprets them and settles disputes.

Example: Parliament is the legislature at the Union level.

In real life: Courts can stop unfair actions of the government.

💡 Did you know? Separation of powers prevents misuse of power.

🗺️Three Levels

India has governments at three levels: Union (central), state, and local.

Example: Odisha's state government is in Bhubaneswar.

In real life: Your ward member is part of local government.

💡 Did you know? Local government includes panchayats and municipalities.

🏘️Local Government

Panchayats run villages; municipalities and corporations run towns and cities.

Example: The Gram Sabha includes all adult voters of a village.

In real life: Report a broken streetlight to the municipality.

💡 Did you know? Local bodies look after water, roads, cleanliness and streetlights.

🗳️You and Elections

Citizens elect representatives at all three levels.

Example: MPs are elected to Lok Sabha, MLAs to the state assembly.

In real life: Every vote counts in deciding leaders.

💡 Did you know? The Election Commission conducts elections.

🤝Citizens' Role

Citizens follow laws, pay taxes, vote and take part in public life.

Example: Paying taxes funds schools, roads and hospitals.

In real life: Joining clean-up drives is active citizenship.

💡 Did you know? Citizens can ask questions using the Right to Information.
