Class 7 Social Science Chapter 3: Climates of India

NCERT Exploring Society: India and Beyond · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

India has a monsoon climate, with the southwest monsoon bringing most rain between June and September. The four seasons are winter, summer, the southwest monsoon and the retreating monsoon. Latitude, altitude and distance from the sea cause regional differences, from very wet Meghalaya to dry Rajasthan. Climate change is altering these patterns.

Key concepts

🌧️The Monsoon

India's climate is called monsoon type. The southwest monsoon brings most of India's rain from about June to September.

Example: The monsoon first reaches Kerala, usually around 1 June.

In real life: Kharif crops like rice are sown with the monsoon rains.

💡 Did you know? The word 'monsoon' comes from the Arabic word 'mausim', meaning season.

📅Seasons of India

India has four main seasons: winter, summer, the southwest monsoon and the retreating monsoon.

Example: Winter is roughly December to February; summer is March to May.

In real life: School holidays often follow seasons.

💡 Did you know? Tamil Nadu gets much of its rain from the retreating (northeast) monsoon in October-November.

🌍Factors Affecting Climate

Latitude, altitude, distance from the sea and winds shape a region's climate.

Example: Places near the sea, like Mumbai, have moderate temperatures.

In real life: Delhi has hot summers and cold winters because it is far from the sea.

💡 Did you know? Places high up, like Shimla, are cooler.

☔Wettest and Driest

Mawsynram and Cherrapunji in Meghalaya get some of the highest rainfall in the world, while western Rajasthan gets very little.

Example: Hills facing rain-bearing winds get more rain.

In real life: People in dry regions store rainwater in tankas and stepwells.

💡 Did you know? Rainfall varies a lot from place to place in India.

🌡️Climate Change

Climate change means long-term changes in temperature and weather patterns, largely due to human activities.

Example: More intense heatwaves and heavy rains are being seen.

In real life: Planting trees and saving energy help reduce it.

💡 Did you know? Burning coal and petrol releases greenhouse gases.

  • Mawsynram is among the wettest places on Earth.
  • Places near the sea have moderate climate.
