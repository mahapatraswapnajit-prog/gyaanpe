Class 7 Social Science Chapter 8: How the Land Becomes Sacred

NCERT Exploring Society: India and Beyond · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

In India, rivers, mountains, forests and temple towns are seen as sacred, and pilgrimages connect people across regions. Networks like the Char Dham (including Puri in the east), the 12 jyotirlingas and the Shakti peethas link the land together. Community-protected sacred groves conserve nature, and sites sacred to many faiths reflect India's shared heritage.

Key concepts

🛕Sacred Geography

Many places in India are considered sacred: rivers, mountains, forests and temple towns.

Example: The Ganga is revered as a sacred river.

In real life: Families travel together on tirtha yatras.

💡 Did you know? Pilgrimages connect people from distant regions.

🧭Char Dham

The four dhams in the four directions are Badrinath (north), Dwarka (west), Puri (east) and Rameswaram (south).

Example: Puri's Jagannath Temple is the eastern dham.

In real life: The Rath Yatra at Puri draws lakhs of devotees.

💡 Did you know? Visiting all four dhams links the whole of India.

🔱Networks of Sacred Sites

The 12 jyotirlingas and the Shakti peethas are spread across India.

Example: Somnath in Gujarat and Kashi Vishwanath in Varanasi are jyotirlingas.

In real life: Pilgrims carry stories and customs across regions.

💡 Did you know? Such networks help people imagine India as one land.

🌳Sacred Groves

Communities protect patches of forest as sacred groves, where trees are not cut.

Example: They are called devrai in Maharashtra, kavu in Kerala and sarna in Jharkhand.

In real life: They are early examples of community conservation.

💡 Did you know? Sacred groves conserve plants and animals.

🕌Many Faiths, Sacred Places

Places sacred to different faiths are found across India.

Example: Bodh Gaya (Buddhism), the Golden Temple in Amritsar (Sikhism) and Ajmer Sharif (Sufi tradition).

In real life: Respecting all sacred places is part of our heritage.

💡 Did you know? Kumbh Mela is among the largest gatherings of people on Earth.
