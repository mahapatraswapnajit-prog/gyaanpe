Class 8 English Chapter 1: The Wit that Won Hearts

NCERT Poorvi · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Set in the Vijayanagara court of Krishnadeva Raya, this story celebrates Tenali Ramakrishna's quick wit. It shows how clever, good-humoured thinking can settle quarrels and win hearts, and introduces idioms and humour in writing.

Key concepts

😄Wit at the Royal Court

This story is set in the court of King Krishnadeva Raya of Vijayanagara, where Tenali Ramakrishna was famous for his clever, funny solutions.

Example: A sharp, kind joke can settle a quarrel without anyone feeling insulted.

In real life: Tenali stories are popular in comics and cartoons.

💡 Did you know? Tenali Ramakrishna was also a Telugu poet, one of the 'Ashtadiggajas' (eight great poets) of the court.

🧠What is Wit?

Wit is quick, clever thinking expressed in words, often with humour.

Example: Answering a tricky question with a smart twist that makes everyone smile.

In real life: Witty replies can calm tense situations at home or school.

💡 Did you know? Wit is different from rudeness: good wit wins hearts, it does not hurt them.

🗣️Idioms

An idiom is a group of words whose meaning is different from the meanings of the separate words.

Example: 'Break the ice' = start a friendly conversation; 'a piece of cake' = very easy.

In real life: 'Bite off more than you can chew' warns against taking on too much.

💡 Did you know? Idioms make writing lively but should suit the situation.

🎭Humour in Stories

Humour comes from surprise, word play and situations that turn out differently from what we expect.

Example: A trickster outsmarts a boastful courtier.

In real life: Comedy shows also use surprise and timing.

💡 Did you know? Folk tales across India use humour to teach lessons.

💡Lesson of the Story

Intelligence used with kindness earns respect and friendship.

Example: Solving a problem cleverly without embarrassing others.

In real life: Leaders value people who think clearly and stay calm.

💡 Did you know? Many Tenali tales show the king valuing honest advice.

Key terms

TermMeaning
WitQuick, clever humour in words

Quick revision

  • Tenali Ramakrishna was in Krishnadeva Raya's court.
  • 'A piece of cake' means very easy.
  • Good wit wins hearts without hurting anyone.
