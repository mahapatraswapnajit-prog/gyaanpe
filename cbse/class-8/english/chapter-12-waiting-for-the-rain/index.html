Class 8 English Chapter 12: Waiting for the Rain

NCERT Poorvi · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

During a drought, Velu searches for answers and learns patience and hope from an old woman's wisdom. The story connects to India's dependence on the monsoon and saving water, with practice in homophones.

Key concepts

☀️A Village in Drought

During a long drought, Velu worries about his village and crops. He consults astrologers, but an old woman's wisdom teaches him patience and trust in nature.

Example: Dry fields, empty wells and anxious farmers.

In real life: Many Indian farmers still depend on monsoon rain.

💡 Did you know? A drought is a long period with much less rain than usual.

🌧️Hope and Patience

The story shows how hope and patience help people face hard times.

Example: Waiting calmly while doing what you can.

In real life: Communities share water during droughts.

💡 Did you know? Traditional knowledge often comes from careful observation of nature over generations.

🌦️The Monsoon

The monsoon brings most of India's rainfall between June and September.

Example: The monsoon usually reaches Kerala first, around 1 June.

In real life: Farmers plan sowing around the monsoon's arrival.

💡 Did you know? The India Meteorological Department forecasts the monsoon each year.

🔤Homophones

Homophones sound the same but have different meanings and spellings.

Example: rain / reign / rein; weather / whether; there / their.

In real life: Careful reading avoids such errors.

💡 Did you know? Spell-check cannot always catch homophone mistakes.

💧Saving Water

Rainwater harvesting, check dams and careful use help in dry times.

Example: Collecting rooftop rainwater in tanks.

In real life: Turning off taps saves litres every day.

💡 Did you know? Drip irrigation gives water directly to plant roots.

  • The monsoon usually reaches Kerala first.
  • A drought is a long spell of very little rain.
  • 'Rain' and 'reign' are homophones.
