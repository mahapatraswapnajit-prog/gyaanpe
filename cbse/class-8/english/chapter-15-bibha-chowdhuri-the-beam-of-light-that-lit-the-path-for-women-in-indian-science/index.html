Class 8 English Chapter 15: Bibha Chowdhuri: The Beam of Light that Lit the Path for Women in Indian Science

NCERT Poorvi · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

This chapter tells the story of Bibha Chowdhuri, a pioneering Indian physicist who studied cosmic-ray particles and in whose honour a star was named in 2019. It discusses women in Indian science, biography writing and abstract nouns.

Key concepts

🔬A Pioneering Physicist

Bibha Chowdhuri was an Indian physicist who studied cosmic rays and tiny particles at a time when very few women worked in science.

Example: At the Bose Institute in Kolkata she worked with D. M. Bose on detecting particles using photographic plates.

In real life: Her life inspires girls to choose science careers.

💡 Did you know? She later earned her PhD in Manchester, England.

⭐A Star Named Bibha

In 2019 the International Astronomical Union named a star 'Bibha' in her honour.

Example: The star (HD 86081) lies in the constellation Sextans.

In real life: Naming stars after scientists keeps their memory alive.

💡 Did you know? Her early work is linked to the discovery of particles later called pions.

👩‍🔬Women in Indian Science

Women scientists overcame many barriers to study and work.

Example: Anna Mani (weather instruments), Kamala Sohonie and Janaki Ammal are other pioneers.

In real life: Scholarships support girls in science today.

💡 Did you know? Today many women scientists work at ISRO, including on Chandrayaan missions.

📝Writing a Biography

A biography tells the story of a real person's life in the third person, in time order, with key events and achievements.

Example: 'She was born in Kolkata... She studied physics...'

In real life: Profiles of achievers appear in newspapers and textbooks.

💡 Did you know? An autobiography is written by the person themselves.

🔤Abstract Nouns

Abstract nouns name ideas, qualities or feelings that cannot be touched.

Example: courage, curiosity, determination, success.

In real life: Speeches about heroes are full of abstract nouns.

💡 Did you know? Many are formed with suffixes: brave → bravery, curious → curiosity.
