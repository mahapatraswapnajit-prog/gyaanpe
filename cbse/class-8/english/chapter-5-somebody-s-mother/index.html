Class 8 English Chapter 5: Somebody's Mother

NCERT Poorvi · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

In this poem a kind schoolboy helps an old woman cross a crowded, icy street that others hurry past, thinking she is 'somebody's mother'. It teaches empathy and kindness, along with rhyme, rhythm, imagery and adjectives.

Key concepts

👵The Poem

This poem shows an old woman waiting to cross a busy, icy street, ignored by passers-by, until a kind schoolboy helps her across.

Example: The boy thinks that she is 'somebody's mother' and deserves the care he would want for his own.

In real life: Helping elders cross a road is a simple act of kindness.

💡 Did you know? The poem is by the American poet Mary Dow Brine (19th century).

❤️Kindness and Empathy

Empathy is understanding and sharing another person's feelings.

Example: Imagining your own grandmother in the old woman's place.

In real life: Volunteering at old-age homes builds empathy.

💡 Did you know? Small acts of kindness can change someone's whole day.

🎵Rhyme and Rhythm

Rhyme is the repetition of similar sounds at line endings. A rhyme scheme is shown with letters like aabb.

Example: 'street/feet', 'snow/slow' are rhyming pairs.

In real life: Nursery rhymes use strong rhyme and rhythm.

💡 Did you know? Rhythm is the beat or flow created by stressed and unstressed syllables.

🖼️Imagery in the Poem

Images of cold, snow and hurrying crowds help readers feel the old woman's difficulty.

Example: Cold weather makes her loneliness feel stronger.

In real life: Films use similar contrasts to stir feelings.

💡 Did you know? Contrast (busy crowd vs one kind boy) makes the message powerful.

🔤Adjectives

Adjectives describe nouns: their size, colour, age, feeling and more.

Example: old woman, busy street, kind boy, icy road.

In real life: Good adjectives make descriptions vivid.

💡 Did you know? Several adjectives together usually follow an order: opinion, size, age, colour.

Practice questions

Q1. Who helps the old woman cross the street?

  1. A schoolboy
  2. Her son
  3. A policeman
  4. A shopkeeper
Show answerA) A schoolboy

Q2. The poem 'Somebody's Mother' is by

  1. Ruskin Bond
  2. Mary Dow Brine
  3. Rabindranath Tagore
  4. William Wordsworth
Show answerB) Mary Dow Brine

Q3. The main value in the poem is

  1. Pride
  2. Kindness and empathy
  3. Greed
  4. Anger
Show answerB) Kindness and empathy

Q4. Understanding and sharing someone's feelings is

  1. Pride
  2. Fear
  3. Empathy
  4. Envy
Show answerC) Empathy

Q5. Which pair rhymes?

  1. Street – road
  2. Snow – slow
  3. Snow – walk
  4. Boy – girl
Show answerB) Snow – slow
