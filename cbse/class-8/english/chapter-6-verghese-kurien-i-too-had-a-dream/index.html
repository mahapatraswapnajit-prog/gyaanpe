Class 8 English Chapter 6: Verghese Kurien – I Too Had a Dream

NCERT Poorvi · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

This chapter draws on the life of Dr Verghese Kurien, whose work with farmers' cooperatives at Anand led to Amul and India's White Revolution. It highlights service, integrity and teamwork, with practice in active and passive voice.

Key concepts

🥛The Milkman of India

Dr Verghese Kurien helped turn India from a milk-deficient country into the world's largest milk producer.

Example: He led the cooperative movement at Anand in Gujarat that grew into the Amul brand.

In real life: Amul products are found in shops across India.

💡 Did you know? He is called the 'Father of the White Revolution'.

🤝Cooperatives

A cooperative is owned and run by its members, who share the profits.

Example: Village dairy farmers sell milk to their own cooperative society.

In real life: Many sugar and handloom cooperatives work the same way.

💡 Did you know? Operation Flood, led by Kurien's NDDB, connected village milk producers to cities.

🎂National Milk Day

India celebrates National Milk Day on 26 November, Kurien's birthday.

Example: 'I Too Had a Dream' is the title of his autobiography.

In real life: Odisha's OMFED is a dairy cooperative brand.

💡 Did you know? He chose to serve farmers in India instead of taking jobs abroad.

🔁Active and Passive Voice

In active voice the doer comes first; in passive voice the action or receiver comes first.

Example: Active: 'Farmers sell milk.' Passive: 'Milk is sold by farmers.'

In real life: News reports often use passive voice.

💡 Did you know? Passive voice is common when the doer is unknown or less important.

🌟Values

Integrity, service and belief in ordinary people's strength are central to Kurien's story.

Example: Farmers became owners, not just suppliers.

In real life: Students can start small cooperative clubs in school.

💡 Did you know? Dreams become real through hard work and teamwork.
