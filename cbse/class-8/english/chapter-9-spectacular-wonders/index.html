Class 8 English Chapter 9: Spectacular Wonders

NCERT Poorvi · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

This chapter takes readers on a tour of India's natural wonders, including the Valley of Flowers, the Sundarbans, Lonar Crater Lake and Odisha's Chandipur Beach. It builds descriptive vocabulary and practises degrees of comparison.

Key concepts

🏞️Natural Wonders of India

This chapter describes seven amazing natural sites of India, from the Valley of Flowers to Chandipur Beach.

Example: Each place shows a different side of India's natural beauty.

In real life: Eco-tourism helps local people earn while protecting nature.

💡 Did you know? India has mountains, deserts, mangroves, lakes and coasts within one country.

🌸Valley of Flowers and Sundarbans

The Valley of Flowers in Uttarakhand blooms with hundreds of flower species in the monsoon. The Sundarbans in West Bengal is the largest mangrove forest in the world and home to the Royal Bengal tiger.

Example: Mangrove roots stick out of the mud to breathe.

In real life: Mangroves protect coasts from cyclones.

💡 Did you know? Both are UNESCO World Heritage Sites.

☄️Lonar Crater Lake

Lonar Lake in Maharashtra formed in a crater made by a meteorite impact thousands of years ago.

Example: Its water is both salty and alkaline.

In real life: Scientists study it to learn about craters on other planets.

💡 Did you know? It is one of the few such impact craters in basalt rock on Earth.

🌊Chandipur Beach, Odisha

At Chandipur, the sea goes far out during low tide and comes back at high tide, so you can walk on the seabed.

Example: This is why it is called the 'hide and seek' beach.

In real life: DRDO's Integrated Test Range for missile trials is located at Chandipur.

💡 Did you know? Tides are caused mainly by the Moon's pull.

📏Degrees of Comparison

Adjectives have three degrees: positive, comparative and superlative.

Example: big – bigger – biggest; beautiful – more beautiful – most beautiful.

In real life: Travel guides use superlatives like 'largest' and 'highest'.

💡 Did you know? Use 'than' with comparatives: 'taller than'.

Key terms

TermMeaning
MangroveTree that grows in salty coastal swamps
CraterBowl-shaped hollow, often from an impact

SuperlativeHighest degree, like 'biggest'
Eco-tourismNature travel that protects the environment

Quick revision

  • The Sundarbans is the largest mangrove forest in the world.
  • Lonar Lake formed from a meteorite impact.
  • At Chandipur the sea recedes far at low tide.

Practice questions

Q1. The Sundarbans is famous for its

  1. Coral islands
  2. Mangrove forests
  3. Deserts
  4. Snow peaks
Show answerB) Mangrove forests

Q2. Lonar Lake was formed by

  1. A dam
  2. A glacier
  3. A meteorite impact
  4. An earthquake
Show answerC) A meteorite impact

Q3. Chandipur Beach is in

  1. Goa
  2. Gujarat
  3. Kerala
  4. Odisha
Show answerD) Odisha

Q4. At Chandipur, during low tide the sea

  1. Goes far out
  2. Turns red
  3. Freezes
  4. Rises very high
Show answerA) Goes far out

Q5. The Valley of Flowers is in

  1. Tamil Nadu
  2. Uttarakhand
  3. Odisha
  4. Rajasthan
Show answerB) Uttarakhand
