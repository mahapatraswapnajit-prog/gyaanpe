Class 8 Maths Chapter 10: Proportional Reasoning–2

NCERT Ganita Prakash · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

The chapter extends proportional reasoning to map scales, ratios with three terms, dividing a whole in a ratio, drawing pie charts, and inverse proportion, where one quantity rises as the other falls.

Key concepts

🗺️Ratios in Maps

A map scale like 1 cm : 5 km means 1 cm on the map is 5 km on the ground.

Example: 4.5 cm on the map = 4.5 × 5 = 22.5 km.

In real life: Map apps show a scale bar in a corner.

💡 Did you know? A scale can also be written without units, like 1 : 500000.

🔺Ratios with Three Terms

A ratio can compare more than two quantities, like a : b : c.

Example: Concrete mix cement : sand : gravel = 1 : 2 : 4.

In real life: Builders and cooks use three-term ratios.

💡 Did you know? Each term can be scaled by the same number.

🍕Dividing in a Ratio

Add the terms to get the number of equal parts, then share.

Example: ₹1200 in 1 : 2 : 3 → 6 parts of ₹200 → ₹200, ₹400, ₹600.

In real life: Prize money is often split this way.

💡 Did you know? Check: the shares should add back to the total.

🥧Pie Charts

Each sector's angle = (part ÷ whole) × 360°.

Example: 25% of the whole is a 90° sector.

In real life: Budgets and survey results are shown as pie charts.

💡 Did you know? All the angles of a pie chart add up to 360°.

🔁Inverse Proportion

When one quantity goes up and the other goes down so that their product stays the same, they are in inverse proportion.

Example: 6 workers take 10 days → 12 workers take 5 days (6 × 10 = 12 × 5).

In real life: More taps fill a tank faster.

💡 Did you know? For a fixed distance, speed and time are inversely proportional.

Key terms

Quick revision

  • The whole pie chart is 360°.
  • In inverse proportion, x × y stays the same.
  • Real distance = map distance × scale.

Practice questions

Q1. A map scale is 1 cm : 10 km. 3.5 cm on the map is

  1. 13.5 km
  2. 3.5 km
  3. 35 km
  4. 350 km
Show answerC) 35 km

Q2. ₹900 is divided in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4. The largest share is

  1. ₹200
  2. ₹450
  3. ₹400
  4. ₹300
Show answerC) ₹400

Q3. In a pie chart, a sector showing 50% has angle

  1. 90°
  2. 360°
  3. 50°
  4. 180°
Show answerD) 180°

Q4. 8 workers finish a job in 15 days. 12 workers will take

  1. 10 days
  2. 20 days
  3. 22.5 days
  4. 12 days
Show answerA) 10 days

Q5. At 60 km/h a trip takes 4 hours. At 80 km/h it takes

  1. 3 hours
  2. 2 hours
  3. 4 hours
  4. 5 hours
Show answerA) 3 hours
