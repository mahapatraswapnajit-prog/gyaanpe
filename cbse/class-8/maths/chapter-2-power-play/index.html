Class 8 Maths Chapter 2: Power Play

NCERT Ganita Prakash · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Powers let us write repeated multiplication compactly. The chapter covers the laws of exponents, negative powers, writing very large and very small numbers with powers of 10, and how exponential growth races ahead of linear growth.

Key concepts

✖️Exponents

A power is a short way to write repeated multiplication. In 2⁵, 2 is the base and 5 is the exponent.

Example: 2⁵ = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 32.

In real life: Computer memory sizes like 64, 128 and 256 are powers of 2.

💡 Did you know? Any non-zero number raised to the power 0 equals 1.

📏Laws of Exponents

When bases are the same: aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ, aᵐ ÷ aⁿ = aᵐ⁻ⁿ and (aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐⁿ.

Example: 3² × 3⁴ = 3⁶ = 729.

In real life: These rules make long calculations quick.

💡 Did you know? (a × b)ⁿ = aⁿ × bⁿ, for example (2 × 5)³ = 2³ × 5³ = 1000.

🔻The Other Side of Powers

A negative exponent means a reciprocal: a⁻ⁿ = 1 ÷ aⁿ.

Example: 10⁻² = 1 ÷ 100 = 0.01.

In real life: Very small lengths, like the size of cells, are written with negative powers of 10.

💡 Did you know? 2⁻¹ = ½ and 10⁻³ = 0.001.

🔟Powers of 10 and Standard Form

Big and small numbers are written as a number between 1 and 10 times a power of 10.

Example: 59,00,000 = 5.9 × 10⁶.

In real life: The Sun is about 1.5 × 10⁸ km away from the Earth.

💡 Did you know? 1 lakh = 10⁵ and 1 crore = 10⁷.

📈Linear vs Exponential Growth

Linear growth adds the same amount each step. Exponential growth multiplies by the same amount each step, so it becomes huge very fast.

Example: A sheet folded 10 times becomes 2¹⁰ = 1024 times as thick.

In real life: A message forwarded to 2 new people each time spreads exponentially.

💡 Did you know? In the chessboard story, the last square alone would need 2⁶³ grains of rice.

Key terms

TermMeaning
BaseThe number being multiplied repeatedly
ExponentHow many times the base is used

Quick revision

  • a⁰ = 1 for any non-zero a.
  • aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ.
  • 2¹⁰ = 1024, a little more than a thousand.
