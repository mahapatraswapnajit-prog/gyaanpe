Class 8 Maths Chapter 4: Quadrilaterals

NCERT Ganita Prakash · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

The chapter studies four-sided shapes and their properties: angle sum 360°, parallelograms, rectangles, squares, rhombuses, kites and trapeziums, and what their sides, angles and diagonals do.

Key concepts

🔷What is a Quadrilateral?

A quadrilateral has 4 sides, 4 angles, 4 vertices and 2 diagonals. Its angles always add up to 360°.

Example: If three angles are 90°, 80° and 100°, the fourth is 360° − 270° = 90°.

In real life: Doors, books and kites are quadrilaterals.

💡 Did you know? A diagonal splits it into 2 triangles, and 2 × 180° = 360°.

▱Parallelogram

Both pairs of opposite sides are parallel and equal. Opposite angles are equal, adjacent angles add to 180°, and the diagonals bisect each other.

Example: If one angle is 70°, the angles are 70°, 110°, 70°, 110°.

In real life: Folding gates open into parallelogram shapes.

💡 Did you know? Pushing a rectangle sideways turns it into a parallelogram.

⬛Rectangle and Square

A rectangle is a parallelogram with all angles 90°; its diagonals are equal. A square is a rectangle with all sides equal; its diagonals are equal and meet at 90°.

Example: A 3 cm × 4 cm rectangle has diagonals of 5 cm each.

In real life: Phone screens and tiles are rectangles and squares.

💡 Did you know? Every square is a rectangle, but not every rectangle is a square.

◆Rhombus

A rhombus has all four sides equal. Its diagonals bisect each other at right angles.

Example: A rhombus with side 5 cm has a perimeter of 20 cm.

In real life: The diamond shape on playing cards is a rhombus.

💡 Did you know? A square is a special rhombus with all angles 90°.

🪁Kite and Trapezium

A kite has two pairs of equal sides that are next to each other; its diagonals meet at 90°. A trapezium has a pair of parallel sides.

Example: A paper kite and the side view of a bucket show these shapes.

In real life: Kites in the sky are named after this shape.

💡 Did you know? In a kite, one diagonal cuts the other into two equal halves.

Key terms

TermMeaning
DiagonalLine joining opposite vertices
ParallelogramBoth pairs of opposite sides parallel
RhombusAll four sides equal
KiteTwo pairs of equal adjacent sides
TrapeziumHas a pair of parallel sides

Quick revision

  • The angles of a quadrilateral add up to 360°.
  • The diagonals of a parallelogram bisect each other.
  • The diagonals of a rhombus meet at 90°.

Practice questions

Q1. The angles of a quadrilateral add up to

  1. 540°
  2. 180°
  3. 360°
  4. 90°
Show answerC) 360°

Q2. Three angles of a quadrilateral are 80°, 95° and 105°. The fourth angle is

  1. 80°
  2. 100°
  3. 75°
  4. 90°
Show answerA) 80°

Q3. One angle of a parallelogram is 65°. The angle next to it is

  1. 65°
  2. 25°
  3. 115°
  4. 135°
Show answerC) 115°

Q4. A quadrilateral with all four sides equal is a

  1. Kite
  2. Trapezium
  3. Rhombus
  4. Rectangle
Show answerC) Rhombus

Q5. In which shape are the diagonals always equal?

  1. Rectangle
  2. Parallelogram that is not a rectangle
  3. Rhombus that is not a square
  4. Kite
Show answerA) Rectangle
