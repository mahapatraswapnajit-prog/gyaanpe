Class 8 Maths Chapter 5: Number Play

NCERT Ganita Prakash · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

The chapter plays with multiples, sums of consecutive numbers, even and odd sums, quick divisibility tests, and letter-digit puzzles. Place value and simple algebra explain why each trick works.

Key concepts

✖️Multiples and Patterns

Multiples of a number come from multiplying it by 1, 2, 3… Common multiples are shared by two numbers.

Example: Common multiples of 4 and 6 are 12, 24, 36…; the smallest is 12.

In real life: If one bus comes every 4 minutes and another every 6, they meet every 12 minutes.

💡 Did you know? Every number is a multiple of 1 and of itself.

➕Sums of Consecutive Numbers

1 + 2 + 3 + … + n = n × (n + 1) ÷ 2.

Example: 1 + 2 + … + 10 = 10 × 11 ÷ 2 = 55.

In real life: Young Gauss is said to have added 1 to 100 in seconds: 5050.

💡 Did you know? Powers of 2 (like 8, 16, 32) cannot be written as a sum of two or more consecutive positive numbers.

⚖️Even and Odd Sums

even + even = even, odd + odd = even, even + odd = odd. A sum of an odd count of odd numbers is odd.

Example: 3 + 5 + 7 = 15, which is odd.

In real life: Parity puzzles help check answers quickly.

💡 Did you know? So five odd numbers can never add up to 50.

✅Divisibility Shortcuts

By 3 or 9: digit sum divisible by 3 or 9. By 4: last two digits. By 8: last three digits. By 11: alternate digit sum difference is 0 or a multiple of 11.

Example: 7,128: digit sum 18, so divisible by 9; last two digits 28, so divisible by 4.

In real life: Shopkeepers use these tricks to split bills evenly.

💡 Did you know? A number divisible by both 2 and 3 is divisible by 6.

🕵️Digits in Disguise

In letter puzzles, each letter stands for a digit. Writing numbers using place value helps crack them.

Example: AB + BA = 121 means 11 × (A + B) = 121, so A + B = 11.

In real life: Such puzzles are called cryptarithms.

💡 Did you know? AB + BA is always a multiple of 11.

Quick revision

  • 1 + 2 + … + n = n(n + 1) ÷ 2.
  • A number is divisible by 9 if its digit sum is divisible by 9.
  • odd + odd = even.
