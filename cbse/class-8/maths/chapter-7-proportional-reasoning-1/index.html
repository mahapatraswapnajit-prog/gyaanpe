Class 8 Maths Chapter 7: Proportional Reasoning–1

NCERT Ganita Prakash · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

The chapter builds proportional thinking: ratios in simplest form, checking proportions, sharing in a ratio, converting units, and comparing quantities using fractions and percentages.

Key concepts

📐Ratios

A ratio compares two quantities by division. Divide both terms by their HCF to get the simplest form.

Example: 12 : 18 = 2 : 3.

In real life: Recipes use ratios, like 2 cups rice to 3 cups water.

💡 Did you know? Both quantities must be in the same unit before comparing.

⚖️Proportion

Two ratios are in proportion when they are equal: a : b = c : d exactly when a × d = b × c.

Example: 3 : 4 and 15 : 20 are in proportion since 3 × 20 = 4 × 15.

In real life: Enlarging a photo without stretching keeps it in proportion.

💡 Did you know? When a photo is resized correctly, length and width change in the same ratio.

🍰Sharing in a Ratio

To share in a : b, split the total into a + b equal parts.

Example: ₹600 in 1 : 2 → ₹200 and ₹400.

In real life: Business partners share profit in the ratio of money invested.

💡 Did you know? The parts of a ratio always add back to the whole.

📏Unit Conversions

Convert to the same unit before comparing or calculating.

Example: 3.5 km = 3500 m; 2 hours = 120 minutes.

In real life: Recipes, maps and travel all need unit conversions.

💡 Did you know? 1 inch is about 2.54 cm.

💯Comparing Proportions

Convert to fractions or percentages with the same base to compare.

Example: 18 out of 24 = 75% and 21 out of 30 = 70%, so the first is better.

In real life: Comparing marks out of different totals uses percentages.

💡 Did you know? 3/4 = 75% because 3 ÷ 4 = 0.75.

Key terms

Quick revision

  • a : b = c : d means a × d = b × c.
  • Compare ratios after converting to the same base.
  • 1 km = 1000 m.

  1. 3 : 4
  2. 9 : 16
  3. 6 : 8
  4. 2 : 3
Show answerA) 3 : 4

Q2. Are 4 : 6 and 10 : 15 in proportion?

  1. Only if doubled
  2. Cannot say
  3. Yes
  4. No
Show answerC) Yes

  1. ₹700
  2. ₹450
  3. ₹600

  2. ₹120
  3. ₹96
  4. ₹100
