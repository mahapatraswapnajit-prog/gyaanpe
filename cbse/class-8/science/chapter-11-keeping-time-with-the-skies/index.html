Class 8 Science Chapter 11: Keeping Time with the Skies

NCERT Curiosity · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

The Moon's phases and the Sun's yearly path helped people create calendars, and many festivals follow these sky events. The chapter also explains how artificial satellites help us communicate, forecast weather and navigate.

Key concepts

🌙Phases of the Moon

The Moon shines by reflecting sunlight. As it orbits the Earth, we see different portions of its lit half, giving its phases.

Example: New moon (Amavasya) → waxing → full moon (Purnima) → waning.

In real life: Fishermen track tides, which follow the Moon.

💡 Did you know? One full cycle of phases takes about 29.5 days.

📆Calendars

Solar calendars follow the Sun (a year of about 365¼ days); lunar calendars follow the Moon (12 lunar months ≈ 354 days).

Example: A leap day is added every 4 years to keep the solar calendar in step.

In real life: Our school year follows the solar (Gregorian) calendar.

💡 Did you know? Many Indian calendars are lunisolar and add an extra month (adhik maas) now and then.

🪔Festivals and the Sky

Many festivals are set by the Moon or the Sun's position.

Example: Diwali falls on the Amavasya of Kartika; Holi on the Purnima of Phalguna.

In real life: Eid dates depend on sighting the new crescent Moon.

💡 Did you know? Makar Sankranti marks the Sun moving into Makara (Capricorn), so its date stays near 14 January.

🛰️Artificial Satellites

Satellites orbit the Earth to help with communication, weather forecasting, navigation and mapping.

Example: India's NavIC system provides navigation over India.

In real life: TV, mobile maps and cyclone tracking all use satellites.

💡 Did you know? ISRO launches Indian satellites from Sriharikota.

🚀India in Space

Space missions help us study the Moon, planets and our own Earth.

Example: Chandrayaan-3 landed near the Moon's south pole on 23 August 2023.

In real life: Satellite images help farmers and disaster teams.

💡 Did you know? 23 August is celebrated as National Space Day in India.
