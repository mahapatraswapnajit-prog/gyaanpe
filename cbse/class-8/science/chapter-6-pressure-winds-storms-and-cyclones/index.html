Class 8 Science Chapter 6: Pressure, Winds, Storms, and Cyclones

NCERT Curiosity · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Pressure depends on force and area, and acts in liquids and air. Uneven heating creates pressure differences that cause winds; rising moist air builds thunderstorms, and over warm seas powerful cyclones form. Early warnings and safety steps save lives.

Key concepts

📌Pressure

Pressure is force acting on a unit area: pressure = force ÷ area. Its SI unit is the pascal (Pa).

Example: A sharp knife cuts easily because its thin edge has a tiny area.

In real life: Camels' wide feet stop them sinking into sand.

💡 Did you know? Wide school-bag straps spread the weight and hurt less.

💧Pressure in Liquids and Air

Liquids press in all directions, and pressure increases with depth. Air also presses on everything around us.

Example: Water spurts farthest from the lowest hole in a bottle.

In real life: A straw works because air pressure pushes the drink up.

💡 Did you know? Dams are built thicker at the bottom.

🌬️How Winds Form

Uneven heating makes air pressure differ. Air moves from high-pressure areas to low-pressure areas; this moving air is wind.

Example: Sea breeze blows from sea to land in the day; land breeze at night.

In real life: Monsoon winds bring rain to India each year.

💡 Did you know? Fast-moving air has lower pressure, so strong winds can lift roofs.

⛈️Thunderstorms and Lightning

Warm, moist air rises fast and forms tall clouds. Charges build up in clouds and discharge as lightning.

Example: During lightning, stay indoors; avoid open fields, tall trees and water.

In real life: Lightning conductors protect tall buildings.

💡 Did you know? Thunder is the sound of air heated suddenly by lightning.

🌀Cyclones

A cyclone is a huge storm with a calm low-pressure centre called the eye, surrounded by very strong winds. Cyclones form over warm ocean water.

Example: Odisha faced the Super Cyclone of 1999 and Cyclone Fani in 2019.

In real life: The India Meteorological Department (IMD) issues cyclone warnings so people can move to shelters.

💡 Did you know? They are called hurricanes and typhoons in other parts of the world.

  • Wind blows from high pressure to low pressure.

Practice questions

Q1. Pressure is equal to

  1. Area ÷ force
  2. Force ÷ area
  3. Force × area
  4. Mass × area
Show answerB) Force ÷ area

Q2. The SI unit of pressure is the

  1. Newton
  2. Joule
  3. Watt
  4. Pascal
Show answerD) Pascal

Q3. A sharp knife cuts easily because

  1. Air pushes it
  2. It is heavier
  3. Its small edge area creates more pressure
  4. It has more friction
Show answerC) Its small edge area creates more pressure

Q4. Wind blows from

  1. High pressure to low pressure
  2. Low pressure to high pressure
  3. Only east to west
  4. Only at night
Show answerA) High pressure to low pressure

Q5. In a water tank, pressure is greatest

  1. Equal everywhere
  2. At the top
  3. At the bottom
  4. In the middle
Show answerC) At the bottom
