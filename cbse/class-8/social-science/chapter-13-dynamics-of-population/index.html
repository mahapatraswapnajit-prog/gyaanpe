Class 8 Social Science Chapter 13: Dynamics of Population

NCERT Exploring Society: India and Beyond · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

The chapter studies how population is counted, how it is spread out, how it changes through births, deaths and migration, and what age, sex ratio and literacy tell us — including India's opportunity from a young population.

Key concepts

👨‍👩‍👧Counting People

A census counts the population and collects details like age, education and work. India's census is usually held every 10 years.

Example: India's first complete census was in 1881.

In real life: Census data helps plan schools and hospitals.

💡 Did you know? India became the world's most populous country in 2023, as estimated by the United Nations.

📏Population Density

Population density = number of people per square kilometre.

Example: The Ganga plains are densely populated; deserts and high mountains are thinly populated.

In real life: Fertile land and water attract people.

💡 Did you know? Bihar is one of India's most densely populated states.

📈Births, Deaths and Growth

Population grows when births exceed deaths. Better healthcare lowers death rates.

Example: Birth rate and death rate are counted per 1,000 people.

In real life: Vaccination has helped reduce child deaths.

💡 Did you know? Education of girls is linked to smaller, healthier families.

🚆Migration

People move from one place to another for jobs, education, marriage or safety. Moving from villages to cities is rural–urban migration.

Example: Many workers from Odisha migrate to cities like Surat and Bengaluru.

In real life: Festivals see trains crowded with migrants going home.

💡 Did you know? Migrants send money home (remittances) that supports families.

👶Population Composition

Age, sex ratio and literacy describe a population. India has a large young population — a demographic dividend if they get education and jobs.

Example: Sex ratio = number of females per 1,000 males.

In real life: Skill training can turn a young population into strength.

💡 Did you know? More than half of Indians are below 30 years of age.
