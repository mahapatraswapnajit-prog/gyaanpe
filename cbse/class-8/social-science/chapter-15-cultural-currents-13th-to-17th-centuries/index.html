Class 8 Social Science Chapter 15: Cultural Currents: 13th to 17th Centuries

NCERT Exploring Society: India and Beyond · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Between the 13th and 17th centuries, the Bhakti and Sufi movements spread messages of devotion, love and equality. Saints like Kabir, Mirabai, Chaitanya, Sankaradeva and Guru Nanak, and Sufis like Nizamuddin Auliya, enriched regional languages, music and culture.

Key concepts

🙏The Bhakti Movement

Bhakti saints taught devotion to God through love, simple language and equality, rejecting caste discrimination.

Example: Kabir, Mirabai, Tulsidas, Surdas and Ravidas wrote devotional poems.

In real life: Bhajans by these saints are still sung across India.

💡 Did you know? Tulsidas wrote the Ramcharitmanas in Awadhi.

🌸Saints of the East and South

Chaitanya Mahaprabhu spread Krishna bhakti in Bengal and spent his later years in Puri. In Assam, Sankaradeva founded the Ekasarana tradition.

Example: Jayadeva's Gita Govinda, from Odisha, is sung at the Jagannath temple.

In real life: Kirtans in Puri continue this tradition.

💡 Did you know? Sankaradeva set up namghars (prayer halls) in Assam.

☪️Sufi Traditions

Sufis spoke of love for God and service to people. Their khanqahs (hospices) welcomed everyone.

Example: Nizamuddin Auliya in Delhi and Moinuddin Chishti in Ajmer.

In real life: Qawwali music comes from Sufi gatherings.

💡 Did you know? Amir Khusrau, a poet and musician, was Nizamuddin Auliya's disciple.

🪯Guru Nanak and Sikhism

Guru Nanak (1469–1539) taught one God, equality and honest work. His teachings founded Sikhism.

Example: The langar (community kitchen) feeds everyone together.

In real life: Gurdwaras serve free meals to all visitors.

💡 Did you know? The Guru Granth Sahib also includes verses of Kabir and other saints.

📚Languages, Music and Art

Regional languages, literature and music flourished as saints wrote in the language of the people.

Example: Poets wrote in Hindi, Marathi, Odia, Bengali, Tamil and more.

In real life: Classical and folk music still use these devotional songs.

💡 Did you know? Sarala Das wrote the Mahabharata in Odia in the 15th century.

  • Guru Nanak founded Sikhism and the langar tradition.
