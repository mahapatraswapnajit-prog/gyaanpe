Class 8 Social Science Chapter 3: The Rise of the Marathas

NCERT Exploring Society: India and Beyond · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

The Marathas rose in the Deccan under Shivaji, who used forts, a navy and swift warfare and ran a well-organised state with the Ashtapradhan. Under the Peshwas Maratha power spread widely before the setback at Panipat in 1761.

Key concepts

🚩Shivaji's Rise

Shivaji, born in 1630 at Shivneri fort, built an independent Maratha kingdom in the Deccan against the Bijapur Sultanate and the Mughals.

Example: He was crowned Chhatrapati at Raigad in 1674.

In real life: Shivneri and Raigad forts attract many visitors.

💡 Did you know? His mother Jijabai shaped his values and courage.

⛰️Forts and Smart Warfare

The Marathas used hill forts and quick surprise attacks suited to the hilly Sahyadri region (guerrilla warfare).

Example: Shivaji captured and built many forts, including sea forts like Sindhudurg.

In real life: Indian armies still study these mountain tactics.

💡 Did you know? He built a navy to guard the western coast.

📜Administration

Shivaji ran his kingdom with a council of eight ministers, the Ashtapradhan, and fair land revenue.

Example: Officers were paid salaries instead of being given land.

In real life: A council of ministers advises leaders even today.

💡 Did you know? He encouraged the use of Marathi and Sanskrit in administration.

📈After Shivaji

Under the Peshwas the Maratha power spread across much of India in the 18th century.

Example: Peshwa Baji Rao I led swift campaigns into North India.

In real life: Pune was the Peshwas' seat of power.

💡 Did you know? The Marathas lost the Third Battle of Panipat (1761) to Ahmad Shah Abdali.

🎭Culture and Legacy

The Marathas supported temples, literature and local traditions, and inspired later ideas of self-rule.

Example: They collected taxes called chauth and sardeshmukhi from many regions.

In real life: Shivaji Jayanti is celebrated widely in Maharashtra.

💡 Did you know? Saint-poets like Tukaram lived during this time.

Quick revision

  • Shivaji was crowned Chhatrapati in 1674 at Raigad.
  • Shivaji had a council of eight ministers (Ashtapradhan).
  • The Marathas lost the Third Battle of Panipat in 1761.
