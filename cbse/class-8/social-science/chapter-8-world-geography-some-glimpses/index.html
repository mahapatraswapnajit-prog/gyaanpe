Class 8 Social Science Chapter 8: World Geography: Some Glimpses

NCERT Exploring Society: India and Beyond · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

The chapter gives a quick world tour: continents and oceans, latitudes and longitudes, major mountains, deserts and plains, climate zones from the Equator to the poles, and India's place in the wider world.

Key concepts

🌐Continents and Oceans

The Earth has seven continents and five oceans. Asia is the largest continent; the Pacific is the largest ocean.

Example: Continents: Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Antarctica, Europe, Australia.

In real life: Ships carry most world trade across the oceans.

💡 Did you know? Oceans cover about 71% of the Earth's surface.

📍Latitudes and Longitudes

Imaginary lines help locate places. The Equator (0° latitude) divides north and south; the Prime Meridian (0° longitude) passes through Greenwich, London.

Example: India lies north of the Equator; the Tropic of Cancer passes through it.

In real life: GPS uses latitude and longitude.

💡 Did you know? India's standard time is based on 82½° E longitude.

🏔️Major Landforms

Mountains, plateaus, plains and deserts shape where people live.

Example: Mount Everest in the Himalayas is the highest peak on Earth.

In real life: Most people live on fertile plains near rivers.

💡 Did you know? The Sahara is the largest hot desert in the world.

🌦️Climate Zones

Places near the Equator are hot; places near the poles are cold; the temperate zones in between have milder seasons.

Example: The Amazon rainforest near the Equator gets rain almost every day.

In real life: Climate decides crops, clothes and houses.

💡 Did you know? Antarctica is the coldest continent.

🤝India and the World

India is connected to the world by trade, people and ideas across oceans and mountains.

Example: The Indian Ocean is the only ocean named after a country.

In real life: Indians live and work in almost every country.

💡 Did you know? India shares land borders with seven countries.

  • Asia is the largest continent; Pacific the largest ocean.
  • The Tropic of Cancer passes through India.
