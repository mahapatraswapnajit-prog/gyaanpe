Class 8 Social Science Chapter 9: India's Long Road to Independence

NCERT Exploring Society: India and Beyond · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

From the founding of the Congress in 1885 to independence in 1947, Indians struggled through Swadeshi, Gandhi's non-violent mass movements, revolutionary action and the INA. Freedom came with the pain of Partition.

Key concepts

🇮🇳Early Nationalism

After 1857 educated Indians formed organisations to demand rights. The Indian National Congress was founded in 1885.

Example: Leaders like Dadabhai Naoroji explained how Britain drained India's wealth.

In real life: Newspapers spread national ideas.

💡 Did you know? Bal Gangadhar Tilak said, 'Swaraj is my birthright and I shall have it.'

🧵Swadeshi Movement

The Partition of Bengal in 1905 led to the Swadeshi movement: boycott of British goods and use of Indian-made goods.

Example: People made bonfires of foreign cloth.

In real life: 'Buy Indian' campaigns echo this idea.

💡 Did you know? The partition was cancelled in 1911.

🕊️Gandhi and Mass Movements

Mahatma Gandhi led non-violent struggles: Champaran (1917), Non-Cooperation (1920), the Salt March to Dandi (1930) and Quit India (1942).

Example: The Jallianwala Bagh massacre of 1919 shocked the whole nation.

In real life: 2 October, Gandhi's birthday, is a national holiday.

💡 Did you know? Gandhi called his method satyagraha — the force of truth.

⚔️Many Paths to Freedom

Revolutionaries like Bhagat Singh and Subhas Chandra Bose's Indian National Army also fought for freedom.

Example: Odisha leaders like Gopabandhu Das and Madhusudan Das inspired people in the state.

In real life: Netaji's birthday, 23 January, is Parakram Diwas.

💡 Did you know? Women such as Sarojini Naidu and Rama Devi played major roles.

🎉Freedom and Partition

India became independent on 15 August 1947, but was partitioned, causing huge suffering and migration.

Example: Jawaharlal Nehru became the first Prime Minister.

In real life: Independence Day and Republic Day honour this long struggle.

💡 Did you know? The Constitution came into force on 26 January 1950.

Key terms

  • The Salt March to Dandi was in 1930.
  • India became independent on 15 August 1947.
