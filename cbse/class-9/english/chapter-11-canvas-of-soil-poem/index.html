Class 9 English Chapter 11: Canvas of Soil (Poem)

NCERT Kaveri · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

The poem compares gardening to painting on a canvas of soil, using metaphor and nature imagery to show that beautiful creation needs patience.

Key concepts

🌱About the Poem

The poem presents gardening as an art: the soil is like a canvas and the gardener like a painter who creates beauty with patience.

Example: Seeds, colours of flowers and green leaves become 'paint'.

In real life: Kitchen gardens are popular on terraces.

💡 Did you know? Gardening needs patience — results come slowly.

🖼️Metaphor

A metaphor compares directly without 'like' or 'as': 'The soil is a canvas.'

Example: 'The garden is a rainbow.'

In real life: Advertisements use metaphors.

💡 Did you know? A simile uses 'like' or 'as': 'The garden is like a rainbow.'

🌸Nature Imagery

Words about colour, smell and touch — fragrant blossoms, damp earth, soft petals — create rich images.

Example: 'The damp earth smells sweet after rain.'

In real life: Nature walks calm the mind.

💡 Did you know? The smell after rain is called petrichor.

⏳Patience and Creation

Creating anything beautiful — a garden, a skill, a friendship — takes time and care.

Example: Watering a sapling daily until it blooms.

In real life: Long-term projects need patience.

💡 Did you know? Some trees take years to bear fruit.

📝Vocabulary

Canvas (cloth for painting), bloom (flower), tend (look after), fragrant (sweet-smelling), sapling (young tree).

Example: She tends her garden every morning.

In real life: Nurseries sell saplings.

💡 Did you know? 'Horticulture' is the science of gardening.

Quick revision

  • 'The soil is a canvas' is a metaphor.
  • A simile uses 'like' or 'as'.
  • Gardening teaches patience.
