Class 9 English Chapter 13: Nine Gold Medals (Poem)

NCERT Kaveri · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

David Roth's narrative poem tells of Special Olympics runners who stop to help a fallen competitor and finish together, redefining winning through empathy and sportsmanship.

Key concepts

🏃About the Poem

David Roth's poem (also a song) describes a race at the Special Olympics. When one runner stumbles, the others turn back to help him, and they all cross the finish line together.

Example: In the end, every runner receives a gold medal.

In real life: Many schools sing this song on sports day.

💡 Did you know? The Special Olympics are for people with intellectual disabilities.

🤝Redefining Winning

The poem shows that kindness can matter more than coming first. Empathy and teamwork make everyone a winner.

Example: Helping a fallen teammate instead of rushing ahead.

In real life: Fair-play awards exist in many sports.

💡 Did you know? Sportsmanship means fair play and respect.

📖Narrative Poem

A narrative poem tells a story with characters, setting and events, often with rhythm and rhyme.

Example: The poem sets the scene, builds to the fall, then the surprising act of kindness.

In real life: Folk songs often tell stories.

💡 Did you know? Ballads are a kind of narrative poem.

🎵Rhyme and Rhythm

Rhyme scheme names end-sounds with letters (aabb, abab). Rhythm is the beat of the lines.

Example: Lines ending in 'race/place/face' rhyme.

In real life: Jingles use rhyme to stick in memory.

💡 Did you know? Songs need strong rhythm.

📝Vocabulary

Empathy (understanding others' feelings), stumble (trip), spectators (watchers), sportsmanship, triumph (great victory).

Example: The spectators stood and cheered.

In real life: Team captains need empathy.

💡 Did you know? Empathy differs from sympathy: empathy feels with, sympathy feels for.

Quick revision

  • The poem is by David Roth.
  • It is set at the Special Olympics.
  • All the runners got gold medals.

Practice questions

Q1. 'Nine Gold Medals' was written by

  1. Rabindranath Tagore
  2. Sudha Murty
  3. Charles Swain
  4. David Roth
Show answerD) David Roth

Q2. The poem is set at the

  1. Asian Games
  2. Special Olympics
  3. Cricket World Cup
  4. School exam
Show answerB) Special Olympics

Q3. When one runner stumbled, the others

  1. Ran faster
  2. Went back to help him
  3. Laughed
  4. Left the race
Show answerB) Went back to help him

Q4. At the end of the race,

  1. All the runners got gold medals
  2. Only one got gold
  3. The race was cancelled
  4. No one got a medal
Show answerA) All the runners got gold medals

Q5. The main value in the poem is

  1. Winning at any cost
  2. Speed only
  3. Empathy over competition
  4. Fame
Show answerC) Empathy over competition
