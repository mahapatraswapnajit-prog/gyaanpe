Class 9 English Chapter 15: Words (Poem)

NCERT Kaveri · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Charles Swain's poem 'Words' shows that deep feelings often go beyond what words can express, valuing sincerity over eloquence.

Key concepts

💬About the Poem

Charles Swain's poem reflects on the limits of language: words can be beautiful, but feelings like true love, grief or gratitude are often deeper than words can express. Sincerity matters more than fancy speech.

Example: A silent hug can say more than a long speech.

In real life: Kind actions often speak louder than words.

💡 Did you know? Charles Swain was a 19th-century English poet.

❤️Sincerity over Eloquence

Eloquence is fluent, impressive speaking; sincerity is honesty of feeling. The poem values sincere hearts over clever words.

Example: A simple 'thank you' meant truly is powerful.

In real life: Good leaders speak sincerely.

💡 Did you know? Empty flattery is easy to spot.

🤐Proverbs on Words

'Actions speak louder than words.' 'Think before you speak.' 'A soft answer turns away anger.'

Example: Keeping a promise shows more than saying it.

In real life: Elders often teach through proverbs.

💡 Did you know? Proverbs carry wisdom in few words.

🔤Rhetorical Question

A question asked for effect, not for an answer: 'What words can tell how deep love is?'

Example: 'Who can count the stars?'

In real life: Speeches and ads use them.

💡 Did you know? Rhetorical questions make readers think.

📝Vocabulary

Eloquent (fluent and persuasive), sincere (genuine), express, inadequate (not enough), heartfelt (deeply felt).

Example: Her heartfelt letter moved everyone.

In real life: Debaters train to be eloquent.

💡 Did you know? 'Eloquence' comes from Latin 'eloqui', to speak out.
