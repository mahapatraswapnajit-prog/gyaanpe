Class 9 English Chapter 5: The World of Limitless Possibilities

NCERT Kaveri · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Through an interview with Dr Deepa Malik, India's first woman Paralympic medallist, the lesson shows ability beyond disability and teaches interview skills, inclusive language and words of resilience.

Key concepts

🏅About Deepa Malik

This interview-based lesson presents Dr Deepa Malik, who became the first Indian woman to win a Paralympic medal — silver in shot put at Rio 2016 — despite using a wheelchair.

Example: She was later honoured with the Padma Shri and the Khel Ratna.

In real life: She went on to lead the Paralympic Committee of India.

💡 Did you know? She also took part in swimming, biking and rallies.

🌈Ability Beyond Disability

The lesson shows that determination, training and support open limitless possibilities. Disability does not decide a person's dreams.

Example: Para-athletes train as hard as any athlete.

In real life: Accessible ramps and transport help everyone.

💡 Did you know? India won a record medal haul at the Tokyo 2020 Paralympics.

🎤Interview Format

An interview has questions (Q) and answers (A). Good interview questions are open-ended ('How', 'Why', 'What').

Example: 'What kept you going during tough times?'

In real life: Journalists and podcasters use interviews.

💡 Did you know? Closed questions get only yes/no answers.

🗣️Inclusive Language

Use respectful words: 'person with a disability', 'wheelchair user', 'para-athlete'.

Example: Say 'wheelchair user', not 'confined to a wheelchair'.

In real life: Inclusive language builds dignity.

💡 Did you know? India's Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act came in 2016.

📝Vocabulary

Resilience (bouncing back), perseverance (continuing despite difficulty), inspire, accomplish, limitless.

Example: Her resilience inspired millions.

In real life: Coaches build resilience in players.

💡 Did you know? 'Para' in Paralympics means 'alongside' the Olympics.

  • Open questions start with how, why, what.

Practice questions

Q1. Deepa Malik won a Paralympic medal in

  1. Wrestling
  2. Shot put
  3. Archery
  4. Badminton
Show answerB) Shot put

Q2. Deepa Malik won her Paralympic silver at

  1. London 2012
  2. Beijing 2008
  3. Rio 2016
  4. Tokyo 2020
Show answerC) Rio 2016

Q3. She was the first Indian woman to win a

  1. Paralympic medal
  2. Cricket World Cup
  3. Nobel Prize
  4. Chess title

Q4. 'Resilience' means

  1. Weakness
  2. Ability to bounce back
  3. Anger
  4. Laziness
