Class 9 English Chapter 6: Twin Melodies

NCERT Kaveri · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Mitra Phukan's play 'Twin Melodies' explores traditional and fusion music and how honesty bridges generations, while teaching the elements and format of a play.

Key concepts

🎭About the Play

'Twin Melodies' by Mitra Phukan is a short play about music — traditional and modern — and how honest conversation can bridge differences between generations.

Example: A traditional singer and a young musician discover common ground.

In real life: Fusion bands mix classical ragas with modern beats.

💡 Did you know? Mitra Phukan is an Assamese writer and trained classical vocalist.

🎶Tradition and Fusion

Tradition carries old forms with discipline; fusion blends styles to create something new. Both can respect each other.

Example: A sitar played with a guitar in one song.

In real life: Film music often blends styles.

💡 Did you know? Indian classical music has two main traditions: Hindustani and Carnatic.

🎬Elements of a Play

A play has characters, dialogue, stage directions (in brackets/italics), scenes and conflict.

Example: (She picks up the tanpura and smiles.) — a stage direction.

In real life: School annual-day plays follow these elements.

💡 Did you know? Stage directions are not spoken aloud.

💬Dialogue Punctuation

In a play script, the speaker's name comes first, then a colon or full stop, then the words — no quotation marks needed.

Example: RIYA: Shall we practise together?

In real life: TV scripts follow the same format.

💡 Did you know? Stories use quotation marks; scripts do not.

🤝Generation Gap

Differences in taste between generations are natural. Listening and honesty turn conflict into understanding.

Example: A grandparent learns a new app; a teen learns an old raga.

In real life: Family discussions grow stronger with listening.

💡 Did you know? Musical traditions survive because each generation adds something.

  • 'Twin Melodies' is a play by Mitra Phukan.
  • Stage directions are not spoken.
  • Hindustani and Carnatic are India's two classical traditions.
