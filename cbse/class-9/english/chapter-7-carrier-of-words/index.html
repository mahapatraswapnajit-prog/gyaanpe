Class 9 English Chapter 7: Carrier of Words

NCERT Kaveri · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

The lesson portrays Khetaram, a dutiful village postman who connects families and builds trust, and teaches about the postal system and formal letter writing.

Key concepts

✉️About the Lesson

The lesson tells of Khetaram, a village postman whose daily work connects families, carries news and builds trust in the community.

Example: Delivering letters, money orders and pension to remote homes.

In real life: Postmen also deliver Aadhaar and bank services now.

💡 Did you know? India Post has one of the largest postal networks in the world.

🤝Responsibility and Service

Public servants like postmen carry great responsibility: honesty, punctuality and care for people's messages and money.

Example: Keeping a letter safe until it reaches the right hand.

In real life: Teachers, nurses and postmen are everyday heroes.

💡 Did you know? Every post office has a six-digit PIN code area.

📮The Postal System

PIN (Postal Index Number) has six digits; the first digit shows the region. Services include speed post, money orders and savings accounts.

Example: PIN codes beginning with 7 cover eastern areas like Odisha and West Bengal.

In real life: Online orders still need correct PIN codes.

💡 Did you know? The PIN system started in 1972.

✍️Formal Letter Format

Sender's address, date, receiver's address, subject, salutation, body, complimentary close, signature and name.

Example: Subject: Request for a new post box in our village

In real life: Complaints to offices use formal letters.

💡 Did you know? Formal letters are brief and polite.

📝Vocabulary

Punctual (on time), remote (far away), trust, dutiful (doing one's duty well), lifeline (something essential).

Example: For the village, the postman was a lifeline.

In real life: Courier services follow the same idea.

💡 Did you know? 'Postman' is now often called 'postal worker'.
