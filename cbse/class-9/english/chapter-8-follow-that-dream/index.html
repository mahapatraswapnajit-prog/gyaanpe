Class 9 English Chapter 8: Follow That Dream

NCERT Kaveri · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Written as a mother's letter to her teenage daughter, the lesson offers honest guidance on following dreams through effort and courage, and teaches informal letters and modals of advice.

Key concepts

💌About the Lesson

Written as a mother's letter to her teenage daughter, the lesson gives honest guidance about dreams, hard work, failure and courage.

Example: A parent advising a child to choose a path with passion and patience.

In real life: Many famous people wrote letters of advice to their children.

💡 Did you know? Letters let us share feelings we find hard to say aloud.

🌟Dreams Need Work

A dream becomes real through planning, effort and patience. Setbacks are part of the journey.

Example: Practising daily for a dance exam even on tired days.

In real life: Athletes and scientists fail many times before success.

💡 Did you know? Failure is often a lesson, not an end.

💛Honest Advice

Good advice is honest, kind and respects the listener's choices.

Example: 'Follow your interest, but be ready to work hard.'

In real life: Mentors guide students in schools and workplaces.

💡 Did you know? Listening carefully is the first step to good advice.

✍️Informal Letter

An informal letter has the sender's address, date, a friendly greeting, a warm and personal body, and a close like 'With love'.

Example: Dear Riya, … With love, Mom

In real life: Letters to family and friends are informal.

💡 Did you know? Informal letters can use contractions like 'I'm'.

🔮Modals of Advice

Should, ought to, must and had better give advice or show necessity.

Example: You should rest before the exam. You must not give up.

In real life: Doctors and coaches use modals of advice.

💡 Did you know? 'Had better' warns of a bad result.

Quick revision

  • The lesson is a mother's letter to her daughter.
  • Informal letters end with 'With love' etc.
  • 'Should' gives advice.

Practice questions

Q1. 'Follow That Dream' is written as a letter from a

  1. Teacher to a principal
  2. Postman to a village
  3. Boss to an employee
  4. Mother to her daughter
Show answerD) Mother to her daughter

Q2. The lesson teaches that dreams come true through

  1. Wishing only
  2. Luck only
  3. Effort and patience
  4. Sleeping
Show answerC) Effort and patience

Q3. Which modal gives advice?

  1. Can
  2. Should
  3. Might
  4. May
Show answerB) Should

Q4. An informal letter is written to

  1. A government office
  2. Family or friends
  3. A newspaper editor
  4. A bank manager
Show answerB) Family or friends

Q5. A suitable ending for an informal letter is

  1. Regards, Department
  2. Sincerely, Manager
  3. With love
  4. Yours faithfully
Show answerC) With love
