Class 9 English Chapter 9: Bharat Our Land (Poem)

NCERT Kaveri · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

Subramania Bharati's poem celebrates India's land, heritage and knowledge with pride, using a refrain and vivid imagery.

Key concepts

🇮🇳About the Poem

Written by the Tamil poet Subramania Bharati, the poem (in translation) celebrates India — its rivers, mountains, knowledge, arts and people — with deep pride.

Example: The poem praises the land's beauty and heritage.

In real life: Patriotic songs are sung on Independence Day.

💡 Did you know? Bharati is also called 'Mahakavi Bharathiyar'.

🔁Refrain

A refrain is a line or phrase repeated at regular places in a poem. It stresses the main idea and gives rhythm.

Example: A repeated line praising 'our land' after each stanza.

In real life: Song choruses are refrains.

💡 Did you know? Refrains make poems easy to remember and sing.

🏔️Imagery

Imagery uses words that appeal to the senses to create pictures in the mind — snowy peaks, flowing rivers, green fields.

Example: 'Rivers that sparkle in the sun' creates a visual image.

In real life: Travel ads use imagery.

💡 Did you know? Good imagery lets readers 'see' the poem.

📜Subramania Bharati

Bharati (1882–1921) was a poet and freedom fighter who wrote for women's rights, social reform and national unity.

Example: His birth anniversary, 11 December, is celebrated as Bharatiya Bhasha Diwas.

In real life: His songs are still sung across Tamil Nadu.

💡 Did you know? He worked as a journalist too.

❤️Patriotism

Patriotism is love for one's country, shown by respect for its people, nature and heritage and by doing one's duties.

Example: Keeping public places clean is patriotic too.

In real life: Volunteering serves the nation.

💡 Did you know? The poem shows pride without hatred for others.
