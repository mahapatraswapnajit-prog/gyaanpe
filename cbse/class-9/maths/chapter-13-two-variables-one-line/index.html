Class 9 Maths Chapter 13: Two Variables, One Line

NCERT Ganita Manjari · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

The chapter studies linear equations in two variables: their infinitely many solutions, straight-line graphs, intercepts, and solving a pair of equations graphically and algebraically.

Key concepts

✏️Linear Equations in Two Variables

An equation like ax + by + c = 0 (a and b not both 0) is linear in two variables. It has infinitely many solutions.

Example: x + y = 5: (0, 5), (1, 4), (2, 3), … all work.

In real life: Combinations of ₹2 and ₹5 coins making ₹20.

💡 Did you know? Each solution is an ordered pair (x, y).

📈The Graph is a Line

All solutions of a linear equation in two variables lie on a straight line, and every point on the line is a solution.

Example: 2x + y = 7 passes through (2, 3) and (0, 7).

In real life: y = 2x passes through the origin.

💡 Did you know? x = 3 is a vertical line; y = 2 is a horizontal line.

🎯Intercepts

The x-intercept is where the line meets the x-axis (y = 0); the y-intercept is where it meets the y-axis (x = 0).

Example: 3x + 2y = 12: x-intercept (4, 0), y-intercept (0, 6).

In real life: Quick sketches of cost graphs use intercepts.

💡 Did you know? Two points are enough to draw a line.

✖️Two Lines Together

Two linear equations together may meet at one point (one solution), never meet (parallel, no solution) or coincide (infinitely many).

Example: x + y = 10 and x − y = 2 meet at (6, 4).

In real life: Comparing two mobile plans finds where they cost the same.

💡 Did you know? Parallel lines have the same slope.

🧮Solving Algebraically

Substitution or elimination finds the common solution without a graph.

Example: Add x + y = 10 and x − y = 2: 2x = 12, x = 6, y = 4.

In real life: Splitting a bill between items with two unknown prices.

💡 Did you know? Algebra gives exact answers when graphs are hard to read.

Key terms

TermMeaning
Linear equation in two variablesax + by + c = 0
SolutionOrdered pair that satisfies the equation
InterceptWhere a line meets an axis
Parallel linesNever meet; no common solution
EliminationAdding/subtracting equations to remove a variable

Quick revision

  • A linear equation in two variables has infinitely many solutions.
  • Its graph is a straight line.
  • Parallel lines → no solution.

Practice questions

Q1. x + y = 5 has

  1. No solution
  2. Two solutions
  3. Infinitely many solutions
  4. Exactly one solution
Show answerC) Infinitely many solutions

Q2. The graph of a linear equation in two variables is a

  1. Circle
  2. Straight line
  3. Parabola
  4. Point
Show answerB) Straight line

Q3. Which point satisfies 2x + y = 7?

  1. (1, 1)
  2. (0, 5)
  3. (2, 3)
  4. (3, 2)
Show answerC) (2, 3)

Q4. Solve x + y = 10 and x − y = 2.

  1. (5, 5)
  2. (4, 6)
  3. (6, 4)
  4. (8, 2)
Show answerC) (6, 4)

Q5. Two parallel lines as a pair of equations have

  1. Infinitely many solutions
  2. No solution
  3. One solution
  4. Two solutions
Show answerB) No solution
