Class 9 Science Chapter 3: Tissues in Action

NCERT Exploration · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

The chapter explains plant tissues (meristematic and permanent, including xylem and phloem) and animal tissues (epithelial, connective, muscular and nervous) and how they work together.

Key concepts

🧩What is a Tissue?

A tissue is a group of similar cells working together for a particular job.

Example: Muscle tissue contracts; blood carries materials.

In real life: Doctors study tissue samples (biopsy).

💡 Did you know? Cells → tissues → organs → organ systems → organism.

🌱Meristematic Tissue

Plant meristems are dividing tissues found at root and shoot tips (apical), at the base of leaves (intercalary) and in the sides of stems (lateral).

Example: Apical meristem makes the root longer.

In real life: Grass regrows after mowing due to intercalary meristem.

💡 Did you know? Lateral meristem makes stems thicker.

🪵Permanent Plant Tissues

Parenchyma stores food; collenchyma gives flexibility; sclerenchyma gives hardness. Xylem carries water up; phloem carries food.

Example: Coconut husk fibres are sclerenchyma.

In real life: Jute and coir come from plant fibres.

💡 Did you know? Xylem vessels in wood are dead cells.

🩸Epithelial and Connective

Epithelium covers and lines organs. Connective tissues — blood, bone, cartilage, tendons, ligaments, fat — join and support.

Example: Tendons join muscle to bone; ligaments join bone to bone.

In real life: Sports injuries often involve ligaments.

💡 Did you know? Blood is a fluid connective tissue.

💪Muscle and Nerve

Striated (voluntary) muscle moves limbs; smooth (involuntary) muscle lines the gut; cardiac muscle keeps the heart beating. Nerve cells (neurons) carry messages.

Example: The heart beats without our control.

In real life: Reflexes depend on quick nerve signals.

💡 Did you know? The longest neurons can be about a metre long.

Quick revision

  • Xylem carries water; phloem carries food.
  • Ligaments join bone to bone.
  • Cardiac muscle never tires in normal life.

Practice questions

Q1. Water is carried in plants by

  1. Phloem
  2. Parenchyma
  3. Epidermis
  4. Xylem
Show answerD) Xylem

Q2. Food is carried in plants by

  1. Cork
  2. Phloem
  3. Sclerenchyma
  4. Xylem
Show answerB) Phloem

Q3. Tendons connect

  1. Bone to bone
  2. Muscle to bone
  3. Nerve to muscle
  4. Skin to bone
Show answerB) Muscle to bone

Q4. Ligaments connect

  1. Bone to bone
  2. Muscle to bone
  3. Skin to muscle
  4. Heart to lungs
Show answerA) Bone to bone

Q5. Blood is a type of

  1. Connective tissue
  2. Nervous tissue
  3. Muscle tissue
  4. Epithelial tissue
Show answerA) Connective tissue
