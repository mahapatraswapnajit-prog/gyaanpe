Class 9 Science Chapter 4: Describing Motion Around Us

NCERT Exploration · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

The chapter describes motion using distance, displacement, speed, velocity and acceleration, interprets distance-time and velocity-time graphs, and uses the three equations of motion.

Key concepts

📍Distance and Displacement

Distance is the total path length (scalar). Displacement is the shortest straight-line distance from start to finish in a given direction (vector).

Example: Walk 3 m east then 4 m north: distance 7 m, displacement 5 m.

In real life: A runner finishing a full lap has zero displacement.

💡 Did you know? Displacement can be zero even if distance is not.

🚗Speed and Velocity

Speed = distance ÷ time. Velocity = displacement ÷ time, with direction. Unit: m/s.

Example: 72 km/h = 20 m/s (divide by 3.6).

In real life: Speedometers show instantaneous speed.

💡 Did you know? Average speed = total distance ÷ total time.

🚀Acceleration

Acceleration = change in velocity ÷ time, unit m/s². Slowing down is negative acceleration (retardation).

Example: 0 to 20 m/s in 5 s → a = 4 m/s².

In real life: Braking cars decelerate.

💡 Did you know? A body moving in a circle at constant speed is still accelerating, since direction changes.

📈Motion Graphs

On a distance-time graph, slope = speed. On a velocity-time graph, slope = acceleration and area under it = displacement.

Example: A straight sloping d-t line means uniform speed.

In real life: Fitness apps draw such graphs.

💡 Did you know? A flat d-t line means the object is at rest.

🧮Equations of Motion

For uniform acceleration: v = u + at; s = ut + ½at²; v² = u² + 2as.

Example: u = 0, a = 2 m/s², t = 5 s → v = 10 m/s, s = 25 m.

In real life: Engineers use them to design braking distances.

💡 Did you know? These work only when acceleration is constant.

  • Slope of a v-t graph = acceleration.
  • v = u + at.

Practice questions

Q1. A person walks 3 m east and 4 m north. The displacement is

  1. 5 m
  2. 1 m
  3. 7 m
  4. 12 m
Show answerA) 5 m

Q2. 72 km/h in m/s is

  1. 36 m/s
  2. 20 m/s
  3. 7.2 m/s
  4. 72 m/s
Show answerB) 20 m/s

Q3. The SI unit of acceleration is

  1. m/s²
  2. m/s
  3. s/m
  4. m
Show answerA) m/s²

Q4. A car goes from 0 to 20 m/s in 5 s. Its acceleration is

  1. 5 m/s²
  2. 4 m/s²
  3. 100 m/s²
  4. 25 m/s²
Show answerB) 4 m/s²

Q5. The slope of a distance-time graph gives

  1. Acceleration
  2. Force
  3. Speed
  4. Displacement
Show answerC) Speed
