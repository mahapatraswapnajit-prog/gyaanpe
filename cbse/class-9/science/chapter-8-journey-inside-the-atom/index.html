Class 9 Science Chapter 8: Journey Inside the Atom

NCERT Exploration · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

The chapter traces atomic models from Thomson to Rutherford and Bohr, introduces electrons, protons and neutrons, electron shells, valency, atomic and mass numbers, isotopes and isobars.

Key concepts

⚡Electrons, Protons, Neutrons

J.J. Thomson discovered the electron (negative). Protons are positive; neutrons have no charge and were discovered by Chadwick.

Example: A proton is about 1840 times heavier than an electron.

In real life: TV and X-ray tubes use electron beams.

💡 Did you know? Neutrons and protons sit in the nucleus.

🍮Thomson's Model

Thomson pictured the atom as a positive sphere with electrons embedded in it, like seeds in a watermelon.

Example: Called the 'plum pudding' model.

In real life: Models improve as evidence grows.

💡 Did you know? It could not explain Rutherford's results.

🥇Rutherford's Experiment

Alpha particles fired at thin gold foil mostly passed straight through; a few bounced back. So the atom is mostly empty space with a tiny, dense, positive nucleus.

Example: Like a fly in a large stadium.

In real life: Nuclear medicine relies on understanding the nucleus.

💡 Did you know? His model could not explain why electrons don't fall into the nucleus.

🪐Bohr's Model

Bohr said electrons move in fixed orbits (shells K, L, M, N) with fixed energies and do not lose energy while there.

Example: Maximum electrons in a shell = 2n²: K 2, L 8, M 18.

In real life: Neon signs glow when electrons jump between levels.

💡 Did you know? The outermost shell holds at most 8 electrons.

🔢Atomic Number, Mass Number, Isotopes

Atomic number (Z) = number of protons. Mass number (A) = protons + neutrons. Isotopes have the same Z but different A; isobars have the same A but different Z.

Example: Carbon-12 and carbon-14 are isotopes. Sodium 2,8,1 has valency 1.

In real life: Carbon-14 helps date old remains.

💡 Did you know? Cobalt-60 isotope is used in cancer treatment.

  • Neutron discovered by Chadwick.
  • Shell capacity = 2n².
