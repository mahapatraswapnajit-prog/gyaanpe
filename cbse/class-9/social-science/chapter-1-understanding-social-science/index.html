Class 9 Social Science Chapter 1: Understanding Social Science

NCERT Understanding Society: India and Beyond · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

The chapter introduces social science, its branches, primary and secondary sources, the value of multiple perspectives and why studying society makes us informed citizens.

Key concepts

🌐What is Social Science?

Social science studies people and society — how we live together, use resources, make rules and change over time.

Example: Why do some villages have better schools? Social science asks such questions.

In real life: Government planning depends on social science research.

💡 Did you know? It looks at the same society from many angles.

📚Its Branches

History studies the past; geography studies places and the environment; political science studies power and government; economics studies how resources are used; sociology studies groups and relationships.

Example: A flood can be studied by all five branches.

In real life: Newspaper sections mirror these branches.

💡 Did you know? Branches often overlap — this is called an interdisciplinary approach.

📜Sources and Evidence

Primary sources come from the time studied (inscriptions, coins, letters, surveys). Secondary sources are later studies based on them (textbooks, articles).

Example: An Ashokan inscription is a primary source.

In real life: Courts also weigh evidence from sources.

💡 Did you know? Historians cross-check several sources before concluding.

👀Many Perspectives

The same event can look different to different people. Good social scientists listen to many voices and separate facts from opinions.

Example: A new dam may help cities but displace villagers.

In real life: Debates on new laws show many perspectives.

💡 Did you know? Asking 'who benefits and who loses?' is a key habit.

🧭Why Study It?

Social science helps us become informed, responsible citizens who understand rights, duties and how to solve shared problems.

Example: Knowing your rights as a consumer helps you complain about a faulty product.

In real life: Civil services exams test social science.

💡 Did you know? Voting wisely needs understanding of issues.

Key terms

Quick revision

  • History, geography, political science and economics are branches of social science.
  • Inscriptions and coins are primary sources.
  • Good conclusions use many sources.
