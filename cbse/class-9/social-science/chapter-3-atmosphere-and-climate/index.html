Class 9 Social Science Chapter 3: Atmosphere and Climate

NCERT Understanding Society: India and Beyond · Simple notes, key terms and practice questions

The chapter covers the composition and layers of the atmosphere, the difference between weather and climate, factors affecting climate and India's monsoon climate.

Key concepts

🌬️Composition of Air

Air is mainly nitrogen (about 78%) and oxygen (about 21%), with argon, carbon dioxide, water vapour and dust in small amounts.

Example: Carbon dioxide is tiny in amount but traps heat.

In real life: Pollution changes the mix of air.

💡 Did you know? Dust particles help raindrops form.

🪜Layers of the Atmosphere

Troposphere (weather happens here), stratosphere (ozone layer, smooth for jet planes), mesosphere (meteors burn), thermosphere (auroras, radio reflection), exosphere.

Example: Temperature falls as you climb in the troposphere.

In real life: Satellites orbit far above in the outer layers.

💡 Did you know? Most clouds and storms are in the troposphere.

🌦️Weather and Climate

Weather is the day-to-day state of the atmosphere. Climate is the average weather of a place over many years (about 30+).

Example: 'It rained today' is weather; 'Kerala gets heavy rain' is climate.

In real life: Farmers plan sowing using climate.

💡 Did you know? Climate shapes crops, houses and clothes.

📍Factors Affecting Climate

Latitude, altitude, distance from the sea, winds, ocean currents and relief affect climate.

Example: Shimla is cooler than Delhi due to height.

In real life: Hill stations are popular in summer.

💡 Did you know? Coastal places have milder (maritime) climates.

☔India's Monsoon

India has a monsoon climate: south-west monsoon winds bring most rain from June to September; the north-east monsoon gives winter rain to Tamil Nadu. Local winds include the hot 'loo' and pre-monsoon showers.

Example: Mawsynram in Meghalaya is among the wettest places on Earth.

In real life: Indian farming depends heavily on the monsoon.

💡 Did you know? 'Monsoon' comes from an Arabic word meaning season.

Key terms

TermMeaning
AtmosphereBlanket of air around the Earth
TroposphereLowest layer where weather occurs
ClimateLong-term average weather

LooHot dry summer wind of north India

Quick revision

  • Nitrogen ≈ 78%, oxygen ≈ 21% of air.
  • Weather happens in the troposphere.
  • The south-west monsoon brings most of India's rain.

  2. Mesosphere
