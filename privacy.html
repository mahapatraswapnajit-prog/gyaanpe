Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

GyaanPe ("we", "our", "the app") is a CBSE-aligned kids' learning app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you and your child have. GyaanPe is designed for use by children under parental supervision, and we take children's privacy seriously.

1. Information We Collect

GyaanPe is built to work with minimal personal information. We collect:

  • Learning progress data — XP points, coins, badges, streaks, quiz scores, and completed lessons, stored to track a learner's progress within the app.
  • Device/app identifiers — a randomly generated identifier used to store your progress and app preferences (not linked to your real-world identity).
  • Purchase information — if you subscribe to GyaanPe Premium, purchase status and subscription details are processed via Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details.
  • Usage data — general app usage statistics (e.g. features used, session length) that may be collected automatically to help us improve the app.

We do not knowingly collect a child's name, photo, precise location, contact list, or other directly identifying information.

2. How We Use Information

  • To save and display learning progress (XP, streaks, badges, quiz history)
  • To unlock Premium features for subscribed users
  • To show age-appropriate advertising (see Section 4)
  • To maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the app

3. Data Storage

Learning progress and app data are stored using Google Firebase (Realtime Database), a secure cloud service provided by Google. Data is associated with a device-level identifier, not with a child's real name or contact details.

4. Advertising

GyaanPe shows ads through Google AdMob to free-tier users. Ads shown to children are configured to comply with Google's child-directed treatment settings, meaning:

  • No behavioral or interest-based ad targeting is used for child users
  • No personalized advertising based on browsing history
  • Ad content is limited to formats appropriate for a general/family audience

Premium subscribers do not see ads.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Families)

GyaanPe is intended to be used by children with the involvement of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect more personal information from children than is necessary to provide the app's core functionality. We do not allow children to create public profiles, chat with other users, or share personal information within the app.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information beyond what is described here, please contact us using the details below and we will take appropriate action, including deletion of such data.

7. Parental Controls & Consent

Parents can review, manage, or request deletion of their child's app data at any time by contacting us. Purchases (subscriptions) require parental action through the Google Play payment flow.

8. Data Retention

Learning progress data is retained as long as the app is in use. You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Contact section).

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared only with the third-party service providers listed in Section 5, solely to operate the app's core features (progress storage, ads, billing).

10. Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect data stored via Firebase and Google Play Billing. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
