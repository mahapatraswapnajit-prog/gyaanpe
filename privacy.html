1. Who we are

GyaanPe is a learning app for children from Nursery to Class 7, published on Google Play as com.gyaanpe.kidslearning.

This app is operated by Swapnajit Mahapatra, based in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, India. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" refer to that operator, and "the app" refers to GyaanPe.

This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and what choices parents have. It applies to the GyaanPe mobile app and the GyaanPe web app.

2. A note for parents

GyaanPe is designed for children, so we have built it to work with as little personal information as possible. There is no sign-up, no login, and no need to give an email address or phone number to start learning.

If you are a parent or guardian and you have any question about your child's information, or you want it deleted, write to us at the address in section 12. We respond to every request.

4. Where information is stored

Most information — the display name, avatar, class and local settings — stays in the app's storage on the device itself. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Learning progress, quiz results and course progress are stored in Google Firebase Realtime Database so that progress is not lost. This information is stored against the random device ID described above, which means the records are not tied to a child's real identity.

5. The AI tutor and AI-generated practice

GyaanPe includes an AI tutor that answers a child's study questions, and an AI feature that generates practice worksheets. When a question or request is made, that text is sent over an encrypted connection to our processing service, which passes it to Google's Gemini AI model to generate a reply. The reply is then shown in the app.

We do not store these conversations. Our processing service keeps only a daily count of how many requests a device has made, so that fair usage limits can be applied. The content of what is typed is not saved by us and is not written to any database or log.

We use Google's paid Gemini API. Under those terms, the text sent is used only to generate the reply — it is not used to train or improve AI models, and it is not reviewed by human raters.

We instruct the AI to give age-appropriate, education-focused answers. AI systems can still occasionally produce an answer that is wrong or unsuitable, so we ask parents to supervise use of this feature. If you see an answer that seems inappropriate, please report it to us at the email address in section 12 and we will review it.

Please tell your child not to type personal details — such as their full name, address, school or phone number — into the tutor.

6. Advertising

GyaanPe does not show advertisements. There is no advertising network in the app, and no advertising identifier is collected, used or shared.

If we ever introduce advertising, we will update this policy first and give notice inside the app. Any advertising in a children's app would be contextual only — never personalised, never based on behavioural profiling, and never used to build a profile of a child.

7. Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time course purchases are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Payment details — card numbers, UPI IDs, bank details — go directly to Google. We never see, receive or store them. We only receive confirmation of whether a purchase is active, so the app knows what to unlock.

9. Children's privacy

GyaanPe is intended for children and is designed to comply with children's privacy laws, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the United States' Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Our approach is data minimisation: the app is built so that a child can learn without providing personal information at all. Specifically:

  • No account, email address or phone number is required.
  • The app does not track children across other apps or websites.
  • The app does not carry out behavioural monitoring, profiling or targeted advertising directed at children.
  • The display name is optional and may be a nickname.

Indian law treats anyone under 18 as a child and requires verifiable parental consent before a child's personal data is processed. Because a parent installs the app, controls the device and can review or delete everything the app holds, we ask that a parent or guardian install GyaanPe and supervise its use. By installing the app and allowing a child to use it, you confirm that you are the parent or guardian and that you consent to the limited processing described in this policy.

If you believe a child has provided us with information we should not hold, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Data retention and security

Information stored on the device remains until the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared. Progress records stored in Firebase are kept while the app is in use and are deleted on request.

All connections between the app and our services use HTTPS encryption. Access to our database is restricted, and content data is set to read-only for the app. No system can be guaranteed completely secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the limited information we hold.

11. Your rights and how to exercise them

As a parent or guardian, you may ask us to:

  • tell you what information the app holds about your child;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your child's information entirely;
  • stop any further processing.

You can delete most information yourself by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in your device settings. For records held in our database, email us from the address in section 12 with the subject line Data Deletion Request. We will confirm within 30 days.

Some purchase records are retained by Google Play and by us where tax or accounting law requires it, even after a deletion request.
