Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

We want parents to buy with confidence. This policy explains when you can get your money back.

1. Purchases on gyaanpe.com (unlock codes)

7-day refund for unused codes. If you change your mind, you can ask for a full refund within 7 days of payment, as long as the code has not been entered on any device.

  • Payment done but no code received: we will send your code, or give a full refund if you prefer.
  • Code does not work: we will fix it or give a full refund.
  • Charged twice for one purchase: the extra payment is refunded in full.
  • After a code is entered on a device: Premium time and courses start being used, so we cannot refund them, except where a technical problem on our side stops the code from working.

2. How to ask for a refund

Email gyaanpe.support@gmail.com with:

  • Your order ID or unlock code
  • The mobile number used for payment
  • The reason for the refund

We reply within 2 working days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method (UPI, card or net banking) and usually reach you within 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank.

3. Failed payments

If money was deducted but the payment failed, it is usually reversed automatically by your bank or payment app within 5 to 7 working days. If it is not, write to us with the details and we will help.

4. Purchases inside the Google Play app

Purchases made inside the GyaanPe app on Google Play are processed by Google, so refunds follow Google Play's refund policy. You can ask Google for a refund from Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Budget & history. You can also write to us and we will help where we can.

5. Cancelling a subscription

  • Google Play subscriptions can be cancelled anytime from Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. You keep access until the end of the period already paid for, and you will not be charged again.
  • Website Premium is a one-time purchase and does not renew automatically, so there is nothing to cancel.
